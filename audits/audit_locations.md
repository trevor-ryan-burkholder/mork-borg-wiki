# Locations Wiki — Source Audit

Diagnostic only. For each wiki entry under `/wiki/locations/`, compares against the canon PDFs in `/source/` and flags details that are missing, paraphrased away, or only partially quoted. Also lists named places in the source that have no wiki entry at all.

Severity:
- **HIGH** — substantive canon content is absent (named NPC, named geographic feature, named historical event, full Bare Bones flavor block).
- **MED** — flavor language is paraphrased so close that the texture is lost, or a directly relevant adventure-seed / class-origin / Misery line is missing.
- **LOW** — minor phrasings, secondary cross-references.

## Tveland.md — HIGH

**Source:** MÖRK BORG BARE BONES EDITION p.12 (gazetteer line "In Tveland also lies …"), p.61 (Lady Porcelain entry), p.68 (adventure seeds).

Missing:

- The Bare Bones gazetteer line **"In Tveland also lies"** is the actual link-text that introduces Sarkash inside the Galgenbeck/Tveland section. The wiki paraphrases this connection but never quotes the canon's framing that Tveland *contains* Galgenbeck, then Sarkash with Graven-Tosk and the Shadow King's Palace rising over it as a single nested gazetteer block.
- The Lady Porcelain canon phrase **"In Tveland relic thieves, defamers and corrupt clerks suffer a punishment of exquisite and deeply impractical cruelty"** is paraphrased — "exquisite and deeply impractical cruelty" is a load-bearing phrase that should be quoted.
- Bare Bones p.61: *"Despite warding rituals, many return as vengeful undead, often gathering in large throngs to hunt their tormentors. Their mad gaze demands a Presence dr12 test at the start of combat to avoid being frozen with fear for d4 rounds."* — the "warding rituals" detail and the "large throngs hunting their tormentors" are unstated.
- The Eat-Prey-Kill "Tveland" region table (p.4) names six Tveland creatures: **Antideer, Flayed Vultures, Ratbit, Feral Horses, Steppe Wolfe, Tusked Bison.** None appear in the wiki.
- Adventure seed (Bare Bones p.68) places risk **"In the untamed wilds of Tveland"** — wiki captures this loosely but doesn't quote.
- "Klefunheim" (a Galgenbeck noble holding, source: MBC Goblin Grinder p.4) is named as a place: *"Jota Klefunheim of Klefunheim"* — the wiki Gaps note mentions Klefunheim but doesn't tie it to Tveland.

## Galgenbeck.md — HIGH

**Source:** MÖRK BORG BARE BONES EDITION p.10, p.12 (gazetteer); MBC Goblin Grinder pp.2–14; MBC Unheroic Feats p.55 ("the alleys of Galgenbeck"); MBC Merchant p.4 (Galgenbeck Deathmask); MBC Graves Left Wanting p.16 ("Arga, a Galgenbeck priest"); MBC The Death Ziggurat p.2 (Galgenbeck scribes forgot Akünh).

Missing direct-quote canon flavor:

- Bare Bones p.12: *"GAlgenbeck in the lAnd of tvelAnd is the greatest city that ever was. No King or Queen rules in Galgenbeck but an arch-priestess: Josilfa Migol."* — the **"No King or Queen rules … but an arch-priestess"** phrasing is canon.
- Bare Bones p.12: *"Josilfa, old but still young, commoners gossip that she colludes with the god Nechrubel, who gave her eternal life. Nechrubel: the shadow that covers all. Nechrubel is melancholy, crop failure, conflict and war. It is said he whispered the apocalyptic prophecies in Verhu's ear."* — wiki summarizes the collusion but loses "Nechrubel: the shadow that covers all" and the Verhu-whispering.
- Bare Bones p.12: *"The road to salvation lies through mortification of the flesh; the apocalypse is to be met with eyes wide open. Only then can the soul be allowed passage to the Shimmering Fields."* — **Shimmering Fields** as the Two-Headed Basilisks' afterlife destination is not mentioned in Galgenbeck.md at all.
- Bare Bones p.10 (Anuk Schleger / Creton order / Two-Headed Basilisks): canon establishes that the Two-Headed Basilisks are "an orthodox branch of the Creton order" who **broke ground for a new Cathedral and uncovered Schleger's tomb and with it the Scriptures** — this founding event belongs in Galgenbeck's origin, currently only in Cathedral.md.

Missing named NPCs / districts / landmarks:

- **Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks** is the only named building. The wiki doesn't surface:
  - **Klefunheim** (district/noble holding of "Jota Klefunheim of Klefunheim … tall, perpetually annoyed, some kind of baroness or princess" — Goblin Grinder p.4).
  - **Urgrip Wikt**, a poor Galgenbeck peasant who owns a Calumny Pearl heirloom (Goblin Grinder p.4).
  - **Arga, a Galgenbeck priest** (Graves Left Wanting p.16) — HP 4, Morale 10, Scale -d4, Staff d4, carries a random sacred scroll. Named NPC tied to the city.
  - **The Bastard**, goblin-overlord mutant born in the Derelict Mill, who as "torturer for a group of unsavory royals" implies Galgenbeck has unsavory royals (Goblin Grinder p.5).
- Adventure seed 73–74 *"Slave revolt in Galgenbeck"* is named, but the wiki only says "Slave revolts (adventure seed 73–74) and unholy infiltrations of the court (45–46) are local hazards" — fine, but seed **6 "At the city wall of Galgenbeck"** as a "risk your life" location is missing.
- Misery **1:1** *"The City shall be made hollow. Of those who rest in hollowness, they shall not be seen."* is canonically the prophecy aimed at Galgenbeck (the gazetteer's Hollow City). Wiki gestures at "1:1 prophecy 'the city shall be made hollow'" in Gaps but does not present it as the city's primary Misery in the body.
- Eat-Prey-Kill identifies Galgenbeck-adjacent fauna implicitly (the "streets of Galgenbeck littered with corpses … goblins crawl inside basements, nest within cupboards" — Goblin Grinder p.2). The "goblin infestation" canon is present in wiki but the **"mottled, ropy flesh rots but never decomposes; even the oldest skins in the darkest alleys hold their shape to warn passersby of their swiftly approaching end"** detail of dead goblins lying around as warnings is not quoted.

# Named Places in Source PDFs Without Wiki Entries

These are canon-named locations that currently have no entry under `/wiki/locations/`. Severity reflects how much canon lore exists per place.

## HIGH (substantial named lore, no entry)

### Pale Mountain (in the Infinitely-Miserable Forest of Dark Leaves)
**Source:** Bare Bones p.60 (Nodh / zombie). *"the only cure or vaccine is said to be found at the peak of a pale mountain within an infinitely-miserable forest of dark leaves. king fAthmu ix of wästlAnd in particular seeks this cure and knows the name and location of the forest which the mountain overlooks."* — Two named geographic features (Pale Mountain, Infinitely-Miserable Forest of Dark Leaves) tied to a named NPC quest (Fathmu IX's zombie-cure hunt).

### The Strangly Tree, Plague Pit, Erhard's Tomb, Catacomb Corridor, Colander Room, Maus' Vomatorium, Origin Fountain, Roach Herder's Lair, Undertaker's Hut
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting (entire booklet). Nine distinct named locations within Graven-Tosk, each with canon descriptions, named NPCs (Erhard, Fela Maus, the Roach Herder, the Undertaker / Übertaker, the Deadn't), magical items (Roseate Baritona, Skeleton Unkey), and at least one named geographic event ("the place's palace's ruination" implying a past calamity at the Shadow King's). These are arguably sub-locations of Graven-Tosk but each is fully described in canon and warrants its own entry.

### Cretun Order (location implied — monastery/seat)
**Source:** Bare Bones p.10 ("Anuk schleger, monk of the Creton order"); MBC Death Ziggurat p.2 ("the Cretun monks as they hired you"). Spelled both Creton and Cretun in canon — possible **CANON CHECK**. The order has hireling-monks who give scenario hooks; their physical seat/monastery is undescribed but canonically exists as the orthodox source from which the Two-Headed Basilisks split.

# Cross-Cutting Issues

1. **Eat-Prey-Kill regional bestiary is untapped across the wiki.** Each region table (Tveland/Sarkash/Graven-Tosk/Grift/Kergüs/Wästland/Lake Onda/Valley/Bergen Chrypt) contributes six named native creatures with canon flavor lines. None of the regional location entries list their native fauna or quote any of it. This is the single largest body of regional canon currently absent.

2. **Bare Bones gazetteer flavor text is paraphrased, not quoted.** The locations.md entries are competent summaries but the load-bearing canon phrases ("the bridges scream and roar like great ships grinding upon rocks," "a waterfall of white stone," "rage rising over pain," "blank-eyed cherubs, stagnant fountains, plague pits and ordinary graves," "huge parchments dot the streets," "Galgenbeck in the land of Tveland is the greatest city that ever was") are present in spirit but not as direct quotation. For a wiki built on "the text itself barely survived" tone, more direct quotation would help.

3. **Adventure-seed locations are inconsistently surfaced.** Seeds 9–10 (Lake Onda), 11–12 (Schleswig sinkhole), 31–32 (Grift caves), 65–66 (Sarkash fire), 73–74 (Galgenbeck slave revolt), 83–84 (strange ship), 85–86 (weirdness leaves Bergen Chrypt), 89–90 (icon's eyes), 91–92 (beast beneath the bridge), 93–94 (black star's crater), 99–00 (Sarkash path) — only some appear in their respective location entries.

4. **Misery prophecies tied to specific regions aren't always surfaced in those entries.** Mapping:
   - 1:1 → Galgenbeck (Hollow City) — *not in body*
   - 2:3 → Bergen Chrypt — *present*
   - 2:6 → SHE reveals herself / Bergen Chrypt subtext — *not surfaced*
   - 3:1 → Graven-Tosk — *present*
   - 3:2 → Sarkash — *present*
   - 4:3 → Western Kingdom — *present*
   - 4:5, 4:6 → Endless Sea / Leviathan — *4:5 present, 4:6 partial*
   - 6:3 → Anthelia / Kergüs — *not in Kergüs.md*

5. **The Creton Order's spelling is inconsistent in canon** (Creton in Bare Bones p.10 and Death Ziggurat references, Cretun in MBC Death Ziggurat p.2). **CANON CHECK** — flag in wiki.

6. **River names are missing throughout.** Canon implies rivers (Lake Onda river-trade, Sarkash river flowing into the Death Ziggurat depression, the Múr-as-strait, Endless Sea "where" rivers feed it). None are named in source but the absence is worth flagging as a canonical gap.

7. **Distances are given between locations** in MBC Overland Travel p.2 (Alliáns ↔ Graven-Tosk d8+6 days; Bergen Chrypt ↔ Schleswig d10+8 days; etc.). The wiki nowhere uses or links to this canonical travel-distance map.
