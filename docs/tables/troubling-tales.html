Troubling Tales (d20)

table MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.41–42

A fourth d20 character-creation table, companion to Terrible Traits, Broken Bodies, and Bad Habits. The whole group can share a single backstory; smaller groups within the party can share a tale; or the GM can use one entry to give a mundane NPC sudden history.

These are tales that travel with the character. None of them resolve. All of them follow.

The Tales (d20)

d20 Tale

18 Tracked and observed by a golem, after an agreement which you know has been wiped from your mind.

20 Your flesh heals twice as fast, but your companions' twice as slow. You see a many-eyed "guardian angel."
Connections

Terrible Traits (companion tables: Broken Bodies, Bad Habits). Tale 11's puzzle cube echoes Cube-Violet; tale 17's underground temple gestures toward Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch or the toad-cities beneath Grift; tale 18's golem-pact may connect to Tergol's experiments; tale 20's many-eyed guardian is a [CANON CHECK] hook open to whichever divine horror the GM prefers.

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Each entry is a one-line proper-noun engine — the bounty, the debt-traders, the puzzle cube, the corvid war, the unnamed golem, the many-eyed angel. Every one of them can carry a session.
