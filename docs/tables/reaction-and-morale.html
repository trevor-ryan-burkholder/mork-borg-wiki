Reaction and Morale

mechanic / table MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.32

Reaction (2d6)

When meeting creatures whose reaction is uncertain.

| 2d6 | Reaction |

|---|---|

| 2–3 | Kill! |

| 4–6 | Angered |

| 7–8 | Indifferent |

| 9–10 | Almost friendly |

| 11–12 | Helpful |

Morale (2d6)

Most enemies will not fight to the last drop of blood.

Roll Morale if:

  • The leader is killed
  • Half the group is eliminated
  • A single enemy has only 1/3 of its HP left

If you roll greater than the creature's Morale value with 2d6, it is demoralized. Roll d6: (1–3) flees or (4–6) surrenders.
