The Basilisks Demand (d20)

table MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.36

When the Two-Headed Basilisks demand tribute — or when HE demands a gift (adventure seed 69–70) — roll to learn what they require this time.

d20 Tribute
1 A sword that has killed exactly one dozen times
2 A widower's wedding ring
3 Silver from a sinner's grave
4 Eyes that have seen the Shimmering Fields
5 The year's first-born goat
6 Blutday bread
7 The cuticle of an executed innocent

9 A dagger onto which the condemned carved their victims' name
10 Rare anti-obsidian from the Urilian crypts
11 The forbidden brew of the hermit of Terion

13 A body mutilated by those who loved it in life
14 Joy's lampoon written in blood
15 The gall of a Chrypt-vulture
16 Moss upon which a dying man has slept
17 A child born with the third eye
18 A body drowned in Lake Onda
19 The rear molar of the Gluttonous
20 Gems from overflowing pockets
Connections

Two-Headed Basilisks, Verhu, Gorgh, HE, Shimmering Fields, Terion, Bergen Chrypt, Kergüs, Lake Onda

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Several named gaps: Blutday is an unspecified holiday. Urilian crypts and anti-obsidian are named only here. The Gluttonous is one named being whose teeth matter. The orgh-maggot of Kergüs (12) and the Chrypt-vulture (15) are named creatures with no other appearance. Each is an adventure seed.
