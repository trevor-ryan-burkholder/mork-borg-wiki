Connections

Two-Headed Basilisks, Verhu, Gorgh, HE, Shimmering Fields, Terion, Bergen Chrypt, Kergüs, Lake Onda

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Several named gaps: Blutday is an unspecified holiday. Urilian crypts and anti-obsidian are named only here. The Gluttonous is one named being whose teeth matter. The orgh-maggot of Kergüs (12) and the Chrypt-vulture (15) are named creatures with no other appearance. Each is an adventure seed.
