Stone Dagger

item / weapon MBC The Merchant, p.4 (Tveland wares)

D4 damage. To plunge it into a living creature, test Strength DR12 — on a success the creature instantly turns to stone. Remove the dagger and the petrified thing springs back to life, as if nothing ever happened, except it remembers everything.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Tveland for a portion of the buyer's soul.

Connections

Mikhael the Merchant, Merchant Inventory, Tveland

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the petrified victim is conscious through the stone, or whether the stone is the soul, is left to the GM — and to whoever buys the dagger second-hand.
