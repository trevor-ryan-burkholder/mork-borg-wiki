Vibrant Red Ribbon

item / wearable MBC The Merchant, p.6 (Kergüs wares)

A crimson silk ribbon. When worn, all colour drains from your vision — the world goes black and white, but you see the pulsating heartbeats of living creatures as splashes of red. Allows you to see the living in complete darkness.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Kergüs for a portion of the buyer's soul. A useful gift for hunting in pits and crypts, less useful for finding the door.

Connections

Mikhael the Merchant, Merchant Inventory, Kergüs

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the ribbon shows undead — who have no pulse — is the silence the wearer learns to fear.
