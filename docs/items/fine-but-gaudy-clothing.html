Fine but Gaudy Clothing

item / wearable MBC The Merchant, p.5 (Wästland wares)

Allows you to pass as nobility, or as a moderately ranked official, on a successful DR10 Presence test. Mercilessly mocked by common folk and outcasts. Bandits will always attack you on sight.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Wästland for a portion of the buyer's soul. Either you are court-fit or you are bandit-bait; there is no third use.
