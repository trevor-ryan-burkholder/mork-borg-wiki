Vial of Goblin Ichor

item / consumable / detection MBC The Merchant, p.4 (Tveland wares)

Grows hot when Goblins are about. Adding a drop of cursed blood causes the mixture to bead up, pointing in the direction of the goblin that cursed you.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Tveland for a portion of the buyer's soul. Useful only to those already marked — most of Galgenbeck qualifies.

Connections

Mikhael the Merchant, Merchant Inventory, Tveland, Goblin, Goblin Cure

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the ichor cools again once the cursing goblin dies, or whether it merely beads toward where the corpse lies, is unsaid.
