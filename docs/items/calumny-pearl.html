Calumny Pearl

item The Goblin Grinder (Urgrip Wikt's heirloom)

Place it underneath your tongue and you entirely believe the first lie told to you.

Urgrip Wikt (worried peasant, infected with the Goblin Curse, exceedingly poor) offers it as payment if PCs resolve Galgenbeck's goblin problem. Unknown to him, this is what the heirloom is.

Connections

The Goblin Grinder, Galgenbeck

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether there are other Calumny Pearls in the world, and who originally crafted them, is open.
