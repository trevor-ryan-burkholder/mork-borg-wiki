Sict-Shroom

item / fungus MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.45

A poisonous mushroom found only within Cube-Violet. Crumbled and fed to a close friend; victim tests Toughness DR16 or takes −d6 hp and suffers hallucinations.

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether Sict-Shroom can be harvested from the cube and exported, or whether it dissolves outside, is unwritten. Its name suggests "sick" + "shroom" but no etymology is canonical.
