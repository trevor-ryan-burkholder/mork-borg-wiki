Tongue of a False Prophet

item / consumable MBC The Merchant, p.4 (Tveland wares)

Consuming the tongue allows you to cast Powers at DR6 for one day. But any failure results in an arcane catastrophe.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Tveland for a portion of the buyer's soul. Whose tongue, exactly, and what passes through the chewer alongside the easy magic, is unsaid. A prophet who was wrong before death is wrong after it, and now wrong inside you.

Connections

Mikhael the Merchant, Merchant Inventory, Tveland, Powers

Gaps / Expansion Notes

No catalogue of False Prophets — Mikhael's supply is unlimited as far as the booklet shows. A campaign-shaped GM could give the buyer the prophet's dreams.
