Horn of the Schleswig Lords

item / artifact MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.53

a dented old trumpet, last blown in better days

Once per day, release a blare from this horn and test Presence dr12. On success, one creature may make their next non-combat test an automatic success.

The Lords of Schleswig are long absent, replaced or scattered or simply forgotten in the slow drowning of the city. Their horn survives in a Wretched Royalty's baggage, dented from many strikes, still loud enough to crack a chapel window.

Connections

Wretched Royalty, Schleswig

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Schleswig Lords are named only here — a vanished aristocratic faction. Whether they were displaced by Fathmu IX's court, by the rising waters, or by the Inquisition is open. The horn's blare being audible across the city is a useful adventure-prompt — every blow attracts something.
