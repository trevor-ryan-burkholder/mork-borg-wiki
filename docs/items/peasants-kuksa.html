Peasant's Kuksa

item / utensil MBC The Merchant, p.5 (Wästland wares)

A wooden cup carved from fine birch burl. Any liquid scooped by the cup turns into adequate drinking water.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Wästland for a portion of the buyer's soul. "Adequate" is doing most of the work in this sentence — the kuksa does not make wine, only the absence of dying.

Connections

Mikhael the Merchant, Merchant Inventory, Wästland

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the kuksa transmutes a scooped potion (Elixir Vitalis? Emerald Venom?) into water is the question that will eventually break it.
