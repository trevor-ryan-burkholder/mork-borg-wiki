Pilgrim's Compass

item / detection MBC The Merchant, p.5 (Wästland wares)

A small, bloodstained compass. Its needle always points to the most dangerous thing to your life in the immediate vicinity.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Wästland for a portion of the buyer's soul. It does not name the danger — only nods at it, patient as a martyr. It will, on bad days, point at a party member.

Connections

Mikhael the Merchant, Merchant Inventory, Wästland

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The radius of "immediate vicinity" is unprinted. Whether the needle locks on or hesitates between rivals is GM-fiat.
