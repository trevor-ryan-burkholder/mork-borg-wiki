Lunar Zweihänder

item / weapon Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch

A shining greatsword longer than the Swamp Witch herself, slung on her back. Made from moonlight. Deals d12 + radiance.

  • Radiance: Ignores all non-magical armor. Each hit fills the room with radiant light. Everyone within 50' tests Presence DR12 or takes d4 damage.
Connections

Swamp Witch, Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the blade can be taken from the Swamp Witch's corpse — and whether it then functions for any wielder — is unspecified. The mechanics of "made from moonlight" beyond the special are GM judgment.
