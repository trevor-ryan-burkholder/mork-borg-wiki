Two-headed Silver Ring

item / wearable MBC The Merchant, p.4 (Tveland wares)

A silver ring in the shape of twin basilisk heads. Wearing it increases your crit and fumble range by one. Multiple rings stack.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Tveland for a portion of the buyer's soul. A trinket of the Two-Headed Basilisks; the wearer's luck swings wider in both directions, the world becoming both more lethal and more merciful at once.

Connections

Mikhael the Merchant, Merchant Inventory, Tveland, Two-Headed Basilisks

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether a ring per finger is the limit, or whether a wrist of them turns combat into pure dice noise, is the obvious table-question.
