Bright Yellow Flower

item / single-use MBC The Merchant, p.6 (Kergüs wares)

A sunny, sweet-smelling flower that does not seem to wither. Given as a token of affection to another person, the recipient becomes extremely agreeable to the next thing you say.

Sold by Mikhael the Merchant in Kergüs for a portion of the buyer's soul. A single-use influence-by-gift; afterwards they remember the smell and not much else.

Connections

Mikhael the Merchant, Merchant Inventory, Kergüs

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the flower itself is consumed by being given, or merely the spell on it, is unstated — Mikhael will not say. Whether two gifts in one day stack, or cancel, is open.
