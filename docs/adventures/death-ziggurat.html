The Death Ziggurat — A Cosmic Necrocrawl

adventure / scenario MBC_The-Death-Ziggurat.pdf (pp.2–10; player map: MBC_The-Death-Ziggurat_Player-map.pdf)

"Find the ziggurat and stop the demon from laying waste to our world before the true time!"

The Creton Order hires the PCs after cryptic scrolls report a demon escaping her ziggurat prison deep within Sarkash. The ziggurat is "hidden in another realm" and "the priests carry keys." Generous pay keeps the PCs from asking too many questions.

Deep in the woods of Sarkash, an unnaturally cold depression yawns — a sinkhole as if formed by the strike of an impossibly large fist. Around its edges, the death-obsessed cult camps. Inside it, rot-priests set up symbol-covered conical tents, Sarku hangs his dried-up victims upside-down from the trees, ivy-covered ruins rise between fir, the Frozen Lake of black water-lilies holds moaning undead frozen in mud, and a foul cave opens through a rotting mouth in the woods. Murky clouds whirl down into the depression like a tornado — Akünh's channeling.

Akünh is a spawn of SHE — conceived in the unholy bonding between the basilisk and a demonic being from a parallel world filled with undeath and torment. Centuries ago, unsung knights of the Cretun order subdued her and bound her into the Death Realm in a ziggurat deep within Sarkash. Now the bindings have lost their potency. Like one more Misery, Akünh has broken free.

Surroundings

  • The Depression. Fog and clouds. Steep cliffs 150–300 feet high. The river flows in from the southwest as a frozen waterfall, forming a lake at the bottom under a thin sheet of ice. Clouds whirl tornado-like in the northern end down into a great fog cloud where the ziggurat hides.
  • Death-Obsessed Cultist Camp. 25 cultists half an hour's walk from the depression. Strange headdresses signaling rank, tattooed faces, thick furs dangling with skeletal parts.
