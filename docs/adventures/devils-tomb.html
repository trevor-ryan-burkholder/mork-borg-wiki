Devil's Tomb

adventure / one-page dungeon Devil's Tomb (a dungeon by Johan Nohr, made using the MÖRK BORG digital dungeon generator)

A one-page hellhole deep in Graven-Tosk. The Eyes of the Icon of St Largoth have been stolen and brought here; the PCs are sent to retrieve them. The Eyes will see all hidden things — including the passage to the Plant of Life.

The tomb is currently occupied by Drowned, amphibian exiles from a poisoned lake who worship the Plant. Their traitor Belpheduk is imprisoned with her own severed arm nailed to a wall. The Madman crawls at the entrance, naked and spore-mad. Goblins squabble over a two-handed sword (d10+1) in a side chamber. The Pit to Hell drops straight into the fiery torture-realms; a narrow bridge crosses to a chained bronze door inscribed in blood: GO TO NECH. The Angelic Choir corridor walls bloom with angel-faced flowers singing wordless songs. Spores in the air induce visions of death, vertigo, monochromatic outlining, or invisibility-of-the-living.

The Plant of Life waits at the back — a giant pulsing cactus in every color whose tendrils reach for the unwary.

Connections

Graven-Tosk, Icon of St Largoth, Drowned, Plant of Life, Belpheduk, Madman, Pit to Hell, Angelic Choir, Goblin

Gaps / Expansion Notes

What lies beyond the GO TO NECH chained door is the adventure's greatest unwritten promise. The cult that originally made the hellhole is gone; its relics survive.
