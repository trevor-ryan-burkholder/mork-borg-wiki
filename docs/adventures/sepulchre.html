Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch

adventure / scenario Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch

A murdered messenger wearing mad King Fathmu IX's crest was found in a ditch. On him: forbidden words for the Dead Root Altar inside the Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch — words that fulfill any wish. Even stopping the approaching Doom is not outside the powers of that altar. The PCs just need to get past the Strange Serpent Drug Cult of Ueth lairing in the ancient tomb and reach the altar.

You also heard, perhaps, that snakes guard the entrance and you must not harm them (true); that the cult kidnaps people, desecrating the altar with viscera (true); that chanting the words backwards raises the Swamp Witch from the dead (true); that occult treasures are buried, guarded by slime-covered amphibians (true); that merchants trade illicit goods with the cult (true); that entrants risk catching a psychic parasite, so it's safer to just kill everyone and burn the swamp (uncertain, but tempting).

The Sepulchre's eleven chambers conceal hidden paths visible only to those carrying Emerald Venom in their blood — the cult's drug-key, delivered by Emerald Serpent bite at the entrance. The Croaking Trident hides among the Hidden Ferns. The Swamp Witch floats above an empty sarcophagus surrounded by 13 obelisks of spiraling snakes. The Downward Spiral descends into darkness guarded by Srolki & Yaoxl, two antediluvian three-eyed toads — and beyond them, great caverns and entire cities of their kind.

The altar grants wishes via unwilling sacrifice. Willing sacrifices are punished. Asking for the world not to end only delays one Misery. Roll d6 for side-effects of every wish — from minor cracks in the altar to fully draining maximum HP from everyone in the room.

Connections

Fathmu IX, Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch, Strange Serpent Drug Cult, Ueth, Swamp Witch, Dead Root Altar, Emerald Serpent, Emerald Venom, Croaking Trident, Lunar Zweihänder, Srolki & Yaoxl, Klopstock, Schleswig

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Why Fathmu wanted the altar's wish is the adventure's central unanswered question. The cities of the three-eyed toads below are entirely unmapped — natural expansion module.
