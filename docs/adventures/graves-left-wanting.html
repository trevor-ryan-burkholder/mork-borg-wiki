Graves Left Wanting

adventure / scenario MBC Graves Left Wanting (by Karl Druid; design and art Johan Nohr; editing Fiona Maeve Geist and Jarrett Crader)

"An immense cemetery, older and greater than memory, Graven-Tosk sits in Sarkash like a festering scab. To the east, the Shadow King's manse looms always, impossibly equidistant. Although abandoned since the palace's ruination, bodies somehow keep finding their way inside. All the while, living and unliving attempt to claw their way out."

A starting adventure, a one-shot, or a post-TPK reset. The graveyard is so massive and unsettling that even PCs familiar with other parts are oblivious to its locales. The PCs awaken in The Plague Pit — in coffins under suffocating earth, or wherever the GM finds appropriate. There is no defined ending and no clear escape path. Once the Undertaker's Hut is explored, players should plan a getaway or be hopelessly lost. "Add a more obvious exit or method of escape if you feel that would end their misery. It won't."

Map (Not To Scale)

Five named places sit on the surface map — the Strangly Tree (1), the Plague Pit (2), the Origin Fountain (3), Maus' Vomatorium (4), and the Undertaker's Hut (5). The Plague Pit connects underground through the Catacomb Corridor to Erhard's Tomb, the Colander Room, and (via the Catacomb Corridor's north tunnel) the Roach Herder's Lair, which opens onto the Shadow King's Sewer. The sewer's hatch rises to the inside of the Strangly Tree.

Places may not be where they appear. The fog rearranges them.

Walking the Trails (d4)

When traveling between surface locations, roll d4 for where the path leads — and roll a random encounter (d10):

  1. The Strangly Tree
  2. The Plague Pit
  3. The Origin Fountain
  4. Maus' Vomatorium

Once all four primary surface locations have been visited, the next path leads to the Undertaker's Hut. The Roach Herder knows which way to go.

  2. a ghastly Widow-wraith.
  3. d4 Unbred Mutts digging.
