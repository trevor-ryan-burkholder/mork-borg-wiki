Kulvan

NPC / goblin MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.45

A riddling goblin, strong, found within Cube-Violet. Holds three colorless pearls. Must be slain as one of four trials to escape the cube.

Connections

Cube-Violet, Goblin

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Kulvan is named, statted as "strong," and given a riddler's role — but his actual riddles, the pearls' nature, and why he is in the cube are unwritten.
