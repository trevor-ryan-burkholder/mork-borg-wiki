Stein

NPC / merchant, one of The Deadn't MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.16

a Grift merchant with a rusted shortsword and the most silver in the hut

A trader out of Grift — one of the bridge-city's many travelling merchants — now stranded in Graven-Tosk with Arga the Galgenbeck priest and Benzen the Schleswig guard. He helped kill The Undertaker in the Undertaker's Hut when she refused to show them the way out. He is still there, and so is the cemetery.

His morale is steady but his hands are not. The shortsword is rusted; the purse is the most silver anyone in the hut owns, and there is nowhere to spend it.

Stat block: HP 2, Morale 8, No armor, Shortsword d4 Carries a purse containing 50 silver.

Connections

The Deadn't, Graves Left Wanting, Graven-Tosk, The Undertaker, The Übertaker, The Undertaker's Hut, Grift, Arga, Benzen

Gaps / Expansion Notes

What he was carrying when he walked into Graven-Tosk — and what is left of his wagon, his beasts, his trade-goods — is unwritten. Whether he came from Grift before Sigfúm the Kind began calmly walking the city toward Terion, or after, is open. His name is not from the Bare Bones d6 / d8 name tables.
