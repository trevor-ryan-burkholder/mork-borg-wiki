Belpheduk, the Traitor

NPC / Drowned Devil's Tomb

the imprisoned Drowned who wants the Plant burned

A traitor among the Drowned in Devil's Tomb. She killed a fellow Drowned and this cell — a muddy, cramped cave with her severed arm nailed to the wall — is her punishment.

HP 5, Morale 3, Scales −d2, Claws d4

Hungry and spiteful. Seeks revenge. Wants to see the Plant of Life destroyed. Knows where it is hidden.

Connections

Devil's Tomb, Drowned, Plant of Life, Graven-Tosk

Gaps / Expansion Notes

What Belpheduk did to deserve her cell — beyond "killed a fellow Drowned" — is unwritten. Whether she would still be useful as an ally once the Plant is destroyed (or whether she would then turn on her liberators) is open.
