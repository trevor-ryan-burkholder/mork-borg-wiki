Wretched Old Mikhael, the Merchant

NPC / merchant The Merchant

Said to have come from a wealthy mercantile bloodline, Mikhael's family were crucified before his eyes for a heretical crime long forgotten. A foul priestess extended her hand and offered him mercy: "Repent for the sins of your family and be forgiven by the Dark Spider, Harbinger of the end." And so Mikhael was forgiven. And cursed with damned eternal life. The mournful dead spit him out from blackened earth if ever he should die, for he has no place among them.

He resumed the trade of his family, but time spent in the dead earth taught him that souls are just as valuable as silver. Eternally he wanders the dying world, bearing a grudge that too will never die.

He only trades with heretics and outcasts. He has a distrust for villagers who would crucify him for being an unfortunate undead.

Stat block: HP 6, Morale 9, No armor, Staff d4.

Eternal Unlife: Should Mikhael die, all of his unsold possessions burst into black flames. The earth spits him out in d4 days. Willing to forgive the PCs the first time.

He sells regional goods depending on where he's found (d6 per item — see Merchant Inventory). The cost of every item is a portion of your soul: d6 → −1 Strength / Presence / Agility / Toughness / Max HP / roll twice. He buys rare items the same way but adds.

He is frequently found alone or with a wagon drawn by a white-eyed mule.

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The Dark Spider, Harbinger of the end is named once and explained never — possibly an aspect of Nechrubel, possibly a deity unique to the priestess who saved him, definitely an expansion seed. The "foul priestess" who forgave him is unnamed. The heretical crime that doomed his family is forgotten by design.
