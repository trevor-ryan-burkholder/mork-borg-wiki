Hyphos

NPC (historic / namesake) MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.57

A name remembered only by a snuff. Hyphos' enervating snuff — an Occult Herbmaster's berserker-brew. Two attacks per round, defence dr14, lasts one fight, must be snorted, causes sneezing.

The first to take the snuff fought through three rooms before tearing their own jaw off, sneezing. So the story goes. The recipe survives. The jaw does not.

Connections

Occult Herbmaster, Ezumiel, Fernor (other named brewers), Berserker (parallel frenzy)

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Hyphos, Ezumiel, and Fernor — three names preserved only through decoctions. The hyphae root in the name suggests fungal origin, consistent with the Herbmaster's mushroom-bed lineage. A reclusive snuff-maker still working out of Sarkash would fit.
