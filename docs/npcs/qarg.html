Qarg, Gravedigger

NPC / mercenary The Goblin Grinder

muscle-for-hire with a shovel and few questions

Dirty, grim stare, shovel strapped to her back, broken nose. Nagel Krat pays her 50 silver per body delivered. She's not picky about what happens to them afterward and works as muscle for Medickal Shoppe for 10 silver per hour. She's a simple woman and likes the opportunities afforded to her when she has a salary beyond gravedigging's pennies.

Easily bribed: convincing her to leave her post or miss a delivery is a DR8 Presence test if she's offered more silver.

Stat block: HP 9, Morale 7, Filthy furs −d2, Cudgel d6 or Shovel d4.

Her trail of dragged blood marks the path from Nagel's barricade through several of the Derelict Mill's floors to the Goblin Grinder itself.

Connections

Nagel Krat, Medickal Shoppe, Derelict Mill, Goblin Grinder, Galgenbeck

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Her usual graveyard, her clients besides Nagel, and her loyalty ceiling (will she sell out the PCs for a higher bid? Will she sell out Nagel?) are open. A natural recurring NPC.
