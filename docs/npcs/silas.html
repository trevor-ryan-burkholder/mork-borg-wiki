Silas the Fattened King

NPC / villain MBC Bloat

A vagabond who sought shelter from a storm in a large animal burrow and found a set of stairs leading down. In the chapel of an obscure bacchanalian cult he found the Gourmand's Cutlery and a statue that compelled him to use it. Silas was overcome. All he wants to do is eat. He compelled two Fleshy Automatons to feed him — now they create more of their kind, kidnapping people to bring back to the Fattened King.

Redolent in travesty, the bloated form of Silas fills the chamber. Too large to fit through the door — he waits for the Fleshy Automatons to feed him. The Gourmand's Cutlery looks tiny in his swollen, greasy hands.

Stat block: HP 20, Morale 9, No armour, Cutlery d4 + Engulf

  • Engulf. On hit, DR10 Strength or be partially swallowed for d6 chewing damage.
  • Flatulence. When Silas takes damage he loses control of his body. All in the room test Toughness DR14 or gag for d3 rounds, increasing all tests by +2 DR (cumulative).
