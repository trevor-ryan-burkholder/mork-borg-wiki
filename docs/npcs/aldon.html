Aldon

NPC Rotblack Sludge

The Shadow King's one true heir. His son.

He sits on a bench in the Son's Room of the Accursed Den, pudgy and arrogant, gnawing human bones. The masked Seer who hired the players warned: "I fear Aldon may be unwilling to leave this place."

He has been corrupted by the den. A bullwhip with cryptic runes (d4 base + d4 necro-plasmic shock damage) hangs on his wall.

The Eye-Socket Key

The Son's Room is sealed by hereditary blood. In an adjoining Statue Room of the Accursed Den stands a "black statue of the one-eyed King Lenard II" — "obvious cavity where the eye would be. Investigation reveals bloodstains in the socket." Place an eye in the king's socket and the door to the Son's Room "opens with a loud crack."
