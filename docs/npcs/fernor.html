Fernor

NPC (historic / namesake) MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.57

A name remembered only by a philtre. Fernor's philtre — a translucent oil dabbed straight into the eye, brewed by an Occult Herbmaster. Heals infection and grants +2 on Presence tests for d4 hours. The eye weeps for a day afterward.

Whether Fernor was the first to take the philtre into their own eye, or the first to put it into another's, is unwritten. The recipe survives. The face does not.

Connections

Occult Herbmaster, Ezumiel, Hyphos (other named brewers)

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Fernor, Ezumiel, and Hyphos — three names of the poisoner-trade. A Fernor lineage of eye-healers, of seers, of one-eyed witches, could be drawn out as a small mountain-cottage faction.
