Sigfúm the Kind

NPC / monarch MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.14

King of Grift. Once mocked in the streets of his own city. He believes the prophecies of Verhu absolutely and so, kindly and calmly, prepares his people for death.

Huge parchments dot the streets — calendars of despair marking each correct preparation and its time. Each day a leaf is turned. When the last page comes, Sigfúm will march his people to the cliff Terion to fulfill what was written.

The Inquisition of the Two-Headed Basilisks is not too keen on his heretical suicide scheme — they hold that suicide is sinful cowardice and that the apocalypse must be met with eyes open.

Adventure seed 57–58: "Stop a Grift suicide-cult."

Connections

Grift, Terion, Verhu, Two-Headed Basilisks, Inquisition, Shimmering Fields

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Sigfúm has no stat block. His court, advisors, hangers-on, and the size of the population he leads to Terion are unwritten. The parchments themselves and their author (Sigfúm? his clergy?) are blank.
