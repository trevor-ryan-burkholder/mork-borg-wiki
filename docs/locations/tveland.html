Tveland

kingdom / region MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.12

The land that holds Galgenbeck. In Tveland the cruel punishments of the Two-Headed Basilisks are practiced — relic thieves, defamers, and corrupt clerks see their children or grandchildren enclosed in porcelain dolls and placed in cathedral windows to die slowly. Many return as Lady Porcelain undead.

In Tveland also lies Sarkash, the spreading forest that contains Graven-Tosk and the Palace of the Shadow King. The death-obsessed cultists who haunt the Death Ziggurat come from a plague-wracked Tveland hovel.

Adventure seeds describe "the untamed wilds of Tveland" as a place where one's life is risked.

Connections

Galgenbeck, Sarkash, Graven-Tosk, Palace of the Shadow King, Lady Porcelain, Two-Headed Basilisks, Death Ziggurat

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Tveland's borders, ruling political structure outside of Galgenbeck, and its other settlements are unmapped. The Merchant carries "Tveland" goods (Stone Dagger, Tongue of a False Prophet, Two-headed Silver Ring, etc.) implying a regional artisan tradition.
