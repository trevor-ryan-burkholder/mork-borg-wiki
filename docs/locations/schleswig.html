Schleswig

city MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.15

The sad-but-gaudy city of the Western Kingdom. Its secret citadel houses King Fathmu IX, who schemes there paranoid, fat, and mad.

The Fanged Deserter (origin 3) may have lived in "a brothel in Schleswig — quite a friendly environment." The Occult Herbmaster (origin 4) may come from the illegal midnight markets of Schleswig. The Heretical Priest (one origin point) tunnels through its alleys. The Sacrilegious Songbird (origin 5) may have made their deal in a Schleswig prison cell. The surviving Klopstock family member lives here, paying 1000 silver for a ring brought from the Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch — five times the usual asking price.

Adventure seeds: "On a dirty Schleswig street" one risks one's life. "Sinkhole swallows half of Schleswig" (11–12).

Connections

Western Kingdom, Fathmu IX, Klopstock, Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch, Sacrilegious Songbird, Heretical Priest, Occult Herbmaster, Fanged Deserter

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The "secret citadel" is undescribed beyond the phrase. The illegal midnight markets, the brothel district, and the prison are real-by-implication but unwritten. The Klopstock family — only one survivor — has unwritten history.
