Graveyard

location / surface of Graven-Tosk MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.8

the surface — fog, trails that move, named stones nobody set

The above-ground sprawl of Graven-Tosk — headstones tilted in wet earth, paths that lead one place going and somewhere else returning. Every direction out of The Plague Pit opens onto the Graveyard; every named sub-site is somewhere in the fog, and the fog rearranges itself when no one is looking. Only The Roach Herder knows the way without help.

Connections

Graven-Tosk, Graves Left Wanting, The Plague Pit, Undertaker's Hut, The Roach Herder, Roach Herder's Lair, Maus' Vomatorium

Gaps / Expansion Notes

How many graves the Graveyard holds, and how many of them are still occupied, is unwritten. The "Strangly Tree" and the "Origin Fountain" — named in Graves Left Wanting's surface map — sit somewhere in this fog without their own dedicated entries.
