Devil's Tomb

dungeon Devil's Tomb

A formerly abandoned hellhole deep in Graven-Tosk, now occupied by Drowned — amphibian creatures exiled from their poisoned, dried-out lake. They worship the Plant of Life and its angelic children, a giant pulsing cactus growing in every color whose tendrils swirl in the spore-filled air. They have nailed the severed arm of Belpheduk (a traitor among them) to a wall.

The Eyes of the Icon of St Largoth have been stolen and brought here. Looking through them reveals all invisible and hidden things — including the passage to the Plant of Life itself.

The tomb contains:

  • A Madman crawling at the entrance, mad with spore-fever
  • The Pit to Hell, a bottlessless chasm with a narrow bridge across to a chained bronze door reading GO TO NECH in blood
  • Goblins squabbling over a sharp two-handed sword in a side chamber
  • Belpheduk in her traitor's den, hungry and spiteful
  • The Drowned's lair, altar, and hoard
  • The Angelic Choir corridor, where vines and bulbous flowers with angel-faces sing wordlessly
  • Spore-filled chambers that induce visions of death, vertigo, monochromatic outlining, and invisibility-of-the-living
Connections

Graven-Tosk, Drowned, Plant of Life, Belpheduk, Icon of St Largoth, Madman, Goblin, Pit to Hell, Angelic Choir

Gaps / Expansion Notes

What lies beyond the GO TO NECH door at the Pit to Hell is unwritten. The original cult that made the hellhole is gone — only their relics survive. The "poisoned, dried-out lake" the Drowned came from is unnamed and unlocated.
