Colander Room

location / sub-site of Graven-Tosk MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.8

every hole drips, none of them clean

A chamber south of Erhard's Tomb, named for the perforated stone of its ceiling — water, mud, and worse strain through the holes onto the floor below. Whatever was sieved here once is long since gone; only the dripping remains, and the smell of old wet stone.

Connections

Graven-Tosk, Graves Left Wanting, Erhard's Tomb

Gaps / Expansion Notes

What the room was originally used for — funerary draining, ritual washing, torture — is not specified. Whether the dripping ever stops, in any season, is canonically silent.
