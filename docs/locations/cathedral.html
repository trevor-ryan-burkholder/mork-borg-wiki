Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks

religious building MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.10, 12

The mother-church of Verhu's faith and the seat of the Two-Headed Basilisks order. Around it grew Galgenbeck, the greatest city that ever was — the Cathedral predates the city around it.

Three hundred years after the monk Anuk Schleger transcribed the lost Nameless Scriptures, orthodox masons of the Creton branch broke ground here for a new Cathedral and uncovered Schleger's tomb beneath, and the writings with it. Every prophecy since has come to pass.

Deep beneath the Cathedral, in a cool black chamber crossed by shards of light, lies the throne of arch-priestess Josilfa Migol.

Lady Porcelain dolls — the children of relic thieves and corrupt clerks — are placed in cathedral windows to die slowly of starvation and heat as punishment. Many return as vengeful undead.

Connections

Galgenbeck, Two-Headed Basilisks, Verhu, Josilfa Migol, Anuk Schleger, Nameless Scriptures, Lady Porcelain, Creton Order

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The Cathedral's plan, undercroft, library, and the exact location of Schleger's discovered tomb are undescribed. The "Heretical Priest" origin 1 places one such priest "near the cathedral" — implying the area around it has its own warren of heresy.
