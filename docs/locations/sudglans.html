Südglans

sunken city MÖRK BORG Forlorn Philosopher (origin 8)

A city now beneath the sea. One origin of the Forlorn Philosopher is to have been "Südglans' most revered philosopher — which sank into the sea and now everyone ruthlessly mocks you."

Connections

Forlorn Philosopher, Endless Sea

Gaps / Expansion Notes

When Südglans sank, how, who survived besides the mocked philosopher, and whether anything still lives down there is all unwritten. The naming convention (German Süd-glans, "south-shine"?) sets it geographically vague — south of the main map? An exiled colony? Open seed.
