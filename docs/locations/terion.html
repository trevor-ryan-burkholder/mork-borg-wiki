Terion

cliff MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.14

A thousand meters of vertical rock with the raging sea biting at its base. Beneath the cliffs is the Endless Sea; above, the doomed kingdom of Grift. When the last page of his calendar of despair turns, King Sigfúm the Kind will march his people to Terion to fulfill what was written.

A hermit lives at Terion brewing a forbidden drink — The Basilisks Demand occasionally asks for "the forbidden brew of the hermit of Terion."

Connections

Grift, Sigfúm the Kind, Endless Sea, The Basilisks Demand

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The hermit is unnamed. The brew is undescribed. The actual moment of Sigfúm's march — and whether the players can interrupt it — are scenario-shaped openings the source does not script.
