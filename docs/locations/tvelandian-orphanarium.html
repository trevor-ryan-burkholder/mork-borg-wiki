Tvelandian Orphanarium

location / slum institution MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.48 (Gutterborn Scum, Bad Birth 5)

A slum orphanage in or near Tveland — named only in the Gutterborn Scum bad-birth table as the place they escaped. Whatever wardens run it, whatever rations they feed the children, whatever the children turn into when they leave through the wrong door — none of it is on the page. What is on the page is that escape is what one does there.
