Galgenbeck

city / capital MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.12; appears throughout Cult content

The greatest city that ever was. It sprawls in the land of Tveland. No King or Queen rules in Galgenbeck — only the arch-priestess Josilfa Migol, whose throne lies in a cool black chamber crossed by shards of light, deep beneath the Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks.

Commoners gossip that Josilfa colludes with Nechrubel, who gave her eternal life. As time grows ever shorter the Two-Headed Basilisks become ever-more desperate in their recruitment. The Inquisition hunts heretics and apostates and corrects them, in public and at length.

The city has its sanatorium (where one origin of the Forlorn Philosopher is born to a nameless mother), its slums full of Scum, its little witches' cottages where one origin of the Occult Herbmaster is raised. Goblins infest its basements (see Goblin Grinder and Goblin). The alchemist Nagel Krat runs the Medickal Shoppe and the Derelict Mill that hides the Goblin Grinder device.

A Sacrilegious Songbird may have struck their devil's-bargain hiding from guards here. Slave revolts (adventure seed 73–74) and unholy infiltrations of the court (45–46) are local hazards.
