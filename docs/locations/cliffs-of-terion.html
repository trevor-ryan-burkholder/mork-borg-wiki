Cliffs of Terion

location MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.50

Sheer rotten faces of grey stone, mouthed open with caverns no one digs. An Esoteric Hermit is sometimes spat from one such cavern — adult, memoryless, dressed in nothing the wind can name. The locals call the wind here the second tongue and do not speak of what the first one is.

The Cliffs are named distinctly from the wider fragments of Terion scattered through the Miseries; canon does not place them on any map, nor say what coast (if any) they overlook.

Connections

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Coastal or inland? Whose name is Terion and is this cliff the bone of him? The caverns are the deep seed — what does the cave that births a Hermit look like from the inside, and what is still in there.
