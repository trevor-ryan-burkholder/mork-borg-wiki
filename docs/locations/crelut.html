Crëlut

island MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.56 (Occult Herbmaster)

A heretic isle, two nautical miles east of Grift in the Endless Sea. The Occult Herbmaster (origin 5) may come from here.

Connections

Grift, Endless Sea, Occult Herbmaster

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Source gives a single line. The island's heresy, its inhabitants, the herbs and decoctions native to it, and what the Inquisition thinks of it are all unwritten. A natural seed for an island adventure.
