Lake Onda

lake MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.15

Once the lake gifted fish and the river-trade thrived. The Western Kingdom knew peace and wealth then. That was before.

The Basilisks Demand occasionally calls for "a body drowned in Lake Onda." Adventure seed 9–10: "Children missing at Lake Onda."

Connections

Western Kingdom, Schleswig, The Basilisks Demand

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Why the fish are gone and the river-trade dead is unstated. The disappearing children of seed 9–10 are unexplained. What lives in the lake now is open.
