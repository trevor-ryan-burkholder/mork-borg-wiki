Daemon of Capillaries

Power / unclean scroll MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.34 (entry 5); MÖRK BORG Rotblack Sludge, p.14 (Fletcher's repertoire)

Effect. One creature suffocates for d6 rounds, losing d4 hp per round.

A small daemon enters the lungs through a capillary and refuses to leave until it has drunk what little wet warmth lives there. The target chokes on something with no name — air becomes mud, blood becomes string. There is no resistance roll printed. Wielded as one of Fletcher's four cycling Powers in the Forge/slaughterhouse of the Accursed Den.

Connections

Powers, Fletcher, Accursed Den, Rotblack Sludge

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The daemon itself is unnamed and unsummonable outside the scroll. Whether the same daemon answers every casting — and what it does to a wielder who fumbles — is open.
