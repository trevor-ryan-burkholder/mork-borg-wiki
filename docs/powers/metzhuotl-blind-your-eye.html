Metzhuotl Blind Your Eye

Power / unclean scroll MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.34 (entry 7); Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch, p.12 (Yaoxl)

Effect. A creature becomes invisible for d6 rounds or until it is damaged, attacking and defending at DR6.

A name nobody can pronounce on the first try, addressed in command. Metzhuotl, blind your eye. The Power is cast on another, not the self — the recipient is unmade in the air, and while they are unseen they swing and dodge with terrifying ease. Wielded by Yaoxl in the Downward Spiral of the Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch (up to d3 times per day).

Connections

Powers, Srolki & Yaoxl, Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Metzhuotl is named and otherwise unmentioned in any canon. A toad-god of the deep cities, a forgotten witness, the eye itself — all unwritten.
