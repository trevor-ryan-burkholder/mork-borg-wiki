Roskoe's Consuming Glare

Power / sacred scroll MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.35 (entry 9); Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch, p.12 (Yaoxl)

Effect. D4 creatures lose d8 hp each.

A sacred Power, somehow — printed on the Sacred d10 table alongside healing graces and bestial speech, but it consumes the living the way a hot iron consumes paper. Roskoe is named once and never identified: a saint, a sinner, a basilisk, a star that died centuries ago and is only now arriving. Wielded by Yaoxl in the Downward Spiral of the Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch (up to d3 times per day).

Connections

Powers, Srolki & Yaoxl, Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Who or what Roskoe was — and why a Glare bearing his name belongs on the Sacred list — is the entire mystery. A clear expansion seed: a found saint's reliquary, or a heretical icon.
