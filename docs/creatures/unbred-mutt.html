Unbred Mutts

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.6 (Graven-Tosk); MBC_Graves_left_wanting.pdf, p.7

the dogs still waiting at the graves

HP 8, Morale −, Bite d6

Encountered in packs of d6.

Special: No morale. They will not break off. Whatever is between them and the grave they came from, they will bite through.

Lore: It is said Unbred Mutts are the descendants of pedigreed dogs waiting by the graves of their late masters. Some say they're the same dogs, still waiting. The pedigrees are forgotten. The waiting is not.

Connections

Graven-Tosk, Graves Left Wanting, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether each mutt is bound to a specific grave, and what happens if you disturb that grave with the mutt present, is left to the obvious horror beat.
