Gold-crested Filth-crow

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.9 (Wästland)

shining gold, latrine-born

HP 2, Morale −, Beak d4 + special

Special: Toughness DR10 or become infected.

Lore: Carries a feathery ring of shining gold around its collar, a perfect mockery for those unable to afford the real thing. It nests in the muck under latrines and seeks the treasures of the chamber pot. The gold is real. The smell is also real.

Connections

Wästland, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The gold-feathers presumably have a market in Wästland's peasantry as cheap mock-jewellery. Whether the collar is plucked clean or carries the bird's filth with it is the buyer's problem.
