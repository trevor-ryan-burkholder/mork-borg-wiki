Megasloth

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.8 (Kergüs)

sometimes confused for snow-mounds

HP 30, Morale 9, Thick fur −d2, Scythe-claws d10

Special: They can run when they choose to.

Lore: A rare sight on the ice fields. Most hunters who pass one mistake it for a snow-mound and walk on. The mistake is corrected only once. The scythe-claws are taller than a man and slower than a thought, until they are not.

Connections

Kergüs, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether Megasloths sleep for years between hunts, like the bears of warmer lands, is open. The "ice fields" of Kergüs they inhabit are canonical-but-unmapped.
