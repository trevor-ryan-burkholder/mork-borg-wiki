Spider-Owl

creature MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.57

the eight-legged silent flier

The Spider-Owl is the third ingredient of Spider-Owl soup — a decoction brewed by the Occult Herbmaster that grants night-vision and wall-climbing for thirty minutes.

The bird is at least eight-legged. The bird at least flies. Whether the legs end in claws or in venomed feet, whether the eyes are eight or two, whether it spins or merely hunts — canon does not say. The soup is grey, and good.

Connections

Occult Herbmaster, Southern Frog (parallel decoction-ingredient)

Gaps / Expansion Notes

No stat block. No habitat. The Spider-Owl exists in the wiki only as something one boils. Where they nest, how an Herbmaster catches one, whether they are hunted to scarcity — all open. Could share a forest with the Bone Bowyer or roost in Sarkash canopy.
