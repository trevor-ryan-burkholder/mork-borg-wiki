Widow-wraith

creature / undead / spirit Graves Left Wanting, p.7 (random encounter)

the sobbing thing that finds you first

HP 15, Morale —, Icy touch d4 + special

Special: Her sobbing alerts anyone to her presence; she always loses initiative. Her touch drains Strength, Presence, and Agility by 1 for the fight's duration.

Lore: Wanders the cold paths of Graven-Tosk alone, weeping a sound that arrives several seconds before she does. Some say she was buried alongside a husband who refused her in death; some say she buried him and then found out. Either way, the cemetery cannot keep her, and she cannot stop searching the living for warmth that comes off them in clouds.

Connections

Graven-Tosk, Graves Left Wanting, Wraith, The Undertaker, Rotted Skeleton

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the drained Strength/Presence/Agility recover at end of combat (the source says "fight's duration") or whether something longer is implied is a small ambiguity. Her name and husband's name — gone.
