Lich

creature / undead spellcaster MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.60

the anti-magical wound in reality

HP 15, Morale −, Barrier (necro) −d4, Strike d6 + special Special: Paralyzing touch (Presence dr14 every round to break free).

No one can use Powers near this anti-magical wound in reality. Every round they can steal the contents of a nearby scroll and use this Power against its owner.

The book labels this entry the "weak necromancer" variant — implying greater liches exist somewhere.

Loot: Captured 200s — Remains 130s — Skull 100s

The Bedeviled Dungeons table includes "Nechrubel-worshipping lich with a skeletal court" as a possible dungeon-occupant.

Connections

Bedeviled Dungeons, Nechrubel, Powers (general — scroll mechanic)

Gaps / Expansion Notes

What a non-weak lich looks like is unwritten. A specific Nechrubel-worshipping lich with a court is named as a dungeon-occupant possibility but not statted or named individually.
