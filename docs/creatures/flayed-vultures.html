Flayed Vultures

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.4 (Tveland)

fearless climbers, skinless flock

HP 3, Morale −, Bite/Claws d4

Encountered in flocks of d8.

Special: May not be able to fly, but they're fearless climbers. They will scale a sheer rockface, a tower wall, or a man's body with the same patient grip.

Lore: Birds with no skin and no fear of falling. The wind that should have killed them did not. They pull themselves up the cliffs of Tveland on raw red feet and they wait for what falls down.

Connections

Tveland, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether they were always flayed or were skinned by something is unsaid. They eat carrion, but a flock of d8 has no problem speeding it along.
