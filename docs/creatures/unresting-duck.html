Unresting Duck

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.10 (Lake Onda)

lives and dies in the air

HP 2, Morale 7, Beak d4

Encountered in flocks of d8.

Special: Lives and dies in the air. Very skilled at flying. Really hard to hit (DR16).

Lore: They never land. The flock crosses Lake Onda day and night and never touches the water. The old fishermen say the first Unresting Duck was a man cursed for sleeping during a vigil. The new fishermen do not say anything. They are too tired.

Connections

Lake Onda, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

How they breed without nesting, how they drink without landing, and what they eat in flight are all canonical impossibilities the booklet does not address.
