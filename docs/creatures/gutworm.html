The Gutworm

creature Rotblack Sludge

unfathomably long, thick as an oak

Hides in the Rotblack Sludge beneath the Accursed Den. Can easily reach rooms 9, 12, and 15 of the den.

HP 50, Morale −, Thick hide −d6

Razor sharp teeth d10: Test Agility dr6 or be devoured, dying instantly.

Every swim through the sludge has a 1–2 on a d6 chance the Gutworm bites (d10 damage); test Agility dr6 when bitten to avoid being devoured and dying.

Lesdy believes consuming the sludge would let her control creatures like the Gutworm — but when she tries, the Gutworm attacks her on sight.

Connections

Accursed Den, Rotblack Sludge, Lesdy, Fletcher

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The Gutworm's age, origin, and the dimensions of "unfathomably long" are open. The deep caves beneath the sludge — where Lesdy wants to reach — are the Gutworm's natural territory; an extension of the Accursed Den downward would naturally encounter more of it (and more like it).
