Überwolf

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.13 (Bergen Chrypt)

born during a full blood moon, three times the size of a normal wolf

HP 18, Morale −, Thick fur −d2, Bite/Claws d8

A regular wolf (HP 6, Morale 8, Bite d6) follows it in packs of d6 or more.

Special: Pack-leader. Always travels with d6+ regular wolves in close support.

Lore: Born during a full blood moon, the Überwolf is three times the size of a normal wolf. And there are usually d6 or more of those following close behind. It runs the high passes of Bergen Chrypt and the lesser wolves know not to run ahead.

Naming note: Not to be confused with the Übertaker (Graven-Tosk, Graves Left Wanting), which is a possessed undead, not a wolf. No disambiguator needed in filename — they share a prefix only.

Connections

Bergen Chrypt, Eat-Prey-Kill, Fanged Deserter (origin 6 — suckled by a wolf in Bergen Chrypt)
