Cellar Crabs

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.7 (Grift)

the clickety clack of twenty-inch legs

HP 6, Morale −, Shell −d4, Bite/Claws d6

Encountered in groups of d4.

Special: Despite the name, found in any number of cold, dark places — not only cellars. When you hear the clickety clack of their 20-inch legs on the stone floor, you know it's time to leave.

Lore: They are not water-things. They are quiet-things, and dark-things. Grift's basements and storehouses host them, and so do the deeper crypts. The cooking is straightforward. The catching is not.

Connections

Grift, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether Cellar Crabs are an exclusively Grift species or migrate through underground passages between cities is open. Twenty-inch legs imply a body to match.
