Uncommon Rats

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.7 (Grift)

large like hounds, twice the spite, thrice the teeth

HP 6, Morale −, Bite/Claws d8/d6

Encountered in packs of d4.

Special: Twin attack profile — Bite d8 and Claws d6. They can grip and rend in the same round.

Lore: A common sight on the streets of Grift. Large like hounds but with twice the spite and thrice the teeth. The bridge-watchmen keep a cudgel by the door. The bridge-watchmen do not always make it home.

Connections

Grift, Múr, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the Uncommon Rats of Grift have a nest-site under the city, in the bridges' undercrofts, or in some city-cellar that the Cellar Crabs also haunt is open. The bestiary places them on the street.
