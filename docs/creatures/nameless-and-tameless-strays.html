Nameless & Tameless Strays

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.7 (Grift)

eating nothing; yet everything, given the chance

HP 4, Morale −, Bite d4

Encountered in packs of d8.

Special: No fear, no master, no name. They will eat anything that does not eat them first.

Lore: The streets of Grift are home to many. They sleep in doorways and starve in alleys and they do not bark. When they bite they do not let go. There used to be names for them. There used to be many things.

Connections

Grift, Uncommon Rats, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether a Nameless & Tameless Stray could ever be re-tamed, given enough silver and patience, is the soft adventure-seed for a kindhearted PC. The booklet does not say.
