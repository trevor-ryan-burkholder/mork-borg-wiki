Horn Beast

creature The Death Ziggurat

chaotic mass of horns and bones with a toothed mouth-where-a-face-should-be

Summoned by Rot Priests from the bottomless pit in the Necropolis of the Death Realm. A chaotic mass of horns and bones with a circular mouth filled with teeth instead of a face. They rip enemies apart with their sharp claws.

HP 8, Morale −, Shifting bones −d4, Claws d6 (attacks twice a round)

No more beasts will appear if the meditation of the two Rot Priests guarding the pit is stopped.

Connections

Rot Priest, Necropolis, Death Realm, Death Ziggurat

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the bottomless pit is the source of the Horn Beasts or merely a portal to wherever they truly live (more of the Death Realm) is unwritten.
