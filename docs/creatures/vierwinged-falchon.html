creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.13 (Bergen Chrypt)

four-winged, brood-eating, arrogantly extinct

HP 16, Morale −, Bite/Claws d10

Special: Eats only its own brood. The undisputed despot of the mountain skies. Still kills other creatures to assert its dominance.

Lore: With four wings and a mean demeanor, the Vierwinged Falchon is the undisputed despot of the mountain skies. It is arrogance which has brought this species of massive birds close to extinction — it eats only its own brood. When the last falchon dies, no one will know. The Bergen Chrypt sky will simply grow quieter.

Connections

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether any Vierwinged Falchons survive to breeding age, given the brood-eating habit, is a logical canonical impossibility the booklet does not solve. Some implication remains that the species is mid-extinction in the world's last decade.
