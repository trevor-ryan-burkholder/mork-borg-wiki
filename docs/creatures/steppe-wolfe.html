Steppe Wolfe

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.4 (Tveland)

the lanky one, long-legged, short of patience

HP 16, Morale −, Tough skin −d2, Bite d6

Hunts with a pack of d8 lesser wolves.

Special: Outlast it for d4 rounds and the pack might leave you alone. The lanky Steppe Wolfe has long legs but a short patience.

Lore: Longer in the leg than any wolf has right to be. The Steppe Wolfe leads. The pack follows. They will chase you across the open ground for as long as their leader has the will. Sometimes that is not long. More often it is long enough.

Connections

Tveland, Feral Horses, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the Steppe Wolfe is the alpha of a true wolf pack or something the pack merely tolerates is unclear. The "lesser wolves" of the pack share Bare Bones wolf stats by inference.
