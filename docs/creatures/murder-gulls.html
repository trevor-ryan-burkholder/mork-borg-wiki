Múrder Gulls

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.7 (Grift)

sawtooth beaks and sticky putty spewn from their gullets

HP 2, Morale −, Beak d4

Encountered in flocks of d20.

Special: Make their own nesting hollows along the Múr's banks, using sawtooth beaks and a sticky putty spewn from their gullets. A flock can pull a man apart.

Lore: They live in hollows along the Múr's banks — hollows they shape themselves, the putty hardening to stone in the wind. Climbers on the bridges hear them all day. They do not stop. Twenty of them in the sky over Grift is not unusual. Twenty of them on a man is a brief story.

Connections

Grift, Múr, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the gull-putty has any alchemical use (sealing wax? false stone?) is the kind of thing the Merchant might know.
