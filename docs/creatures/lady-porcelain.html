Lady Porcelain

creature / undead MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.61

the doll-children who came back wrong

HP 11, Morale −, Porcelain −d2, Claws/piercing bite d4

In Tveland, relic thieves, defamers, and corrupt clerks suffer a punishment of exquisite and deeply impractical cruelty. Their children or grandchildren are enclosed in porcelain dolls then placed in cathedral windows, to die slowly of starvation and heat. Despite warding rituals, many return as vengeful undead, often gathering in large throngs to hunt their tormentors. Their mad gaze demands a Presence dr12 test at the start of combat to avoid being frozen with fear for d4 rounds.

Loot: Head 20s — Captured 80s

The Bedeviled Dungeons table lists "a vengeful cabal of undead porcelain dolls" as a possible dungeon-occupant.

Connections

Tveland, Galgenbeck, Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks, Two-Headed Basilisks, Inquisition

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The warding rituals that fail are unspecified. Specific named Ladies — the inevitable individual character or three behind a vengeance plot — are absent. The wronged families that suffered the dolls' creation are unnamed.
