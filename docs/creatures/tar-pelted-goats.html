Tar-pelted Goats

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.8 (Kergüs)

charcoal-furred and shamefully edible

HP 6, Morale 7, Fur −d2, Horns d6

Encountered in herds of d8.

Special: Thick, warm fur the color of charcoal. They smell of wooden ships, hemp, coal-tar, and vomit. The meat tastes like a bite, but they made sure of that.

Lore: Freshly coated in tar — by themselves, by herd-instinct, by something darker. They reek of sulfur and vomit and the smoke of burning pitch. Hunters in Kergüs eat them anyway, because Kergüs is what it is, and a meal is a meal.

Connections

Kergüs, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Whether the tar serves an insulating purpose in the cold, repels predators, or is the herd's slow worship of some pitch-black god is left to the cold and the herders.
