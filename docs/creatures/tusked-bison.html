Tusked Bison

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.4 (Tveland)

among the kindest creatures out there — as long as you stay well away

HP 16, Morale 10, Tough skin −d4, Tusks d8

Special: Fighting one means fighting d4 more. The herd does not abandon its own.

Lore: They are patient. They are gentle. They are an entire wall of dark wool and yellow tusks the moment one of them screams. The steppe-hunters of Tveland keep their distance and call them kind, because the dead cannot disagree.

Connections

Tveland, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

The tusks are uncanon for a bison — what happened to grow them is unwritten. Whether the herd mourns is a question for slower travelers.
