Blubber Gulls

creature MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.8 (Kergüs)

flying balls of fat — exploding, on occasion

HP 2, Morale −, Blubber −d2, Beak/Dive Bomb d2/special

Encountered in flocks of d8.

Special: When threatened it will either flee or dive (50%) towards its attacker, blowing itself up, dealing d8 damage to anyone within 5 ft.

Lore: They look like flying balls of fat. They make a good soup. The gas-filled corpses serve as buoys for the few fishermen who still hope. The ones who choose to dive paint the sky white for a moment, then nothing.

Connections

Kergüs, Eat-Prey-Kill

Gaps / Expansion Notes

What the gas is, and why it lights so readily, is a Kergüs alchemist's puzzle. Whether the buoys keep their lift for any useful length of time is also unwritten.
