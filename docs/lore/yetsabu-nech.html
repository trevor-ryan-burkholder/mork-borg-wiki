Yetsabu-Nech

entity / apocalypse MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.19 (Psalm VII, 7:7)

The underworld's nightmare. The black disk which stands before the sun.

When the seventh seal breaks for the seventh and final time the world finally dies. Verhu beams with delight. The fire burns all. And the darkness shall swallow the darkness.

All praise Yetsabu-Nech.

Connections

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Yetsabu-Nech is end-of-game. By the time it appears the campaign ends. Lore expansion is therefore retrospective — cults that anticipated it, prophets who tried to summon it early, the artwork and idolatry around the black disk.
