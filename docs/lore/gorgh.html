Gorgh

basilisk head MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.11

One of two heads borne by HE. Envy. Bitter, rank with envy that only his twin Verhu knows the damned truth. Time and time again Gorgh's prophecies are brought to be. The piles of gold-gift riches from his faithful teeter and slide, so tall are they.

Connections

HE, Verhu, SHE, Valley of the Unfortunate Undead, Two-Headed Basilisks

Gaps / Expansion Notes

Gorgh has prophecies but his own scripture is unwritten. His followers — distinct from those of Verhu — are referenced ("his faithful") but their cult, practices, and where they congregate go undescribed. A schismatic Verhuist sect that secretly venerates Gorgh would write itself.
