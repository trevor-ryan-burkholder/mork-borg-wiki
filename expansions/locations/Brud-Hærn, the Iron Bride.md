# Brud-Hærn, the Iron Bride
**Type:** ruined cathedral / event that refuses to end
**Source:** original expansion — not from canon

A wedding was held here. The wedding is still being held here. Two hundred and forty winters and the bride has not yet reached the altar. Her veil is thirty paces of grey lace dragging through dust and pigeon-bone and she walks one slow step every third sunrise. Whoever the groom was is no longer present in any sense that matters. The priest is a column of salt with a mouth. The witnesses sit in their pews and have, over the long patient years, become the pews.

It is bad luck to be inside Brud-Hærn when she takes a step. The ceiling shifts. Something at the altar makes a small wet sound of expectation. Pilgrims who count her steps say the wedding will be completed on the day everything else ends, and not before, and not after.

The dress is a country. People live in its hem.

**Connections:** rumored to lie somewhere in the bleak country between [[Kergüs]] and the [[Endless Sea]], though the road there changes its mind. The [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] forbid mention of it from the pulpit, which is the surest sign of where to look.

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Who the bride was — a name, a province, a debt. What lives in the hem. Whether the wedding's completion is the apocalypse or only a smaller, more local one. A table of guests who married into the pews and what they remember.
