# Plagueglas
**Type:** frozen lake / cursed mirror
**Source:** original expansion — not from canon

A black lake in a black bowl of hills. It does not thaw. It has not thawed in any season anyone alive remembers and the fish beneath it are still moving. Look down into the ice and you will not see your own face. You will see a face you know. You will see the face of whoever next intends you harm. Sometimes a stranger. Sometimes a brother. Sometimes — and this is the worst — your own, but younger, smiling at something behind you.

Travellers come anyway. Lovers come to make sure. Kings have sent envoys and the envoys have come back changed and the kings have died of it within the year. The ice does not lie. The ice has no opinion. The ice merely shows.

There is a hut on the shore. The hut is always tended. No one has ever met the tender.

**Connections:** sits in cold country — could be in the highlands above [[Bergen Chrypt]], or in some forgotten valley of the [[Sarkash]]. Local children sell scraped ice in jars; the jars are useless, but the children survive on it.

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Who tends the hut. What happens when two people look together and see each other. The fish — what they are, what they remember, whether they may yet surface. Whether the ice can be lied to, and what it costs to try.
