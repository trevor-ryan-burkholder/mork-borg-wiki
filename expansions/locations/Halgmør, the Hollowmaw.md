# Halgmør, the Hollowmaw
**Type:** village / cursed settlement
**Source:** original expansion — not from canon

A village of ninety souls built inside the jaw of a buried god. The teeth are houses. The tongue is a market. Wells weep brown saltwater that tastes of someone else's grief, and once a season a well will name a child it wants. The child is given.

The villagers do not speak of the swallow. They speak around it. Every seventh year a girl is born without a mouth and she lives sixteen years and on the sixteenth she opens, and what comes out is not a voice but a list. Names of the dead. Names of those who will be. Listening is mandatory. Writing it down is forbidden. Most who hear go home and tidy their affairs.

Strangers are welcome. Strangers are also seated nearest the back of every meal, which is the part of the jaw closest to closing.

**Connections:** could sit anywhere the land is soft and old — [[Sarkash]] outskirts, or the dead fields east of [[Galgenbeck]]. Whoever's prophecy is loudest that week, the village hears first.

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** What the well wants the children for. Who first set the first house between the molars. Whether the god is dead, sleeping, or merely embarrassed. A d6 of names the mouthless girl spoke last season — and what happened to each.
