# The Vaulted Stomach
**Type:** pilgrimage site / wound in the world
**Source:** original expansion — not from canon

A hill that breathes. Once every fourteen heartbeats of a healthy man it lifts; once every fourteen it settles. At the crown of it a throat. Pilgrims climb the throat. They are made to undress at the lip and leave their names in a clay bowl. The bowls are not collected. The bowls are also not full.

Inside there is no inside. There is wet warm dark and a slow patient pressing. Pilgrims emerge after a day or a year, never sure which, and they are softer now. The skin sits looser. They smile at the wrong times. They cannot remember any unkindness ever done to them and will weep if pressed to try. Three in every ten do not emerge at all and the hill takes a satisfied breath for them.

Children of pilgrims are sometimes born already digested.

**Connections:** the surrounding country fears it and feeds it. Local prophets sell scraps of bowl-clay as cures for hatred. The [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] have not declared it heresy, which is itself a kind of declaration.

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether anything walks back out at night when the pilgrims have gone home. What the hill was before it learned to breathe. Whether a person made entirely of forgiveness is still a person, and what a [[Scvm]] would do with one.
