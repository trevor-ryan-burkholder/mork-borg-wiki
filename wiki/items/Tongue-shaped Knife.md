# Tongue-shaped Knife
*long, curved, lying in the filth where it was swallowed and forgotten*

**Type:** item / cursed weapon
**Source:** MBC Bloat (Sewage Pit beneath [[Silas the Fattened King]])

A long, curved blade shaped like a tongue, buried beneath the sludge of the **Sewage Pit** where the [[Fleshy Automaton]]s deposit [[Silas the Fattened King|Silas']] collected filth. *"The only thing Silas can't digest is metal."* Beneath the muck: d6 silver and this knife. Found on a careful search or a Presence DR14; the same search may trigger the chamber's falling block (d6 damage).

**Damage:** d6.
**Curse:** *"On a hit, the target becomes ravenously hungry and desperately thirsty — they must eat and drink within the hour or die."*

A relic that compels the same hunger the [[Gourmand's Cutlery]] compels in its wielder, but pushes it through a stranger's flesh instead of one's own. A piece of the chapel-cult's worship that someone — possibly Silas, possibly a priest before him — swallowed by accident or by rite, and never passed.

**Connections:** [[Bloat]], [[Silas the Fattened King]], [[Gourmand's Cutlery]], [[Fleshy Automaton]], [[Goddess of Fat and Plenty]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the knife is one of a set (paired with the Cutlery, perhaps — a fork, a knife, a spoon) is unstated. Whether the compulsion to eat-and-drink can be satisfied by anything (a leaf, a swallow of swamp water) or requires a proper meal is GM-call. Whether the target dies of metaphysical hunger, or of literal starvation collapsed into one cruel hour, is canon-silent.
