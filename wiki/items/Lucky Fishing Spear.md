# Lucky Fishing Spear
**Type:** item / weapon
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.5 (Wästland wares)

D6 damage. When thrown into a body of water, it is guaranteed to strike a living creature. Will instantly kill fish. Might strike much larger creatures.

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Wästland]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. Most lakes give you a trout. A few give you whatever was sleeping under it.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Wästland]], [[Endless Sea]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** "Much larger creatures" is the unwritten threat. Thrown into [[Lake Onda]] or the [[Endless Sea]], anything is possible — a Leviathan, a Carcasswan, a god.
