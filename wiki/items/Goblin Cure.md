# Goblin Cure
**Type:** item / fraud
**Source:** The Goblin Grinder

Not actually a cure. Postpones total transformation by d6 days. Costs 40 silver in the beginning, rising by 5 each day [[Nagel Krat]] remains in business. Sold at [[Medickal Shoppe]].

**Connections:** [[Goblin Curse]], [[Nagel Krat]], [[Medickal Shoppe]], [[Derelict Mill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether a *real* cure for the [[Goblin Curse]] exists is unwritten. Compare the [[Zombie]] cure quest — for that affliction, the cure is real but its location only known to [[Fathmu IX]]. No equivalent exists for goblin transformation.
