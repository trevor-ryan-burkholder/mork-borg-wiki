# Wizard Teeth
*four weird teeth in a blackened pouch*

**Type:** item / charm
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.47

Before battle, roll a d6 for each tooth. For every 6, one of the bearer's attacks deals maximum damage.

A [[Fanged Deserter]] who carries them does not know which wizard gave them up, nor whether the wizard gave them willingly. The pouch is black with old blood. The teeth rattle in it like dice.

**Connections:** [[Fanged Deserter]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** *Wizard* in Mörk Borg is a slippery word — a scroll-reader, a [[Forlorn Philosopher]], an [[Esoteric Hermit]], a [[Heretical Priest]]. Whose teeth these were is open. The number four suggests a complete set was taken from one mouth. A fifth tooth out there somewhere might be a class-defining quest item.
