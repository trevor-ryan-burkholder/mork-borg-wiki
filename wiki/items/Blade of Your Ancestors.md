# The Blade of Your Ancestors
*a foppish, unreliable talking sword that quietly despises you*

**Type:** item / weapon (sentient)
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.53

Magnificent and clearly magical. Deals d6+1 damage. Attack/Defence dr 10.

The Blade speaks. The Blade taunts the [[Wretched Royalty]] who carries it, mocks failures, and — if continually disappointed — develops a 1-in-6 chance to "accidentally" strike its bearer or their companions. The court swordsmith who gave it the voice was hanged. The voice remained.

**Connections:** [[Wretched Royalty]], [[Eurekia]] (the *other* royal blade — cursed where this one is merely cruel)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whose ancestors? The royal line of [[Fathmu IX]]? [[Zigmund]]'s line? An older house lost in [[Südglans]]? The blade's *opinions* could be a whole NPC — what it thinks of [[Anthelia]], of the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], of the dying world. Two royal talking-swords in one class (this and [[Eurekia]]) suggests the line is unusually well-armed and badly cursed.
