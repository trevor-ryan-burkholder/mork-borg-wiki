# Elixir Vitalis
**Type:** item / healing potion
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.66 ([[Outcasts]] — Pale One specialty)

Heals d6 hp and stops infection. The [[Pale One]] Outcast can create d2 doses (specialty 2).

**Connections:** [[Pale One]], [[Outcasts]], [[Occult Herbmaster]] (similar decoction-craft)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Ingredients, brewing process, and shelf-life unwritten. Whether non-Pale-One characters can learn the recipe is open.
