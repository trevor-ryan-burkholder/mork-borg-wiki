# The Wrong Jesus Crucifix
*"666" — the cross of the smothered messiah*

**Type:** item / artifact
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.55

Used in encounters with the undead — and lesser trolls and goblins. Check Morale (add or subtract the [[Heretical Priest]]'s Presence modifier) to see if the creatures bow and kindly remove themselves.

The figure on the cross is wrong. Not the saviour the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] teach. Not the gaunt prophet in any orthodox iconography. Someone else. Someone *smothered* — and yet the undead and the lesser things still bow when shown his face. They remember him. They were here when he died.

[CANON CHECK: the source numbers this gift "666" rather than the expected 6, and the entry's heading reads "666 (wrong Jesus) crucifix". Preserved.]

**Connections:** [[Heretical Priest]], [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Dead Gods]] (parallel slain-god seed), [[Dead God's Prophet]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** *Wrong Jesus* implies a parallel messiah whose iconography survives only in heretic hands. The "666" numbering is mechanically odd and thematically loaded. Connection to [[SHE]] (who slew [[Dead God's Prophet]]'s god) or to the Dead Gods generally is implicit and unspelled. A whole cult-faction — *the Smothered Brethren*, *the Hour-Sixth Confessors* — could be drawn from this object alone.
