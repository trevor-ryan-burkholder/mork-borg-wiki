# One-eyed, One-legged, One-winged Gull
**Type:** item / companion creature
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.6 (Kergüs wares)

Needs to be fed a ration every day. Squawks loudly and irritatingly. Utterly useless and never leaves your side. **Allows you to reroll your [[Omens]] gained when resting.**

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Kergüs]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. A gull missing most of itself, somehow surviving on the worst luck a person can also somehow benefit from.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Kergüs]], [[Omens]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the gull can be killed (deliberately or by a misfire) and what happens to the owner's omens if it dies, is unwritten. Stat block not provided.
