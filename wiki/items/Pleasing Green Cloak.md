# Pleasing Green Cloak
**Type:** item / wearable
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.6 (Kergüs wares)

A viridian cloak of unknown make. Protects against the elements remarkably well — you never feel too cold or too hot while wearing it. It is very itchy however.

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Kergüs]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. A small kindness in a freezing country, sold by a man who freezes between deaths.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Kergüs]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** "Unknown make" — the obvious expansion is to make it known. Woven from feather-fox silk, from the hair of the dead in Graven-Tosk, from the moss that crawls.
