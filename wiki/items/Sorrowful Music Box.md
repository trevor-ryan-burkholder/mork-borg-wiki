# Sorrowful Music Box
**Type:** item / single-use Power
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.7 (Grift wares)

A small music box with a crank. Plays extremely sad music. Those that hear it cannot lie unless they pass a DR12 Presence test. Those affected sob uncontrollably.

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Grift]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. Useful for interrogation, useless in combat, devastating at funerals.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Grift]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the music affects the cranker, and whether undead can lie through it, is unwritten — both possibilities make for cruel session moments.
