# Jar of Troll Piss
**Type:** item / consumable / camp aid
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.5 (Wästland wares)

When spilled on the ground, ensures that no beast dare approach the area. Allows you to make safe camp. Might attract a territorial [[Troll]].

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Wästland]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. A trade in which the cure and the cause carry the same smell.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Wästland]], [[Troll]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The size of the jar, and how many camps it makes, is up to the GM. Whether the territorial troll arrives that night or merely "soon" is left for tension.
