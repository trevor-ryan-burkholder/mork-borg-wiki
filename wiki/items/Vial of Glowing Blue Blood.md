# Vial of Glowing Blue Blood
**Type:** item / poison
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.6 (Kergüs wares)

A vial of blue blood, drawn from deep within [[Kergüs]] itself. May be used as a poison; it freezes the veins of any living creature solid, and they die painfully in d4 rounds. **DR16 Toughness to resist.**

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Kergüs]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. "Drawn from deep within Kergüs" is doing more than geography — the ice itself bleeds, evidently, and the bleeding can be bottled.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Kergüs]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** What lives down there to bleed — a god, a worm, the world's own heart — is left for any expansion that crosses the tundra.
