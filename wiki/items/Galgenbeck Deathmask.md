# Galgenbeck Deathmask
**Type:** item / heretical artifact
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.4 (Tveland wares)

Stolen from an esteemed [[Galgenbeck]] priest's sarcophagus. Placed on a corpse, it allows you to see how the corpse died. Considered heretical theft if you are caught wearing or carrying it.

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Tveland]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. The priest's name and face are gone; what is left is a witness with no mouth.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Tveland]], [[Galgenbeck]], [[Inquisition]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the mask shows the death from the corpse's point of view, or as a detached scene, is unsaid. Whether multiple priests' masks exist, or just this one circulating, is open.
