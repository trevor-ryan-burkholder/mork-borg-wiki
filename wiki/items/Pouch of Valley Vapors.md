# Pouch of Valley Vapors
**Type:** item / consumable
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.5 (Wästland wares)

Strange smelling ashy vapors, not made for living lungs. Opening the pouch and breathing in the vapors **replenishes all your HP and cures infection**, but leaves you catatonic for d4 hours.

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Wästland]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. The "Valley" in the name is unmarked — possibly the [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], whose air has already failed.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Wästland]], [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the catatonic hours include dreaming, or merely lying open-eyed in the dirt while the wound closes, is open.
