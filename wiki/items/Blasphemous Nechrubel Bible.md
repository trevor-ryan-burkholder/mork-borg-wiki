# The Blasphemous Nechrubel Bible
*so blasphemous even the Priests can only peruse it once a day*

**Type:** item / grimoire
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.55

Read once per day. Roll a die:
- **Even:** For the rest of the day, all PCs heal d4 hp after just five minutes' rest.
- **Odd:** The reader is plagued by demonic hallucinations. The GM invents d3 things only the reader can see and describes them to the player *as if true.* The effect ends with sunrise.

The Bible bears [[Nechrubel]]'s name. Whether Nechrubel *wrote* it or is merely the *subject* — its dying god, its accuser, its idol — is not made plain. A [[Heretical Priest]] who carries it learns to fear odd numbers.

**Connections:** [[Heretical Priest]], [[Nechrubel]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] (The Miseries), [[Nameless Scriptures]] (parallel canonical text)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Is the Priest who reads the bible actually reading their own future? The hallucinations are mechanically described as *invented by the GM* — but in canon they might be *visions of true things the world has hidden.* Connection to the [[Nameless Scriptures]] (also of Nechrubel, via [[Verhu]]'s whispers) is implicit and unspelled. Possibly the Bible is the *other* book the monk [[Anuk Schleger]] failed to transcribe.
