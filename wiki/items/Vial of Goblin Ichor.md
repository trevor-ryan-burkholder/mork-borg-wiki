# Vial of Goblin Ichor
**Type:** item / consumable / detection
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.4 (Tveland wares)

Grows hot when [[Goblin]]s are about. Adding a drop of cursed blood causes the mixture to bead up, pointing in the direction of the goblin that cursed you.

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Tveland]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. Useful only to those already marked — most of [[Galgenbeck]] qualifies.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Tveland]], [[Goblin]], [[Goblin Cure]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the ichor cools again once the cursing goblin dies, or whether it merely beads toward where the corpse lies, is unsaid.
