# Old Sigûrd's Sling
*woven from a dead man's grey hair*

**Type:** item / weapon
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.47

2d4 damage. Requires fist-sized rocks, which (perhaps regrettably) are everywhere.

Woven from the long grey hair of [[Sigûrd]], the strongest man whose throat a [[Fanged Deserter]] ever gnawed. The sling has never failed its present owner. Whether it would have failed Sigûrd, had he lived to throw stones with it, is not known.

**Connections:** [[Fanged Deserter]], [[Sigûrd]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The sling is sentient enough to "never fail" — implying minor binding magic, or implying a curse of the *strongest man*'s ghost following the pouch. Whether cutting the hair undoes the binding is a GM call. What rocks the sling refuses, if any, is unwritten.
