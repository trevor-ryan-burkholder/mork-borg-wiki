# Giant's Chisel
**Type:** item / weapon
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.7 (Grift wares)

D4 damage. A chisel-like tool the size of a shortsword. Instead of attacking, you may attempt to dislodge a part of an enemy's armour with a DR14 Strength test. If you succeed, their armour is lowered by one tier.

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Grift]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. A specialist's weapon; useless against the unarmoured, devastating against knights.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Grift]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the chisel can be used on the natural armour of beasts (Troll hide, Daggerback exoskeleton) is implied but unconfirmed. The "Giant" whose tool it is, is not described.
