# Portable Laboratory
*the glass-and-leather kit of the herbmaster*

**Type:** item / equipment
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.56

The engine of the [[Occult Herbmaster]] class. Daily, the laboratory's owner has the materials to create two randomly determined decoctions, and can brew a total of d4 doses. Unused doses lose vitality after 24 hours.

The Laboratory is implied to be glass, leather, brass, and a dozen small horror-organic ingredients constantly going off. It travels on a back, on a mule, or strapped into a barrow. It rattles. It stinks.

**Connections:** [[Occult Herbmaster]], [[Elixir Vitalis]], [[Spider-Owl]], [[Southern Frog]], [[Ezumiel]], [[Fernor]], [[Hyphos]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The Laboratory's weight, fragility, and contents are entirely undescribed. What breaking it does (forced loss of decoction-capacity? small fire? gas cloud?) is GM judgment. The "two random decoctions per day" implies a roll on the page-57 d8 table — the laboratory is, mechanically, that table made physical.
