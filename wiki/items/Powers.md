# Powers (named Scrolls and Tablets)
**Type:** mechanic / lore catalog
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.34–35; Cult content

A few so-called Powers are known, and they are usually found written in scrolls. Roll Presence + d4 every morning to determine how many times you can use Powers that day; choose from your available scrolls. When reading a scroll, test Presence DR12. If you succeed, the Power is activated and you subtract one use from your daily total. If you fail, the Power doesn't work, you lose d2 hp, and you become dizzy for the next hour. During this time, Powers will always fail in the worst possible way.

Scrolls divide into **Unclean** and **Sacred**. Scrolls will never work when wielding zweihand weapons or medium/heavy armor.

The [[Tablets of Ochre Obscurity]] are a separate category — clay tablets behaving as scrolls but with different breakage rules. The [[Forlorn Philosopher]] is uniquely able to read them.

## Named Powers in Canonical Sources

- **Slithering Strangulation** (Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch) — d4 rainbow ethereal boa constrictors emerge from the ground, capture one creature each for d8 rounds, d2 damage per round. Melee attacks on a constricted creature have 50% chance of the snake targeting the attacker.
- **Nine Violet Signs Unknot the Storm** (Rotblack Sludge, Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch) — d2 bolts of lightning dealing d6 damage each. Wielded by [[Fletcher]] and [[Srolki & Yaoxl]].
- **Daemon of Capillaries** (Rotblack Sludge) — one creature chokes for d6 rounds, d4 HP damage per round. Wielded by [[Fletcher]].
- **Ich-bin-luft** (Rotblack Sludge) — unique to [[Fletcher]]. Invisibility for two rounds, can still attack.
- **Metzhuotl Blind Your Eye** (Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch) — wielded by [[Srolki & Yaoxl]].
- **Roskoe's Consuming Glare** (Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch) — wielded by [[Srolki & Yaoxl]].
- **Deathlike Silence** (Death Ziggurat) — wielded by high-rank death-obsessed cultists. Presence DR14 or deafened d10 minutes.
- **Beechwood Tube Protean Power** (Rotblack Sludge) — sold by [[Sagsobuth]]; a different unclean Power each dawn, single-use, misinterpreted on 1–3.

**Arcane Catastrophes** (Bare Bones p.43–45) describe what happens on a fumble while wielding Powers — a d20 table of nightmare bargains and impossible errands. Most entries are lost to the typographic spread; only entry 19, **[[Cube-Violet]]**, is recoverable in extraction. It describes four trials inside a perfect empty cube: slay riddling [[Kulvan]] who holds three colourless pearls; poison a close friend with [[Sict-Shroom]]; reach up through fire to a golden key (d4 fingers burn to ash); wait in maddening timeless silence until another fool arrives. The remaining 19 entries are a [CANON CHECK] gap.
