# Wickhead Brain
**Type:** item / consumable / light
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.4 (Tveland wares)

When rubbed, emits a light equivalent to a torch. Extremely fragile — if broken, everyone in the vicinity must succeed a DR12 Toughness test or be blinded for one round.

Harvested from a [[Wickhead]], the head-douser of [[Sarkash]] thickets. Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Tveland]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. There is a small irony in carrying the lantern of a creature whose specialty is putting lanterns out.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Tveland]], [[Wickhead]], [[Sarkash]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether a rubbed brain attracts other Wickheads — and whether the light has the same shape as a Wickhead's own ignited skull — is open.
