# Little Poppet
**Type:** item / unique Power
**Source:** MBC The Merchant, p.7 (Grift wares)

A small sack doll filled with hay. When in your possession, it allows you to cast a unique [[Powers|Power]]:

**You peer into the mind of one person whose eyes you can see. You know their thoughts, their intentions, and what their immediate next action will be.**

Sold by [[Mikhael the Merchant]] in [[Grift]] for a portion of the buyer's soul. Mikhael does not specify how many times per day, nor whether the Power follows the usual scroll-DR rules — read it carefully at the table.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Merchant Inventory]], [[Grift]], [[Powers]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The Power is described as "unique" but no daily cap is printed; GM may rule it once per day, once per long rest, or at the standard Presence + d4 budget. Whether breaking or burning the doll has any cost is unwritten.
