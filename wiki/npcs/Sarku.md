# Sarku
*the sharp-tongued ghost who once stood at Akünh's side*

**Type:** NPC / ghost
**Source:** The Death Ziggurat

Once [[Akünh]]'s closest advisor. He left her when he realized her demonic origin. A gaunt, ghostly form with a long, sharp tongue that sucks the blood of his victims and stretches their tongues. The dried-up corpses hang upside down from the trees around the [[Death Ziggurat]] depression.

He wants to be set free from his earthly prison and move on to the afterlife. Knows where Akünh's heart is and that stabbing it kills her.

**Stat block:**
HP 15, Morale 8, No armor
- **Blood-sucking tongue (d4).** A hit means you are grappled, resulting in automatic damage in future rounds until you break free with a DR14 Strength test.
- **Ethereal.** Immune to physical harm.

**Connections:** [[Akünh]], [[Death Ziggurat]], [[Sarkash]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Why Sarku is bound here despite wishing to depart is unstated. Whether helping him achieve the afterlife requires destroying Akünh's heart, fulfilling a separate task, or something else entirely is open.
