# Urvan Krat
*the alchemist father, three times useless*

**Type:** NPC / deceased
**Source:** The Goblin Grinder

Father, mentor, and teacher to [[Nagel Krat]]. Perished due to poison planted in his medication by an unidentified assassin (a convenient mystery from which Nagel benefits). Despite fulfilling a threefold role in Nagel's life, Urvan was never very good at any of them.

His body lies in the basement of the [[Derelict Mill]] — heavily disfigured, bloody, and rotting. Nagel recently took some anger out on the corpse.

Search of the body yields:
- A vial of sour yellow liquid (alchemical pain remedy: heals d6+1, dulls senses, −2 Presence for 1 hour)
- A spare key, hidden in his boot. **Activates the [[Goblin Grinder]].**

He was the original owner of the mill. Whether he knew of the Goblin Grinder device before death — and what he intended to do with it — is unwritten.

**Connections:** [[Nagel Krat]], [[Derelict Mill]], [[Goblin Grinder]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The actual assassin is undefined — possibly Nagel hired the work out, possibly an unrelated enemy beat him to it. Urvan's career, his original alchemy practice, and what the mill once produced are all open.
