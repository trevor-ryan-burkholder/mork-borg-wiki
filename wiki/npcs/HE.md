# HE
**Type:** NPC / basilisk / deity-as-actor
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.10–11, 16; adventure seed 69–70 (p.70)

> See lore entry: **[[HE]]** (in `wiki/lore/`) for cosmology, parentage, and the Verhu/Gorgh body. This page covers HE **as an active presence in play** — his crypts, his demands, his pilgrims.

The basilisk who survived. [[SHE]] cast every spawn down the cliffs of [[Bergen Chrypt]] and HE crawled away. He bears two heads — [[Verhu]] (Prophecy) and [[Gorgh]] (Envy) — and he coils in the crypts of the [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], eyes locked upon the mountain peak where his mother lives, spitting curses for centuries.

If anything in canon is a *place that is a creature*, it is HE. Adventure modules treat him as a destination.

## What He Does

- **He demands.** Adventure seed 69–70: *"HE demands a gift. See it delivered."* This is the only canonical adventure hook for HE-as-actor. The gift is unspecified. The crypts of the Valley are the address. The delivery is whatever the GM makes it.
- **He receives pilgrims.** Bare Bones p.16: *"Others plumb the crypts seeking Verhu, believing they can persuade him of other fates. Some simply and stupidly leave gifts and sacrifices to a power they cannot comprehend."* HE is the body. Verhu is the mouth. The pilgrims do not always understand the distinction.
- **He kills onlookers.** *"A sight infrequently survived."* Whether the death is a basilisk-stare in the classical sense, or the slow Valley undeath claiming pilgrims who never make it back, is left ambiguous.
- **He hates [[SHE]].** Continuously. His position in the Valley is chosen for the angle of view — he can stare up at Bergen Chrypt's peak and curse her.

## How He Appears in Scenes

HE is a location-encounter. Approaching HE means:

1. Traveling into the [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]] — soil that drags travelers into the [[Realm of the Dead]], air that brings *"sleepless, stumbling death,"* gloom growing over everything.
2. Descending into the crypts. Source does not map them.
3. Reaching the basilisk. Facing both heads at once — [[Verhu]] who will prophesy, [[Gorgh]] who will curse and demand metal.
4. Surviving the audience. (Most do not.)

The "gift" of seed 69–70 frames a whole module: who commissioned the gift, what HE actually requires, what happens to the courier when they arrive.

## Relationships as Actor

- **Mother:** [[SHE]] — cursed eternally; HE is the only spawn who survived the cliffs.
- **Heads (he *is* both):** [[Verhu]] (Prophecy), [[Gorgh]] (Envy). His body cannot be petitioned separately from either head.
- **Half-sibling:** [[Akünh]] (also a spawn of [[SHE]], though by the Death-Realm parent rather than the cliffs).
- **Worshippers:** The [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] worship Verhu through HE's body. Gorgh-faithful (unnamed) leave gold to the other head. Pilgrims to the Valley plead with HE for "other fates."
- **Hunts:** No one. HE does not move. The world comes to him.

The "gift" of seed 69–70 has no canonical content — it is one of the most expansion-friendly hooks in Bare Bones. Whether HE can be slain, and what happens to Verhu's prophecies if so, is the doomsday question canon refuses to answer.
