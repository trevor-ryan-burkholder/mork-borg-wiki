# Aldon
**Type:** NPC
**Source:** Rotblack Sludge

The Shadow King's one true heir. His son.

He sits on a bench in the Son's Room of the [[Accursed Den]], pudgy and arrogant, gnawing human bones. The masked Seer who hired the players warned: *"I fear Aldon may be unwilling to leave this place."*

He has been corrupted by the den. A bullwhip with cryptic runes (d4 + necro-plasmic shock) hangs on his wall.

**Connections:** [[Shadow King]], [[Accursed Den]], [[Fletcher]], [[Rotblack Sludge]], [[Palace of the Shadow King]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Aldon has no stat block — players who try to extract him face an unscripted social/violent encounter. The runic bullwhip's origin and its connection to the Shadow King line is unwritten. Whether Aldon, once returned, can ever rule sanely is left to the GM.
