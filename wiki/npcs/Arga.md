# Arga
*a Galgenbeck priest, hardiest of the Deadn't, who buried the Undertaker and did not bury her well enough*

**Type:** NPC / priest, one of [[The Deadn't]]
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.16

A priestess of the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] out of [[Galgenbeck]]'s [[Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks|Cathedral]], now stranded in [[Graven-Tosk]] with [[Stein]] the [[Grift]] merchant and [[Benzen]] the [[Schleswig]] guard. The three of them confronted [[The Undertaker]] in the [[Undertaker's Hut]] to demand a way out of the cemetery. The Undertaker refused. They killed her. Arga buried her. The cemetery would not let them leave.

She is the hardiest and steadiest of the trio — **Morale 10** in a place that breaks Morale — and the only one of them who can read what the scroll in her belt actually says.

**Stat block:**
HP 4, Morale 10, Scale −d4, Staff d4
Carries a **random sacred scroll**.

**Connections:** [[The Deadn't]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], [[Graven-Tosk]], [[The Undertaker]], [[The Übertaker]], [[The Undertaker's Hut]], [[Galgenbeck]], [[Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Stein]], [[Benzen]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Which sacred scroll she carries is rolled at the table. Her standing in the Cathedral before she left — pilgrim, postulant, deserter, exile — is unwritten. Whether she still believes Verhu's prophecies, after burying a gravedigger who got back up, is a fine roleplay knife. Her name is not from the Bare Bones d6 / d8 name table; canon does not say where it comes from.
