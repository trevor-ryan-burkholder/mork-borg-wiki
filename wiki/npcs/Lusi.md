# Lusi
**Type:** NPC / basilisk-head / deity-as-actor
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.11

> See lore entry: **[[Lusi]]** (in `wiki/lore/`) for the head of Denial's cosmology. This page covers Lusi **as an active presence in play** — what she says, who echoes her, where her presence is felt.

One of two heads borne by [[SHE]]. Lusi is **Denial**. She looks up and looks down. She says, always: *yet all shall be well.*

She is the head least described in all of canon. Source devotes a single sentence and a single phrase to her, and they contradict everything else the Two-Headed Basilisk faith stands on. The world is dying. Verhu predicts annihilation. Lusi says it will be fine.

## What She Does

- **She says it's fine.** That is the entire canonical content of her ministry. *Yet all shall be well.* When everything is going wrong, Lusi's path says it is not. When the Misery wakes, Lusi's path says it didn't. When the world ends, Lusi will say it hasn't.
- **She is walked.** Source: *"many walk her twin paths"* — paired with [[Arkh]], her twin (Deception). Lusi's path is denial of the truth; Arkh's is the lie itself. Together they form a counter-faith to [[Verhu]]'s apocalypse-cult.
- **Her name surfaces in chants.** In the [[Accursed Den]] (room 11), [[Lesdy]]'s hosts chant *"Lesdy... Lusi... Lesdy... the chosen, the delightful!"* before attacking — the only canonical instance of Lusi's name spoken aloud in a scenario. Whether Lesdy is a true Lusi-cultist or a syncretist using the name is open.

## How She Appears in Scenes

She does not appear. Lusi is the canonical embodiment of *not appearing*. Her followers appear. Her name is invoked. Her phrase is repeated. The basilisk-head itself sits atop [[SHE]] in [[Bergen Chrypt]], looks up and down, and says it's fine.

A PC trying to find Lusi will be told there is nothing to find. A PC trying to confirm a disaster will hear Lusi's followers tell them no disaster has occurred. A PC carrying news of the [[Calendar of Nechrubel]]'s daily roll will be politely asked to be quiet.

## Relationships as Actor

- **Mother:** [[SHE]].
- **Twin:** [[Arkh]] (Deception — Lusi denies; Arkh lies).
- **Rivals:** [[Verhu]] (Prophecy — Lusi denies what Verhu predicts); [[Gorgh]] (Envy — too busy resenting Verhu to bother denying anything).
- **Possible followers:** [[Lesdy]] and her three hosts in the [[Accursed Den]] chant her name. Unconfirmed whether this is a real cult or appropriated invocation.

**Connections:** [[Lusi]] (lore), [[SHE]], [[Arkh]], [[Verhu]], [[Gorgh]], [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Bergen Chrypt]], [[Lesdy]], [[Accursed Den]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** No stat block. No physical description beyond *"looks up and down."* Her "twin paths" with Arkh are unmapped. The Lesdy connection is the only scenario-level appearance of her name and is itself ambiguous — Lesdy uses the chant but pursues sludge-power and Gutworm control, neither of which obviously serves Lusi. Whether Lusi-as-cosmic-denial *enables* the world to keep functioning (the only reason the apocalypse hasn't already happened is that her path keeps people from believing it will) is a perfect GM thread and entirely uncanonical.
