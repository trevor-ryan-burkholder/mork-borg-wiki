# Verhu
**Type:** NPC / basilisk-head / deity-as-actor
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.10–11; adventure seeds p.69 (21–22)

> See lore entry: **[[Verhu]]** (in `wiki/lore/`) for cosmology, prophecy, and the Two-Headed Basilisks faith. This page covers Verhu **as an active presence in play** — his demands, his interventions, and what petitioners actually do at his altar.

The head of **Prophecy** atop [[HE]]'s body. Loud. Full of himself. The only basilisk who has ever been *right*, and he has been right every single time. His clergy — the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] — rule [[Galgenbeck]] in his name. His tithe-piles are gold-gift mountains. He hungers for new temples and accepts strange tributes.

## What He Does

- **He prophesies.** Through the [[Nameless Scriptures]] transcribed by [[Anuk Schleger]] in 565. Every prophecy has come true. The world is currently following them, verse by verse, on the [[Calendar of Nechrubel]].
- **He whispers.** [[Nechrubel]] is said to whisper into Verhu's ear; Verhu whispered to Schleger. Whether he still whispers to anyone alive is GM-judgment.
- **He receives.** Pilgrims plumb the crypts of the [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]] *"believing they can persuade him of other fates"* (Bare Bones p.16). Some leave gifts and sacrifices to a power they cannot comprehend.
- **He demands.** Per adventure seed 69–70: *"HE demands a gift. See it delivered."* The gift is unspecified. The delivery is the adventure.
- **He gloats.** At **7:7**, the final Misery: *"All praise Verhu, beaming with delight!"* — the only time the canon shows him reacting to anything. He greets the end laughing.

## How He Appears in Scenes

Verhu is not a creature you meet in a corridor. He is one of the two heads of [[HE]] and shares a body with [[Gorgh]] in the Valley crypts. He is approached, not encountered. Petitioners go to him. Sacrifices are left at his altar. His clergy interpret his will from [[Anuk Schleger]]'s transcriptions and the daily roll of the [[Calendar of Nechrubel]].

A [[Heretical Priest]] (origin 4) starts the game already trying to talk him out of the apocalypse. Adventure seed 21–22 — *"Verhu's prophecy is false!"* — opens that door from the GM's side.

## Relationships as Actor

- **Above:** [[Nechrubel]] (whisperer). The Two-Headed Basilisks worship Verhu; Verhu listens to Nechrubel.
- **Beside:** [[Gorgh]] (twin head, Envy — rots beside him resentful that only Verhu knows the truth).
- **Beneath:** [[Josilfa Migol]] (arch-priestess, [[Galgenbeck]]); the [[Inquisition]] (his enforcement arm); [[Anuk Schleger]] (his stenographer, dead 300 years and counting).
- **Hunts:** [[Heretical Priest]]s who would unmake his predictions.

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** No stat block — Verhu is not statted as a combatant in any surveyed PDF. Whether his head can be approached separately from [[Gorgh]] (same body, opposite mood), and what happens if you actually *find* him in the Valley crypts, is unwritten. The "strange tributes" he demands have no canonical table. Whether the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] clergy speaks *to* him or only *for* him is open. Adventure-seed 21–22 is the one published hook that says yes, you can try to make him wrong.
