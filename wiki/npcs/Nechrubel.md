# Nechrubel
**Type:** NPC / shadow-god / deity-as-actor
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.12; Psalm 6:6 (p.19); adventure seed p.71 entry 2

> See lore entry: **[[Nechrubel]]** (in `wiki/lore/`) for cosmology and the Calendar that bears his name. This page covers Nechrubel **as an active presence in play** — who he reaches, what he gives, what he takes.

The shadow that covers all. Not a creature you meet — a *condition*. Nechrubel **is** melancholy. He **is** crop failure. He **is** conflict, war, and the long whisper that bent [[Verhu]]'s ear into prophecy. He is the only canonical entity who shapes the world *through* the Two-Headed Basilisk faith without being part of it.

## What He Does

- **He whispers.** The [[Nameless Scriptures]] are his whisper at second hand: Nechrubel → [[Verhu]] → [[Anuk Schleger]]. The Calendar bears his name because the apocalypse is his idea.
- **He bargains.** [[Josilfa Migol]] — arch-priestess of [[Galgenbeck]], old but still young — *"colludes with him, who gave her eternal life."* Terms unwritten. She rules from a cool black chamber under the [[Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks]]. He gets something. She gets unending years.
- **He sends underlings.** [[Daejmon]], his left underling, sunders the Veil at Misery **6:6** *"so the unnamed may enter the earth."* His right underling is never named. There are two; only one has been spoken aloud.
- **He has worshippers.** Bedeviled Dungeons adventure seed, p.71 entry 2: *"Nechrubel-worshipping lich with a skeletal court."* The only canonical hint that anyone prays *directly* to him rather than through Verhu.

## How He Appears in Scenes

Almost never directly. Nechrubel is a force the PCs meet through:

- The [[Calendar of Nechrubel|Calendar]] — daily die rolls that flip the world toward 7:7.
- The arch-priestess he keeps young.
- His underling [[Daejmon]] tearing the Veil at 6:6.
- The dead-and-undying — liches whose skeletal courts kneel to him directly, outside the Two-Headed Basilisk hierarchy.
- The *Goddess of Fat and Plenty* line ("Nechrubel is melancholy") — he is named where misery, famine, and despair are.

He is the **god of doing nothing**, in the sense that he is what fills the spaces where good things stopped happening.

## Relationships as Actor

- **Above (peer):** [[Yetsabu-Nech]] (the black disk at 7:7). Source does not say Nechrubel commands Yetsabu-Nech — both are end-state apocalyptic entities, possibly the same thing under two names.
- **Beneath (mouthpiece):** [[Verhu]] (Nechrubel whispered the prophecies into his ear).
- **Bound to him:** [[Josilfa Migol]] (life-for-collusion bargain); [[Daejmon]] (left underling); unnamed right underling.
- **Worshipped directly by:** the Nechrubel-worshipping lich (p.71 seed 2) and his skeletal court — somewhere out there.
