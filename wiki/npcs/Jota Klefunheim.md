# Jota Klefunheim
*tall, perpetually annoyed, some kind of baroness or princess*

**Type:** NPC / noble
**Source:** MBC The Goblin Grinder, p.4

Baroness (or princess — even her hire-script does not commit) of **[[Klefunheim]]**, presently *"unaffected by the goblin curse but — with those disgusting peasants running around all the time — it's bound to happen."* She hires the PCs to uncover and end [[Galgenbeck]]'s goblin trouble before the trouble reaches her gate, paying **200 silver** (*"reduced by 20 each day the problem isn't resolved"*) for the work.

She does not appear in person beyond this hire. She has no stat block. She is a voice on the other side of a desk, paying down by the day.

**Connections:** [[Klefunheim]], [[Galgenbeck]], [[Goblin Grinder]], [[Goblin]], [[Goblin Cure]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Everything past the brief. Her appearance beyond "tall" is unwritten. Whether she has a court, a retinue, a husband, an heir, a guard, an existing rival, or simply a clerk, is GM canvas. The Klefunheim Deathmask in [[Mikhael the Merchant]]'s wares may be a family good, family heirloom, or family victim — none stated. Her annoyance is the only constant.
