# Poltroon the Court Jester
**Type:** NPC / retainer
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.53

Practically useless. Personally irritating. An emotional drain. And yet — when the swords come out, Poltroon's capering somehow throws the enemy off. For the first two rounds, the [[Wretched Royalty]] and their allies gain +2 on attack and defence.

The jest does not land. The face-paint is smeared. The bells are tarnished. The enemy stares too long, trying to understand what Poltroon is *for*, and that staring is the gift.

**Connections:** [[Wretched Royalty]], [[Hamfund the Squire]], [[Barbarister the Incredible Horse]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the jester knows the effect they have is unwritten. Whether they were *hired*, *inherited*, or *summoned* as part of a court that cannot now dismiss them is unwritten. The name is in scare-quotes in the source, suggesting it is a title or a mask rather than a birth-name.
