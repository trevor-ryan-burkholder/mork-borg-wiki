# Zigmund
**Type:** NPC (deceased) / royal
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.52

Brother of King [[Fathmu IX]]. Murdered. He was the father of a [[Wretched Royalty]] who survives now in tatters and rumor, scratched out of the succession by whoever ordered the knife.

Whoever did the killing is unwritten. The motive is unwritten. Whether the murder was political, or whether one of the older powers stepped through Fathmu's court to spill royal blood for its own reasons — unwritten.

**Connections:** [[Fathmu IX]], [[Wretched Royalty]], [[Western Kingdom]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Zigmund is named only on the Wretched Royalty spread. The killer is the largest seed in the class — a hook for any vengeance arc. Possible suspects: [[Fathmu IX]] himself, the [[Shadow King]]'s court, [[Anthelia]]'s collectors, the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], or an unrelated assassin paid in Wästland silver.
