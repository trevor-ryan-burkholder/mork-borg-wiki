# Ezumiel
**Type:** NPC (historic / namesake)
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.57

A name remembered only by a vapor. *Ezumiel's vapor* — a decoction brewed by an [[Occult Herbmaster]]: pass a dr14 test or suffer severe (and arguably fun) hallucinations for d4 hours.

Whoever first named the recipe is gone. Whether *Ezumiel* was the alchemist who distilled it, the saint who survived it, the demon who whispered it, or the first to die screaming under its visions — canon does not say.

**Connections:** [[Occult Herbmaster]], [[Fernor]], [[Hyphos]] (other named brewers)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Ezumiel, [[Fernor]], and [[Hyphos]] are three names of the poisoner-trade preserved only through the decoctions that bear them. A small faction or lineage — the *named brewers*, a half-pantheon of toxicologists — could be drawn from this trio.
