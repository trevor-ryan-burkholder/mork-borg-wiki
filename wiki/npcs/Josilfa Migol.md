# Josilfa Migol
**Type:** NPC / arch-priestess
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.12

Arch-priestess of [[Galgenbeck]]. No King or Queen rules in Galgenbeck — Josilfa does, from a cool black chamber crossed by shards of light deep beneath the [[Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks]].

Old but still young. Commoners gossip that she colludes with [[Nechrubel]], who gave her eternal life.

**Connections:** [[Galgenbeck]], [[Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Nechrubel]], [[Verhu]], [[Inquisition]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Josilfa has no stat block. Her predecessor is unnamed; her successor (if any is groomed) is unmentioned. The terms of her bargain with Nechrubel are speculation in source — a perfect adventure-thread to make canonical at the table.
