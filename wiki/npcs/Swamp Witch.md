# The Swamp Witch
*the diamond-eyed figure floating above the empty sarcophagus*

**Type:** NPC / witch / undead
**Source:** Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch

A scraggy figure dressed in a green robe. Instead of skin she is covered in multi-colored scales, and where her head should be is just black smoke and two bright diamond eyes. On her back is the [[Lunar Zweihänder]], a shining greatsword longer than she is. She floats two feet above the ground in the tomb of the [[Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch]].

She communicates telepathically with either whispers or screeches. She would very much enjoy seeing the party make a wish and pledge at the [[Dead Root Altar]] and can instruct them how, whispering seductive promises of individual gains to them if they just find an unwilling person to pledge. *After all, would all members of the party truly be missed in this world?*

**Stat block:**
HP 30, Morale −, Ethereal barrier −d4, [[Lunar Zweihänder]] d12 + radiance.
- **Radiance.** The Lunar Zweihänder is made from moonlight, ignores all non-magical armor, and each hit fills the room with radiant light. Everyone within 50' tests Presence DR12 or takes d4 damage.
- **Eternal.** As long as the [[Dead Root Altar]] stands, the Swamp Witch does not die at 0 HP but instead evaporates and reforms in the sarcophagus with full health 3d6 rounds later. Should the Swamp Witch truly die, the [[Emerald Serpent]]s, the barrier blocking the Downward Spiral, and all [[Emerald Venom]] hallucinations disappear forever.

**Connections:** [[Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch]], [[Dead Root Altar]], [[Lunar Zweihänder]], [[Emerald Serpent]], [[Emerald Venom]], [[Strange Serpent Drug Cult]] (lairs in her tomb, mostly unaware of her), [[Srolki & Yaoxl]], [[Srolki & Yaoxl]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Her history — when she became the Swamp Witch, why she sleeps in an empty sarcophagus, the original "lost symbols" carved on the altar — is all unwritten. The relationship between her and the cities of three-eyed toads below the Downward Spiral is open.
