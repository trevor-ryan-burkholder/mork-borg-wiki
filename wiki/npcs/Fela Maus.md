# Fela Maus
*the legendary bard-made-noble, drowned in her own bile*

**Type:** NPC / dead noble / curse-victim
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.10 (Maus' Vomatorium)

*"The legendary bard-made-noble Fela Maus was cursed by a bog hag after neglecting to fulfill her part of a long-forgotten occult enterprise."*

She is no longer animate. Her bones float in **[[Maus' Vomatorium]]** — a marble coffin atop a dais leaking yellow gall from a bubbling lid, set in an ornate stone chamber whose flagstone floor has been eroded by pools of green and yellow gall over centuries. The sour stomach-broth corrodes flesh but not bone, dealing d6 damage per round near the dais. *Walking up to the coffin surely results in burnt flesh and ruined shoes.*

Floating with her in the wicked liquid is **[[the Roseate Baritona]]** — her favorite instrument, a heresy-inscribed cursed organ. Human-sized ivory horn bedecked with Maus' family's skulls and bound in magical entrails. When blown, it spews a noise that forces listeners to test Toughness DR10 or vomit acid, taking d6 damage. *Blower beware.*

The bog hag's punishment turned the bard's instrument against her family's faces and reduced her noble line to a slick of skull and gall. That she rose to nobility from a wandering bard's life is the only canonical detail about her career — the songs she sang, the rooms she sang in, and the occult enterprise she failed to complete are gone with her.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Maus' Vomatorium]], [[Roseate Baritona]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], the bog hag (unnamed)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The bog hag is named in canon once and never described — possibly a [[Wästland]] bog-witch (compare [[Pale One]] origin 5: *"hatched from an egg laid by a bog-witch in a Wästland swamp"*), possibly something older. The "long-forgotten occult enterprise" Maus failed is opaque — a pact? a song? a marriage? Maus' family is named through their skulls on the Baritona but no individual member is identified. Whether her bones can be reassembled and re-cursed is uncommented.
