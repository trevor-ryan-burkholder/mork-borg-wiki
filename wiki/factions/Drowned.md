# The Drowned
**Type:** people / race
**Source:** Devil's Tomb

Amphibian creatures exiled from their poisoned, dried-out lake. They have taken refuge in [[Devil's Tomb]] deep in [[Graven-Tosk]], where they sleep in fleshy cocoons in a flooded chamber lit by waterfall-drowned light. Ten are present at any time; four more are usually out raiding.

They worship the [[Plant of Life]] and its angelic children. Their altar — the [[Drowned Altar]] — is a circular granite slab on which a dead Drowned is laid, covered in blue leaves and vines. Thrice daily they mourn him.

Their hoard is a mud-covered jumble of shiny objects: coins, cutlery, chains, nails, horseshoes. The [[Icon of St Largoth]]'s stolen Eyes are kept here.

**Stat block (single Drowned):**
HP 6, Morale 8, Scales −d2, Spear d6.
*Immune to the spores' effects. Upon death, freezes the water nearby, dealing d4 damage.*

The traitor [[Belpheduk]] killed a fellow Drowned and was punished by imprisonment in the Traitor's Den, a muddy cramped cave with her severed arm nailed to the wall.

**Connections:** [[Devil's Tomb]], [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Plant of Life]], [[Drowned Altar]], [[Belpheduk]], [[Icon of St Largoth]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The poisoned lake they fled from is unnamed and unlocated — possibly any number of the world's many failing waters. Their language, leadership, religion before the Plant of Life, and the source of their freezing-on-death are unwritten.
