# Berserker-Slayers
*the frenzy summoned from the dimension of blood*

**Type:** faction / creature (summoned)
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.51 (referenced); p.59 (stat block, as Zukuma [[Berserker]])

When the [[Esoteric Hermit]] opens the [[Book of Boiling Blood]] and reads aloud, d2 Berserker-Slayers may be torn from a forgotten dimension of blood and dropped into the room. On d6 1–4 they fight beside the reader; on 5–6 they turn on the reader and the book, intent on destroying both. After the battle they return to whatever imprisonment held them.

Statistically they are [[Berserker]]s (Zukuma): HP 13, Morale 9, hardened skin −d2, attacking twice per round, no time for defence (dr10 to hit). Whether they are *the same beings* as the catacomb-Berserkers — pulled from this world into the bleeding dimension and pulled back out — or *a parallel cohort* born in the blood, the book does not say.

**Connections:** [[Esoteric Hermit]], [[Book of Boiling Blood]], [[Berserker]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The *forgotten dimension of blood* is named only here — a prison-realm where these things wait between summons. Who built the prison, and whose blood it is built from, is the seed. The name *Berserker-Slayers* (vs. *Berserkers*) implies they kill berserkers — yet their stat block is identical. Perhaps they are slayers of *themselves.*
