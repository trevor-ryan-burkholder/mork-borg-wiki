# Udok Cultists
**Type:** cult
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Forlorn Philosopher (origin 7)

Named once. They captured the [[Forlorn Philosopher]]'s family (one possible backstory) and forced him to debate with them while they devoured his parents alive.

**Connections:** [[Forlorn Philosopher]], [[Inquisition]] (possibly a sub-order or rival; speculation)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Pure expansion seed. Udok could be a name, a deity, an order, or a derived adjective. The "debate while devouring" ritual is canonical but unique to this single line.
