# Slithering Strangulation
**Type:** Power / unclean scroll
**Source:** Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch, p.6 (Scalehunter's Chamber)

**Effect.** D4 rainbow-coloured ethereal boa constrictors emerge from the ground and capture one creature each for d8 rounds, dealing d2 damage each round. Attacking a constricted creature in melee comes with a 50% risk of the snake changing target to the attacker.

The scroll is found among the dirty rags in [[Ueth]] the Scalehunter's chamber — [[Ueth]]'s gospel on one side, this Power on the other. It does not appear on the Bare Bones d10 table; it is unique to the Sepulchre and to the [[Strange Serpent Drug Cult]] whose visions birthed it. The constrictors are the colours nature forgot: jale, dolm, and ulfire, the same hues that bloom in the eye of someone bitten by an [[Emerald Serpent]].

**Connections:** [[Powers]], [[Ueth]], [[Strange Serpent Drug Cult]], [[Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch]], [[Emerald Venom]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the constrictors are summoned from nowhere or pulled out of the wielder's blood (Ueth's permanent venom-high) is left open. The Power's transcription as part of Ueth's gospel suggests a doctrinal use — sacrifice by snake — that the scenario never quite shows on the page.
