# Nine Violet Signs Unknot the Storm
**Type:** Power / unclean scroll
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.34 (entry 6); MÖRK BORG Rotblack Sludge, p.14 (Fletcher); Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch, p.12 (Yaoxl)

**Effect.** Produces d2 bolts of lightning, dealing d6 damage each.

Nine violet sigils, traced in the air or chanted in some forgotten tongue, untie the knot that holds the sky together. What falls out is electricity old enough to remember the first cloud. Wielded by [[Fletcher]] in the [[Accursed Den]] (rolled 1–2 on his d4 Power cycle) and by [[Srolki & Yaoxl|Yaoxl]] in the Downward Spiral of the [[Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch]] (up to d3 times per day).

**Connections:** [[Powers]], [[Fletcher]], [[Srolki & Yaoxl]], [[Rotblack Sludge]], [[Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** What the nine signs *are* — and whether the wielder must draw all nine or merely speak their names — is unwritten. The Power lives in two distant scenarios with no other connection; whether the scrolls came from a common source is open.
