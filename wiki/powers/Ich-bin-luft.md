# Ich-bin-luft
**Type:** Power / unclean scroll (unique)
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Rotblack Sludge, p.14

**Effect.** The wielder is invisible for the next two rounds. Can still attack.

"I am air." Unique to [[Fletcher]] in the [[Accursed Den]]. It does not appear on the Bare Bones d10 Unclean Scrolls table — it is one of the scenario-specific Powers that turn boss fights from violence into pursuit. When Fletcher vanishes the room remembers him only by the rain of human flesh that drops from the rafters whenever he is hit.

**Connections:** [[Powers]], [[Fletcher]], [[Accursed Den]], [[Rotblack Sludge]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** No second wielder is named. Whether the scroll itself survives Fletcher's death — and whether it transfers cleanly or carries his cannibal hunger — is unwritten. A worthy expansion seed for any sequel scenario.
