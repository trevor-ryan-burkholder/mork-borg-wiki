# Powers

The named scrolls and tablets that bend the dying world a little further out of shape. A scroll is read by force of will (Presence DR12). Failure costs blood and the rest of the hour. Success spends one of the day's allotted uses (Presence + d4 each morning) and unleashes the Power written on the parchment.

Scrolls divide into **Unclean** (necromantic, mind-warping, hostile) and **Sacred** (curative, revelatory, commanding). Both fail outright if the wielder carries a zweihänder or wears medium/heavy armor. On a fumble the [[Arcane Catastrophes]] table promises bargains no living thing should sign.

The canonical d10 tables for both kinds — and the catastrophe table — live in **[[Powers]]** under wiki/items/. This directory collects the **named** Powers that appear in canon as scroll names, NPC abilities, and bound demon-Powers, so the dead-link web is closed.

## Gaps

Most of the d20 Arcane Catastrophes table is lost to typographic destruction in the plaintext source — only Cube-Violet survives clean. Several scroll names recur in cult content (e.g. **Deathlike Silence** at the Death Ziggurat, **Beechwood Tube Protean Power** at the Accursed Den) but appear once and never again; these are tracked in [[Powers]] rather than spun out here.
