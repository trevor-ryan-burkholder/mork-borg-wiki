# Fleshy Automaton
*ceramic bile-filled golems serving the Fattened King*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC Bloat

Ceramic bile-filled golems. Smells of rot, meat, and decay. If knocked over, spills their contents and deactivates.

HP 8, Morale −, Ceramic −d4, Violent shove d6.

Animated by the two original automatons (servants of [[Silas the Fattened King]]) pouring bodily fluids into the empty ceramic-humanoid heads stored in vats of rendered fat in the labyrinth's Automaton Storage. Anyone who enters that room who is not an automaton is taken to the Larder for processing.

**Connections:** [[Silas the Fattened King]], [[Bloat]], [[Gourmand's Cutlery]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the original two automatons are distinct individuals from the new wave (different ranks? different powers?) is unspecified.
