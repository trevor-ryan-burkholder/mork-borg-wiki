# Distraught Spirit
*the witness who begs you to stop it*

**Type:** creature / spirit / scenario encounter
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Rotblack Sludge, p.III (random encounter table B)

HP — Morale — No armor, No attack

A once-per-scenario weird encounter inside the [[Accursed Den]]. Agitated by the depravities and cannibalism plaguing the Den, the spirit pleads with the PCs to end the horror. Can tell them about the [[Gutworm]].

**Special:** No stat block is printed. The spirit does not fight, only pleads — it remembers something that was done to it here, but not its own name. Manifests in the Pump room, the Chain room, or the Debris room (the three rooms with random-encounter rolls).

**Lore:** Whatever the spirit was — a prisoner of the Den, a Courtier of the [[Shadow King]] gone looking for [[Aldon]], a cannibal's leftover — it carries enough memory to point at the worm in the sludge and hate it more than it hates being dead. Once the PCs hear it out, it dissolves.

**Connections:** [[Accursed Den]], [[Rotblack Sludge]], [[Gutworm]], [[Fletcher]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** No HP, no Morale, no Special mechanics, no name — a thin-source entity rendered as a scenario hook rather than a creature you fight. The spirit's identity is the obvious expansion seed; tying it to a named NPC (perhaps the Seer who hired the PCs) would deepen the scenario considerably.
