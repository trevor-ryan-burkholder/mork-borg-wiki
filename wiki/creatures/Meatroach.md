# Meatroach
*a delicacy if cooked right*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.6 (Graven-Tosk)

HP 4, Morale −, Exoskeleton −d4, Bite d4

**Special:** Has a poisonous **murder-appendix** that must be removed before cooking (DR16 Agility test). Failure spoils the meat — eating it requires a DR14 Toughness or d8 damage.

**Lore:** A delicacy, if cooked right. If not, you die. The roaches of Graven-Tosk grow on fresh corpses and old myth, palm-sized and oily, and the trick of removing the appendix is taught only by the kind of teacher who has lost an apprentice. Then another.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Roach Herder]] (who breeds them), [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The Roach Herder's cockroach husbandry implies a wider Meatroach population somewhere in Graven-Tosk. Whether one big enough to fly a man, as he hopes, has ever actually emerged is left open by the booklet.
