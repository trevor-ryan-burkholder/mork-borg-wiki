# Ragpie
*a scrunched-up bundle of cloth — until it unfolds*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.13 (Bergen Chrypt)

HP 8, Morale −, Thick skin −d2, Smother-cloak special

**Special:** If defence fails you are embraced by the Ragpie, slowly choking and unable to act. Only the death of the Ragpie or a DR18 Strength test will set you free. Until then, you take **d4 damage per round**, and **half of any damage dealt to the Ragpie is also dealt to you.**

**Lore:** While resting the Ragpie appears as a scrunched up bundle of cloth, with perhaps a sprinkling of old bones in close proximity. When bringing down its prey, it looks more like a dark cloak, enveloping the target in a deathly choke-embrace. Climbers in Bergen Chrypt sometimes mistake one for an abandoned cloak. The mistake is the meal.

**Connections:** [[Bergen Chrypt]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether a Ragpie is woven from real cloth or merely resembles it (skin? mountain-moss? burial wrappings?) is open. The "sprinkling of old bones" suggests it does not eat its kills cleanly.
