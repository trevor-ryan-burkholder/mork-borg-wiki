# Schleswig Bog-feeder
*a particularly omnivorous breed of pig — and someone's*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.9 (Wästland)

HP 5, Morale 8, Bite d4

Encountered in herds of d6, always domesticated, always belonging to someone.

**Special:** The pigs need to be killed in one round, before they have a chance to squeal. Otherwise, fight **d4 farmers** (HP 4, Morale 6, 50% chance they carry a tool or knife d4) come running.

**Lore:** A particularly omnivorous breed of pig. The Schleswig Bog-feeder is completely domesticated — which means the ones you've tracked down belong to someone. Better do this quickly, before anyone notices.

**Connections:** [[Wästland]], [[Schleswig]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Where the bog the Bog-feeder feeds in is, and what it eats out of it, is a Schleswig farmer-secret. The breed implies a swine-husbandry guild somewhere in Schleswig, unnamed in source.
