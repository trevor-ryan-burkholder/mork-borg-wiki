# Grubstoppers
*any bug large enough to calm a man's hunger*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.11 (Valley of the Unfortunate Undead)

HP 1, Morale −, Exoskeleton −d2 (50%), Bite 1 damage + special

Encountered in swarms of d10.

**Special:** Some bugs have a bad bite (Toughness DR12). Roll below for the effect (d4):
1–2. Nothing.
3. d6 damage.
4. Infected.

**Lore:** A common word for any bug large enough to calm a man's hunger. In the Valley they grow on the corpse-soil, fed by what the mud pits offer, and they are nutritious in the sense that they fill you. Whether they nourish or merely fill is a Valley distinction without much practical difference.

**Connections:** [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** What "infected" means in Valley terms — the slow-growing undeath the gazetteer describes? — is implied but unstated. A Grubstopper is, mechanically, the cheapest danger in the booklet.
