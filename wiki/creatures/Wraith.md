# Wraith (Wrat)
*soundless phantom, draining the living*

**Type:** creature / undead
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.59

HP 15, Morale −, No armor, Touch d4 + special
**Special:** Swift, elusive and difficult to hit (dr14).

These soundless phantoms always win initiative. Their touch drains Strength, Presence and Agility by 1 for the duration of the fight.

**Loot:** Captured 120s — Skull 70s — Ectoplasm 25s

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], [[Sarcopha-Ghost]] (a related apparition)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** No origin story, no named individuals, no relation to specific dead. Just a price list for parts.
