# Blood-drenched Skeleton (Belze)
*the bones that mimic voices*

**Type:** creature / undead
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.60

HP 7, Morale 8, No armor, Shortsword d4 or Knife d4, Bony knuckles d2
**Special:** Skulks about, impossibly soundless. Attacks by surprise.

Can mimic voices to lure victims, but can only repeat what it has heard. Attacks on them with piercing weapons are dr14. Any strike doing 5 or more damage destroys the skeleton completely.

**Loot:** Captured 35s — Destroyed 7s

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Bergen Chrypt]] (catacombs)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Why blood-drenched is never explained. Whether they retain personalities or just echoes of speech is left ambiguous — a creature that can only repeat what it's heard suggests something more haunting than blank undead.
