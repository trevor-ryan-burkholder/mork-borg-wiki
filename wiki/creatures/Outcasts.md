# Outcasts (Followers)
*the vagrants who travel with you until they don't*

**Type:** creature category / follower
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.63–67

Money might cross hands but these weirdos don't cost silver to hire. Vagrants and refugees driven by loneliness, they just want somewhere to belong. Which is exactly why they often break into a run and disappear, usually at the most critical moments.

The GM makes a Morale check from time to time (succeed and the outcast stays) and adds the group's highest Presence to the roll. The GM should also consider whether or not the group provides the outcast with the things he/she/it values.

Four canonical types appear in Bare Bones:

## 1. Earthbound
*three parts human, one part dog. Society sees them as foul and disloyal.*
HP 8, Morale 7, Leather −d2, Staff/Femur d4.
**Traits (d4):** Arrogant / Quiet / Joking / Hypochondriac.
**Specialty (d4):** Excellent cooking (d4 extra hp when resting) / Finds the right path / Senses danger / Makes/repairs items.
**Values (d6):** Fireplace discussions / Praise / Endless tasks / Items as payment / Carnal relations / Diluted black poison.

## 2. Wild Wickhead
*the wildest cast out from even the loose Wickhead community.*
HP 10, Morale 7, No armor, Knife d4.
**Traits (d4):** Grumpy / Apathetic / Careless / Moody.
**Specialty (d4):** Walking lightsource (1–2) / Expert with a knife d4+2 / Backstab (test DR8, weapon damage +3). Aside from their specialty they carry up to five items.
**Values (d6):** Holding long monologues / Verbal conflicts within the group / Getting paid in sharp weapons / Burnt meat / Depressing stories / Captured slaves.

## 3. Pale One
*as if fallen from the stars. Destructive and detached.*
HP 5, Morale 8, No armor, unarmed d2.
**Traits (d4):** Bitter / Incoherent / Mute / Self-harming behaviour.
**Specialty (d4):** Create d2 doses of a random decoction / Create d2 doses [[Elixir Vitalis]] (heals d6 hp, stops infection) / [unwritten] / [unwritten].
**Values (d6):** [some unwritten] / Cleanliness / [unwritten] / [unwritten] / Wine / [unwritten].
(See also the [[Pale One]] class.)

## 4. Prowler
*lawless good-for-nothing crooks banished from civilization.*
HP 8, Morale 8, Leather −d2, Knife/Femur d4 (occasionally filthy shortsword d4+1).
**Traits (d4):** Lazy / Bragging / Liar / Traitor.
**Specialty (d4):** [unwritten].
**Values (d6):** [unwritten] / Food / Gossip / Liquor / Pointless death / [unwritten].
