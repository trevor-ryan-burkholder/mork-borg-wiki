# Dredgehog
*daggers on its back, trust in nothing*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.5 (Sarkash)

HP 14, Morale 10, Daggerback −d6, Bite/Daggers d6

**Special:** When provoked, it rolls up into a big ball of murder. Missing an attack against the beast means you strike a dagger and take d4 damage.

**Lore:** Its spines are not quills. They are knives. Long, thin, oiled-dark in the Sarkash damp. The Dredgehog rolls itself into a wheel of edges and waits for you to swing wrong. The smart trapper waits longer.

**Connections:** [[Sarkash]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the daggers regrow when broken, whether a dredgehog quill is useable as a weapon, and what feeds on a daggerball are all unwritten.
