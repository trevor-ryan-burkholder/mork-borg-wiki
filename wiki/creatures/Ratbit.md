# Ratbit
*in a hole there lived*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.4 (Tveland)

HP 6, Morale −, Claws d8

**Special:** Digs. The hole is human-sized. The claws that dug it are ten inches long.

**Lore:** In a hole in the ground there lives a Ratbit. The hole is large enough for a man, because a man is what it eats. Not a rat. Not a rabbit. Something between, and bigger than either, and waiting at the bottom.

**Connections:** [[Tveland]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Population, breeding, whether ratbits cooperate or hold solitary burrows — open. The ten-inch claws imply ten-inch reach from inside a tunnel mouth.
