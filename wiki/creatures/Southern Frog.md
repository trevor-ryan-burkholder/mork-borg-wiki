# Southern Frog
*the stewing thing of the lost coasts*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.57

The Southern Frog is the chief ingredient of *Southern Frog stew* — a decoction brewed by the [[Occult Herbmaster]] that purges the gut. Vomit for d4 hours. Pass a dr14 test or you can do nothing else.

It comes from the south, where the south still is — [[Südglans]] before it sank, the [[Drowned]]'s coastlands now, the marshes no map admits. The skin is thick and the back is freckled and the eyes are sad.

**Connections:** [[Occult Herbmaster]], [[Spider-Owl]] (parallel decoction-ingredient), [[Südglans]], [[Drowned]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** No stat block. Whether the Frog itself causes the vomiting in life (defensive secretion) or only in stew is unwritten. A whole *family* of southern frogs — different colors for different brews — would be an easy table-expansion.
