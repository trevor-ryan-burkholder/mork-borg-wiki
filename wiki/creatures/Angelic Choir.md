# Angelic Choir
*the singing flowers that hide the path*

**Type:** creature group / hazard / collective entity
**Source:** Devil's Tomb, p.1 (Angelic Choir corridor)

HP — Morale — No armor, No direct attack

**Special:** **Wordless Song.** The corridor walls bloom with angel-faced flowers, all of them looking through and into anyone who passes. They sing a wordless song that triggers visions of death, vertigo, monochrome outlining of all things, or invisibility-of-the-living (roll d4 from the corridor's table). The flowers themselves invite hidden things to be revealed — the [[Icon of St Largoth]]'s Eyes, brought through this passage, will see what would otherwise be hidden.

**Lore:** The angelic children of the [[Plant of Life]], worshipped by the [[Drowned]] in their poisoned exile. The faces in the flowers are not human — too many planes, too many eyes — and the song is not music. To walk the corridor is to be sung at by something that thinks of you as a passing weather pattern. The [[Madman]] crawled here once and never returned to himself.

**Connections:** [[Devil's Tomb]], [[Plant of Life]], [[Drowned]], [[Madman]], [[Icon of St Largoth]], [[Graven-Tosk]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the flowers can be plucked, kept, or carried out — and whether one in your pack continues to sing — is the obvious table-question. A PC who learns the song would be the start of something heretical.
