# Rot Priest
*Akünh's clergy of two realms*

**Type:** creature / cultist
**Source:** The Death Ziggurat

Wear black robes and wide-brimmed cylindrical hats with black veils covering the face. A rotting mouth similar to [[Akünh]]'s on the front of the upper body; they must eat human flesh regularly. Wear **medallions** that allow for traveling to the [[Death Ziggurat]] in the [[Death Realm]] — the medallion is the key.

Curious about existential and metaphysical issues. Their touch causes death and ruin.

HP 10, Morale 8, No armor
- **Devour.** Test Strength DR12 or be grappled and next turn devoured (d8 damage per round).
- **Claws d6.**
- **Rotting touch.** Those damaged by a rot priest will not heal when resting until healed magically, or resting one night at least a day's travel from the ziggurat.

**Connections:** [[Akünh]], [[Death Ziggurat]], [[Death Realm]], [[Horn Beast]] (which they summon and command via meditation), [[Necropolis]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Their numbers, recruitment, hierarchy, and what happens to a Rot Priest's body when it dies are unwritten. Whether the medallions could be reverse-engineered or repurposed is open.
