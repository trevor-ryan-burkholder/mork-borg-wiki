# Grim-toothed Squirrel
*teeth made for cracking bones and savoring the marrow within*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.6 (Graven-Tosk)

HP 2, Morale 6, Bite d6

**Special:** Teeth designed for bone. It can crack a femur. It can crack a finger more easily.

**Lore:** Where a normal squirrel goes for nuts, this one goes for the knuckles. It haunts the older mausolea of Graven-Tosk and works the cracked-open coffins. The marrow is its preference. The living are an upset of routine.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the Grim-toothed Squirrel is a Graven-Tosk mutation or a normal forest squirrel that wandered in and adapted is unwritten. Marrow-fed squirrel meat is the alchemist's puzzle.
