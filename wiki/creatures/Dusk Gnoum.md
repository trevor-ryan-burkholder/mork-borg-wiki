# Dusk Gnoum
*small wall-crawler that climbs from holes*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** Rotblack Sludge (random encounter table a)

They crawl from holes and climb the walls. Small, quick but frail.

HP 4, Morale 7, No armor, Knife d4. DR14 to hit them.

**Connections:** [[Accursed Den]], [[Rotblack Sludge]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether Dusk Gnoums are found elsewhere in the world or only inside the Accursed Den is open. Their language, hierarchy, and reason for being are unwritten.
