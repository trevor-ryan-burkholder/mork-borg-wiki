# Forked-Tongue Devotee
*the most fanatic of Ueth's followers*

**Type:** creature / cultist
**Source:** Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch

HP 9, Morale 9, Gatorskin hood and vestment −d4
- **Forked dagger d4 + poison**, or
- **Viper spit:** Agility DR12 or blinded.
- **Poison:** Toughness DR10 or become infected and drowsy, raising the difficulty of all attack and defence tests by +2 for d4 hours.
- **Viper spit:** Agility DR12 to avoid being blinded d3 rounds.

These are the most fanatic of [[Ueth]]'s followers and will die for him if asked. They will not allow any suspicious behaviour in the Worship Hall. They speak with a burning passion and wicked lisp.

**Connections:** [[Ueth]], [[Strange Serpent Drug Cult]], [[Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the forked tongue is a result of [[Emerald Venom]] saturation, ritual splitting, or both is unwritten. Their command structure under Ueth is implied (they're his elite) but unscripted.
