# Alchemical Ooze
*the amber goo that eats weapons*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** The Goblin Grinder

Mindless, hungry. A large puddle of amber-orange goo foaming beneath the laboratory tables in the [[Derelict Mill]]. Created (or escaped) from Nagel's alchemical work.

HP 10, Morale −, Gooey −d2, Acidic splash d8
**Slow.** Defense is DR10.

Whenever a PC damages the ooze they must test Strength DR8 or their weapon becomes stuck to it. If not removed the next round (Strength DR10) the weapon is destroyed.

**Connections:** [[Derelict Mill]], [[Nagel Krat]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the ooze was a deliberate creation, a failed Goblin Grinder experiment, or a spontaneous emergence from boiling reagents is open. A natural seed for other oozes elsewhere in the dying world.
