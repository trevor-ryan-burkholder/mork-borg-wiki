# Antideer
*tastes like venison, but isn't*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.4 (Tveland)

HP 7, Morale 6, Tough skin −d2, Antlers d6

**Special:** After eating Antideer meat, pass a DR12 Presence test to live with yourself. If not, the unknowledge of what you've done weighs on you, lowering Presence by 1.

**Lore:** It looks enough like a deer to fool a hungry hunter and the hunter's tongue. Whatever it actually is, the meat sits in the gut like a confession. The herd grazes the cold steppes of Tveland and watches you with eyes that are almost — but not quite — the eyes of a deer.

**Connections:** [[Tveland]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** What an Antideer actually is, beneath the venison-flavor, is unwritten. Whether they breed with true deer, whether the herd remembers, whether the meat-lowered Presence ever recovers — all open.
