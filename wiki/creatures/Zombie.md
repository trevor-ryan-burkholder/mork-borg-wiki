# Zombie (Nodh)
*the slow plague carriers*

**Type:** creature / undead
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.61

HP 7, Morale −, Leather scraps −d2, Claw/bite d2 + special
**Special:** Anyone bitten tests Toughness dr8 or dies within two days before rising as a zombie. The only cure or vaccine is said to be found at the peak of a pale mountain within an infinitely-miserable forest of dark leaves.

King [[Fathmu IX]] of [[Western Kingdom]] in particular seeks this cure and knows the name and location of the forest which the mountain overlooks.

**Loot:** Dead 30s — Captured 5s — Blood per litre

Compare the **Undead** of the [[Death Ziggurat]] — drooling, red-eyed, rising again after one round — and the [[Drowned Altar]]-mourning Drowned. The world holds many varieties of walking dead, all worsening as the [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] turns.

**Connections:** [[Fathmu IX]], [[Western Kingdom]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] (3:1 at [[Graven-Tosk]]), [[Bergen Chrypt]] (likely the pale mountain), [[Sarkash]] (likely the forest of dark leaves)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The pale mountain in the infinitely-miserable forest is named only as the cure's location. Fathmu knows the name and place — the GM does not. This is the single most explicit "go on a quest" hook in Bare Bones; it has no canonical resolution.
