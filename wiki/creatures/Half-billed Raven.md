# Half-billed Raven
*only the upper beak — a reaching, sticky tongue beneath*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.6 (Graven-Tosk); MBC_Graves_left_wanting.pdf, p.7

HP 2, Morale −, Beak/Choking tongue d4/special

Encountered in murders of d4.

**Special:** DR12 Agility to avoid or d4 damage per round until DR14 Strength succeeds or the raven dies. The tongue is the worse half.

**Lore:** The lower jaw is gone. In its place is a long, gluey tongue that reaches and clutches. The Half-billed Ravens of Graven-Tosk perch on the mausolea and watch the throats of mourners. The tongues taste of grave water.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the missing lower beak is a hatching defect or a curse particular to Graven-Tosk birds is open. Why the tongue chokes specifically — and what happens to a victim ejected too late — is GM-call.
