# Skelelk
*elk-bodied, bones worn on the outside*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.5 (Sarkash)

HP 9, Morale 8, Exoskeleton −d6, Antlers d6

Encountered in groups of d4.

**Special:** The skeleton is worn outside the meat. Blunt weapons always deal maximum damage; the cage of bone cracks but does not give.

**Lore:** An elk grown wrong, its ribs and skull on the outside, its softer parts hidden within the cage. The herd moves through Sarkash in silence — bone does not creak the way leather does. Hunters who bring axes do better than hunters who bring blades.

**Connections:** [[Sarkash]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the inverted skeleton is curse, mutation, or natural Sarkash adaptation is open. What lives inside an exoskeleton-elk's hollow rib-cage is GM's choice for any cracked specimen.
