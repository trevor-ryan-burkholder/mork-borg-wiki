# Cursed Trout
*a hollow fish — and a terrible omen*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.10 (Lake Onda)

(No combat stats — caught by line, not fought.)

**Special:** Live among the regular trout of the lake. It is not apparent that you've caught one until you move to gut it and find that *there are no guts*. A hollow fish. Whether by magic or superstition, the one who guts such a fish **cannot sleep for d6 days.**

**Lore:** Seen as a terrible omen by the fishermen of Lake Onda. The trout that come up empty are blamed for the lake's failing trade, for the river-trade gone, for Wästland's slow collapse. Whether the curse causes the failures or merely announces them, no one alive remembers.

**Connections:** [[Lake Onda]], [[Western Kingdom]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether a Cursed Trout's hollow body holds anything — silt, prayers, nothing — is open. Whether eating one would extend or end the sleepless curse is a question for the brave or the very hungry.
