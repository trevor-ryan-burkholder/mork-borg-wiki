# Scum (Bent)
*backstabbing vermin of the slums*

**Type:** creature / humanoid
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.58

HP 7, Morale 8, No armor, Poisoned knife d4 + special
**Special:** Poisoned knife. Test Toughness dr10 or become infected.

There are few fiends more fell than poverty or monsters more powerful than hate. In this stricken world, the slums and black alleyways are fecund scum, mothering countless abhorred miscreants. The PC with the highest Presence makes a dr14 test at the start of battle. Failure means a random party member is automatically hit with a cowardly backstab — normal damage +3.

**Loot:** Captured 50–120s (wanted, serious crime) — Dead 20–70s

Compare the [[Gutterborn Scum]] class — they grew up like Bent did and may sympathize, recruit, or simply gut the rest of them faster.

**Connections:** [[Galgenbeck]], [[Schleswig]], [[Gutterborn Scum]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Source describes them as generic urban predators. Specific scum gangs, leaders, territory disputes between them, and their (likely awful) culture are wide open.
