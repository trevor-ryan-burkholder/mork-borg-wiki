# Grotesque (Thinx)
*the clay things that watch from gargoyle stone*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.62

HP 18, Morale −, Clay/stone −d6, Claws d6 or Eye-beam d8

Lurking around churches, stalking graveyards, creeping closer when you look away. Disturbingly still, difficult to discern against gray stone and hard to recognize even when seen. They move slowly and are easy to hit (dr10).

Their terrifying gaze is used on 1–2 on a d6 each round. **Always hits.**

**Loot:** Captured 190s — Dead (intact) 100s — Dead (in pieces) 10s

**Connections:** [[Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Graven-Tosk]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether grotesques were made (by whom?) or are a natural rock-folk is open. Their gaze's exact effect is "always hits" — what it does is GM-call beyond raw d8. They're tantalizingly close to the gargoyles of cathedral decoration; whether the stone bodies on church walls are dormant Thinx is an obvious horror-hook.
