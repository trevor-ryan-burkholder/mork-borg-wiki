# Nephalix Monkeys
*the abyss grew tired of them and spat them back out*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.12 (Bergen Chrypt)

HP 7, Morale −, Scales −d2, Bite/Claws d6 + special

Encountered in troops of d4.

**Special:** Test Strength DR8 or be lifted up and flung through the air, landing 10 ft away or somewhere far below.

**Lore:** The Nephalix Monkeys lived in the abyss for so long, the abyss grew tired of them and spat them back out. Now they leap from peak to peak with their bony wings, tossing unfortunates down the cliffs to spite the rocks below, laughing all the while. The laughter carries far in the Bergen Chrypt cold.
