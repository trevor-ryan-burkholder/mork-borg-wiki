# Carcasswan
*mates for life — and eats the lost half*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.10 (Lake Onda)

Lone: HP 15, Morale −, Bite/Claws d8
Pair: HP 5 each, Morale −, Bite/Claws d6

Encountered as a lone bird or a pair (d2).

**Special:** Mates for life. When one of the pair dies, the survivor eats its dead lover and gains a taste for flesh. The lone Carcasswan is the more dangerous form.

**Lore:** A decade-long story eventually ending in the surviving half eating its dead lover. Long story short, when you see a lone Carcasswan, you run. Before it sees you. The pair-stat is the gentler bird; the lone-stat is the bereaved.

**Connections:** [[Lake Onda]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the flesh-tasting lone Carcasswan ever re-pairs is unwritten. The implication is that the population is collapsing as each pair shrinks to one.
