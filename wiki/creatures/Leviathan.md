# Leviathan
**Type:** creature
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.19 (Psalm IV, 4:5 and 4:6)

Named only in the [[Misery Table]]:

**4:5** *"Behold now the [[Endless Sea]], where Leviathan causes waves to be as mountains."*
**4:6** *"And Leviathan shall come among you. Children winter-born and fated to fall before snow, both shall it take."*

When the fourth Psalm's fifth misery wakes, the waves of the Endless Sea become mountains. When the sixth misery wakes, Leviathan walks among the living.

**Connections:** [[Endless Sea]], [[Misery Table]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Form, size, behavior beyond the Psalm's predicted appetite — all unwritten. Whether Leviathan is one entity or many is open. The cultural memory of Leviathan among sailors of [[Grift]] would be a natural expansion.
