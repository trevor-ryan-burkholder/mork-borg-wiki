# Flail-horned Muskox
*horns on keratin chains*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.8 (Kergüs)

HP 18, Morale 9, Thick fur −d2, Flail-horns special

Moves in herds of d12, dragging horns on keratin chains.

**Special:** Attacks in a flurry of whirling chains and horns. Make four attacks as follows (target is chosen randomly for each attack):
- DR16 to avoid d4 damage
- DR14 to avoid d6 damage
- DR12 to avoid d8 damage
- DR10 to avoid d10 damage

**Lore:** Your best bet is going for one of the oldest and slowest, in the back of the chain gang. That means you won't have to fight the entire herd, but it also means you'll have to fight the one with the biggest horns. The keratin chains rattle in the cold of Kergüs like a procession of forgotten bells.

**Connections:** [[Kergüs]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the chains grow naturally from the horns or are looped by herd-elders is uncanonized. The bull at the back of the chain gang is the herd's whole memory.
