# Three-thirds-pheasant
*completely and utterly a pheasant. Just not necessarily the same pheasant.*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.9 (Wästland)

HP 3, Morale −, Beak d4

**Special:** Always has a third of something. A third head. A third knee. A third heart. The other two-thirds are a different bird's.

**Lore:** It is completely and utterly a pheasant. It is just not necessarily the same pheasant. The Wästland farmers eat them like any other gamebird. Sometimes the meat tastes of one bird, sometimes of three. The countryside has stopped asking why.

**Connections:** [[Wästland]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether two-thirds of a Three-thirds-pheasant matches a missing-thirds elsewhere — a kind of bird-jigsaw — is the obvious horror tease. Source does not say.
