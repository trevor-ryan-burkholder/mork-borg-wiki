# Liar-bird
*repeats every lie it hears, and the occasional truth — for fun*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.9 (Wästland)

HP 2, Morale −, Beak d4

**Special:** When the Liar-bird hears a lie uttered, it latches onto every word and repeats them when it pleases. Sometimes it repeats a truth instead. For fun.

**Lore:** Sought-after by inquisitors and others tasked with uprooting falsehood. The bird is a small grey thing with a knowing tilt of the head. It has been used to break heretics. It has been used to break marriages. It is impartial only in the sense that it does not care which.

**Connections:** [[Wästland]], [[Inquisition]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the bird's "for fun" truths are random or pointed is the unanswered question of every Wästland courtroom that has used one. Captured Liar-birds command silver from the Inquisition.
