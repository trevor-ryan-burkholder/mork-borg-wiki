# Twice-grown Corpse Fly
*hatched in the soft skull, hungry for the next*

**Type:** creature / vermin
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.11; MBC Eat-Prey-Kill p.6 (Graven-Tosk regional bestiary)

HP 4, Morale −, Exoskeleton −d4, Bite d4 + special
**Special:** Test Toughness DR12 or become host for a dozen freshly laid fly eggs. Extract them within d6 days or watch them hatch in your corpse.

**Lore:** Bursts out of urns and skulls in the [[Catacomb Corridor]] looking for food. Twice grown because the first cycle was a fly that hatched in a corpse; the second is *you*. The fog of [[Graven-Tosk]] is full of their droning when the ground sweats. Eat-Prey-Kill notes them as native fauna of the cemetery alongside the Meatroach and the [[Unbred Mutt|Unbred Mutts]].

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]], [[The Catacomb Corridor]], [[Meatroach]], [[The Roach Herder]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the eggs can be extracted by another's hand or only by the host is unspecified; "extract them within d6 days" implies surgical, not magical, removal. What "twice grown" means precisely — two life cycles, two abdomens, two sets of wings — is left to the GM's stomach.
