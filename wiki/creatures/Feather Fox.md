# Feather Fox
*silent like a shadow, no trail and no tracks*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.9 (Wästland)

HP 3, Morale −, Bite d6

**Special:** Notorious for its ability to move silent like a shadow, while leaving no trail or tracks.

**Lore:** Its hollow bones are perfect for making bone pipes. Break one of them, drink the magical mercury inside, and you'll be as light as a feather for an hour. Your head will be, too.

**Connections:** [[Wästland]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the mercury can be drained cleanly or only by breaking the bone-pipe is unclarified. What "your head will be" — light-headed in the giddy sense, or buoyant in the falling-off-your-neck sense — is left to the drinker.
