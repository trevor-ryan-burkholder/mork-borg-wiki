# Sagsobuth
*shapeless interdimensional trader, face a vortex of light*

**Type:** creature / merchant
**Source:** Rotblack Sludge (random encounter table b)

An interdimensional trader who manifests as a random encounter inside the [[Accursed Den]] — shapeless and ethereal, her face a vortex of light. She sells **violet poison** (30s — DR14, 2d10 damage, d4+1 doses) and offers a **Beechwood Tube** (30s) containing a foul protean scroll: each dawn this scroll has a new unclean Power. The Power can be used once. It is misinterpreted on 1–3 on a d20 instead of a 1.

Cannot be harmed but will not attack unless provoked. **Provoked Explosion:** 2d10 damage distributed among the group.

**Connections:** [[Accursed Den]], [[Rotblack Sludge]], [[Mikhael the Merchant]] (parallel trader-archetype), [[Powers]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether Sagsobuth appears at other dungeons or only in the Accursed Den is open — "interdimensional" strongly suggests she could surface anywhere. The dimension she comes from, her motives, and her relationship (if any) to [[The Dark Spider]] or [[Heedless Creation]] are blank.
