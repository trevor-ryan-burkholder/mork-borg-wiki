# Madman
*the spore-eaten graverobber at the door*

**Type:** creature / humanoid
**Source:** Devil's Tomb, p.1

HP 2, Morale —, No armor, Bite d2

**Special:** Naked, unarmed, and scared to death of the [[Angelic Choir]]. Cannot remember his name. Tries to flee any room being entered; will only bite when first bitten or cornered.

**Lore:** A former graverobber, mad with spore-fever. Crawls at the entrance of [[Devil's Tomb]], the only living thing the [[Drowned]] haven't bothered to kill — perhaps because the spores already did the work. He has seen the [[Angelic Choir]] singing in the corridor of angel-faced flowers and has no language left to describe it. He has stopped looking for the way out.

**Connections:** [[Devil's Tomb]], [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Angelic Choir]], [[Drowned]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** His name, what he was looking for in the tomb, and whether the spores can be cleared (or whether he can be saved) is unwritten. A merciful party with magickal healing would learn quickly.
