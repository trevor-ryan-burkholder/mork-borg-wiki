# Throat-cutting Warbler
*the tiny one with the best aim*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.5 (Sarkash)

HP 1, Morale −, Beak d10

**Special:** Tiny. Attacks and defence against the warbler are DR14. The sharpest beak and the best aim of all birds in Sarkash.

**Lore:** A bright little bird that sings sweet enough to charm. Then it dives. The carotid is its favored target and it does not miss. Sarkash mothers tell their children not to walk through bird-loud meadows alone, but Sarkash mothers tell their children many things.

**Connections:** [[Sarkash]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether warblers act in concert or solo is unwritten. A single d10 to the throat from a creature with 1 HP is a piece of bestiary engineering that rewards careful play.
