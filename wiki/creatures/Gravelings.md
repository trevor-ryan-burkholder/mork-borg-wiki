# Gravelings
*a bit like skinless, hunch-backed dogs with opposable thumbs*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.11 (Valley of the Unfortunate Undead)

HP 6, Morale 9, Bite/Claws d6

Encountered in packs of d6.

**Special:** Opposable thumbs. They open doors. They draw knives from the belts of the dying. They are not stupid.

**Lore:** They taste about the same as skinless, hunch-backed dogs with opposable thumbs would taste. Worse, even. The pack works in pairs, one to grip, one to bite. The thumbs are wrong on a thing that runs on four feet. The thumbs are what people remember.

**Connections:** [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether Gravelings can wield more than a knife — a club, a torch, a key — is the encounter the source declines to provide and the GM is invited to.
