# Bonemare
*no flesh — a very tasty brain somewhere within*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.11 (Valley of the Unfortunate Undead)

HP 12, Morale −, Bones −d4, Bite/Kick d6

**Special:** On its first turn, the mare unleashes the power of a random unclean scroll.

**Lore:** While it has no flesh to speak of, the Bonemare has a very tasty brain somewhere in its necromanced equestrian being. The skeleton-horse paces the Valley at night, the bones loose and rattling, and a single dark thought rides between the empty ribs. The first thought is always the loudest.

**Connections:** [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], [[Unclean Scrolls]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the Bonemare was once a specific knight's mount and remembers him, and whether the brain — once retrieved — can be eaten or read, is open. The random scroll on turn one is canonically unconstrained: any unclean scroll the GM rolls.
