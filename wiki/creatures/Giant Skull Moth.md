# Giant Skull Moth
*a human skull on tar-black wings*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.6 (Graven-Tosk)

HP 3, Morale −, Stinger d4 + special

**Special:** DR14 Toughness or d4 damage and become infected.

**Lore:** It bears the shape and size of a human skull on its tar-black wings. Perfect bait for squirrels and tomb raiders alike. It drifts through the mausoleum-rows of Graven-Tosk like a face that forgot its body, the wings dusting silence onto everything they touch.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the skull-mark is mimicry or the moth's wings are pressed from real bone is the kind of question Graven-Tosk does not answer. What it infects you with is GM-call.
