# Feral Horses
*more than wild — they know the taste of flesh*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.4 (Tveland)

HP 8, Morale −, Tough skin −d2, Bite/Kick d6

Encountered in herds of d4.

**Special:** Carnivorous. They love the taste of flesh. A bite is as much to be feared as a kick.

**Lore:** Once mounts. Once trained. Then turned loose, and the grass of Tveland was not enough. Now they run in packs of four, hunting whatever the steppe wolves leave standing. Their teeth grew back wrong.

**Connections:** [[Tveland]], [[Steppe Wolfe]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whose horses they were is gone. Whether a tamed feral horse could become a war-mount with the same appetite is left to the unwise.
