# Rusty Bass
*tastes like rusted metal — because it eats metal*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.10 (Lake Onda)

HP 2, Morale −

**Special:** When eating Rusty Bass, test DR12 Toughness or get **deathetanus**. This means you're infected and have a craving for feeling metal inside you (DR +2 on defence against attacks using metal weapons).

**Lore:** The meat tastes like rusted metal because the fish eats it. The lake-floor of Onda is supposed to be sand and silt. The Rusty Bass tells you otherwise — there is iron down there, and someone left it. The fish are not curious. They are merely opportunistic, the way fish are.

**Connections:** [[Lake Onda]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** "Deathetanus" — canon's portmanteau of death and tetanus — affects defence against metal weapons, not other rolls; the precise psychological flavor of "craving metal inside you" is intentionally suggestive. What metal lies on the bed of Lake Onda is the river-trade hook the lake is hiding.
