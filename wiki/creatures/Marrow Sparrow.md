# Marrow Sparrow
*a syringe-like beak — feeds on sleeping victims*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.11 (Valley of the Unfortunate Undead)

HP 1, Morale −, Beak 1 damage

**Special:** Tiny (DR14 to hit). The syringe-beak draws marrow from sleeping victims. One bird makes a very good stock for a soup that fills the stomachs of four.

**Lore:** The Marrow Sparrow lights on a sleeper's collarbone and drinks. Its beak is a thin glass needle. The sleeper wakes up tired. The sleeper wakes up paler. The sleeper wakes up, eventually, never.

**Connections:** [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** How many feedings a Marrow Sparrow needs to drain a man, and whether a victim wakes between meals, is canonically left to dread.
