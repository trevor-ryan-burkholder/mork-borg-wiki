# Nesting Death
*hound-sized spider that built a bone-nest in the ceiling*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** Rotblack Sludge (random encounter table a)

A hound-sized spider that built a nest of bones in the ceiling. Wins initiative on 1–4.

HP 12, Morale −, Thick carapace −d2, Bite d4: test Toughness DR12 to avoid freezing (tests are DR+2 for one hour).

**Connections:** [[Accursed Den]], [[Rotblack Sludge]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The freezing-bite suggests a relative of the [[Drowned]] (who freeze water on death) or simply an arachnid evolved for the den's cold. Whether there are more Nesting Deaths elsewhere in the dying world is open.
