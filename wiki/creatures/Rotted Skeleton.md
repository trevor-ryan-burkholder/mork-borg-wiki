# Rotted Skeleton
*marrow-dust, broken bones, infectious*

**Type:** creature / undead
**Source:** Graves Left Wanting, p.7 (random encounter)

HP 5, Morale 7, No armor, Knuckles d2

**Special:** Whenever it takes damage, a puff of vile marrow-dust leaks from its broken bones. Melee attackers must test Toughness DR10 or become infected.

**Lore:** Skulks the fog-bound paths of [[Graven-Tosk]] in pairs and quartets, the bones not so much *walking* as *failing to fall*. The marrow has gone wrong inside them — not dust, exactly, more a fine spore that knows your name. An infected wound in this cemetery will not heal during rest; instead d6 HP is lost daily until something magickal or distant intervenes.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], [[Widow-wraith]], [[Hungry Zombie]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether a Rotted Skeleton, given enough infection-victims, eventually becomes a Hungry Zombie's source — i.e. whether these are the same cycle at different points — is unwritten and would knit two encounters together.
