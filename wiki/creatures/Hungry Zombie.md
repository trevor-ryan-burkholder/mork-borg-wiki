# Hungry Zombie
*the ones who never stopped eating*

**Type:** creature / undead
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.7

HP 6, Morale −, Leather scraps −d2, Claw/Bite d2 + special
**Special:** Test Toughness DR8 or die turning into a zombie after 2 days.

**Lore:** Encountered in groups of d4, eating. Whatever is at hand. The standard Graven-Tosk variant of the walking dead — leaner than [[Zombie|Nodh]] from Bare Bones, but the bite delivers the same patient sentence. Two days, then up. The cure, if there is one, sits canonically atop a pale mountain in an infinitely-miserable forest of dark leaves, and the Hungry Zombies do not seem to know about it.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], [[Zombie]] (Bare Bones variant), [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] (3:1, *those who rest be made to walk*), [[Fathmu IX]] (seeker of the cure)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Why a Graven-Tosk zombie has 1 HP fewer than a standard Nodh is uncommented in canon — the soil's warming, the cemetery's hunger, the fog. Whether the Hungry Zombie's bite produces a Hungry Zombie or a Nodh zombie when the 2 days pass is unclear; the booklet just says *"a zombie."*
