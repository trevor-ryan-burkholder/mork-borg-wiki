# Mulch-squirrels
*eat dirt and taste about the same*

**Type:** creature
**Source:** MBC_Eat-Prey-Kill.pdf, p.5 (Sarkash)

HP 1, Morale 3, Bite/Claws 1 damage

Encountered in nests of d4.

**Special:** Diet of pure leaf-rot and black soil. The meat is brown all the way through and tastes of the forest floor.

**Lore:** They twitch in the dead leaves of Sarkash, fat little things made entirely of mulch. They run from anything that moves. If you catch one and cook it, you have eaten a fistful of forest. That is the best you can say of the meal.

**Connections:** [[Sarkash]], [[Eat-Prey-Kill]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether eating enough Mulch-squirrels does anything strange to the eater — given Sarkash is unnaturally warming — is the kind of detail the forest keeps to itself.
