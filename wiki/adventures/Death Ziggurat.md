# The Death Ziggurat — A Cosmic Necrocrawl
**Type:** adventure / scenario
**Source:** The Death Ziggurat

*"Find the ziggurat and stop the demon from laying waste to our world before the true time!"*

The [[Creton Order]] hires the PCs after cryptic scrolls report a demon escaping her ziggurat prison deep within [[Sarkash]]. The ziggurat is "hidden in another realm" and "the priests carry keys." Generous pay keeps the PCs from asking too many questions.

The unnaturally cold depression yawns deep in Sarkash. Around it the death-obsessed [[Death-Obsessed Cultists]] have camped. Inside it, [[Rot Priest]]s set up symbol-covered tents, [[Sarku]] hangs his dried-up victims upside-down from the trees, ivy-covered ruins rise between fir, the [[Frozen Lake]] of black water-lilies holds moaning undead frozen in mud, and a foul cave opens through a rotting mouth in the woods. Murky clouds whirl down into the depression like a tornado — [[Akünh]]'s channeling.

The [[Spiral Chapel]] holds a hidden compartment containing the [[Spiral Crown]]. Wearing it reverses Akünh's channeling and drains her HP, though it costs Presence tests to remove. Akünh's heart pulses ten meters below the warm rotting maw inside the ziggurat top — destroying it banishes her until the end of days but instantly activates one [[Calendar of Nechrubel|Misery]].

The ziggurat exists in two realms — the living-world version is a barren waste swarming with undead; the real one requires a Rot Priest medallion. The [[Necropolis]] in the [[Death Realm]] guards its bottomless pit of [[Horn Beast]]s.

Tables: d10 ruin types, d10 search results, d12 random events per hex, d10 minor and d6 major treasures.
