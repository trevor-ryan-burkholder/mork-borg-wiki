# The Tenebrous Reliquary — d66 Items of Doom
**Type:** item table / cursed-item supplement
**Source:** MBC_Tenebrous_Reliquary.pdf (full pamphlet, pp.2–7)

Thirty-six accursed objects, contributed by Anders Arpi, Ben H., Dom Cohen, Ripley C., Johan Nohr, Karl Druid, Leander E., Paul Wilde, and Flora v/d B. Each is a relic, weapon, or curse-object — compatible with [[Powers]]-and-scrolls treatment but standing apart from them. Roll d66.

## The Reliquary

### 11. Mercy's Bane
*Spiked Flail (d8).* Additional **d6 damage to surrendering enemies**. The flail knows the difference between a raised blade and an open palm. It prefers the palm.

### 12. Opium Hook
*Metal hook driven directly into an artery (d4 damage).* Grants twice the user's HP as temporary HP for 2 hours. Effect like an opiate. Toughness **DR10** to resist dependency — every sober day deals damage as an infection, and DR rises with each failed attempt. Eventually the user is addicted and the temporary-HP benefit is lost. The hole never closes.

### 13. Plasmatic Idol
Spill blood over this profane idol while resting and roll a die.
- **Odd:** spilled blood becomes red poison — Toughness **DR12** or −10 HP.
- **Even:** until next rest, Agility and Strength tests are +2 DR.

### 14. Ripper's Blade
Deals **no damage** but removes **d4−1 Armor** per hit. It does not wound flesh. It only wants the shell.

### 15. Roses of Winter
A nearly extinct blood-red thorny flower — not a true rose. A single petal eaten **numbs the consumer to all pain** for a few minutes. Damage is received normally but the eater **cannot be killed or broken** during the flower's effect.

### 16. Spine of God
A shriveled, crusty length of vertebrae, warm to the touch. The bearer **attracts the poor**, their intentions unknown even to themselves. Broken: the clouds part — warm sunbeams pierce the heavens **for the first time in a century**.

### 21. Snort Dagger
Disgusting dripping blade reeking of excrement and rot. Killing a goblin **permanently breaks the blade** but undoes the curse, freeing the creature trapped within.

### 22. Stone Magnet
A pair of magnetic stones that crackle and fizz when rubbed. When the square stone is near danger, the round stone glows dull orange. **The stones can lie.**

### 23. Tentacles of Zen
Shadow tendrils creep from a rusty lamp when rubbed. Presence **DR14** — on success the brain struggles and breaks (−1 Presence); otherwise the tentacles take root without resistance (+1 Presence). The lamp does not care which way it goes.

### 24. Tyrant's Tongue
Placed in a skull's mouth, the skull stands upright and **screams its last words before dying**. Over and over and over again.

### 25. Veil of Blood
Place this crimson shroud upon an enemy's head — it tightens, chokes, strangles, and crushes the skull, drowning the torso in blood (**d6 damage per round**). Victims who succumb **arise the next round as a [[Blood-drenched Skeleton]]**, attacking at random.

### 26. Voodoo Fire
Eternally burning green flame inside an oil lamp. Smashed, the flame spreads with the scattered oil — **d4 fire damage** on impact. **Can only be extinguished by the tears of [[SHE]].**

### 31. Volt Thrower
Ancient javelin of unidentifiable charred and pitted metal (**d6 damage**). On a critical hit a lightning bolt strikes the javelin for an additional **2d10 damage, not reduced by armor**. On a fumble the lightning strikes the wielder *before* the javelin is released. Use indoors is cautioned.

### 32. War Starter
Heavy metal round shield emblazoned with a bright crimson pattern. Anyone looking at it tests Presence **DR14** or is **provoked to attack its wielder**.

### 33. Zodiac Lung
Pressing this blackened organ to the chest absorbs it, leaving fetid scars and pustules. The user may **breathe underwater, provided the water is unclean**. After an hour of submersion, the user may expunge the filth as writhing sludge dealing **d4 damage to the closest target**.

### 34. Cup of Peace
A beautiful ceremonial gold-and-silver cup. Drinking from it triggers a Toughness **DR16** or the drinker dies of acute poisoning. There is no second sip.

### 35. Dust of Paradise
Fine pale-white ashes in a void-black pouch. Anyone inhaling rolls Presence **DR14** or is lost in visions of a long-lost pleasant world of joy and mirth, lasting **d6 minutes or until damaged**. The transfixed cannot act, perceive, or respond in any way to the real world.

### 36. Ebony Tears
A vial of black liquid. On contact a living creature tests Presence **DR14** or is overcome with grief — **immediately failing a morale check** in combat. Outside combat the affected acts as if indifferent, unable to attack as it struggles with despair for **3d10 minutes**.

### 41. Eye of Horus
Placed upon the forehead, the eye grants vision into the abyss at a price. When checking for traps, you **see your grisly death from a less-careful timeline** and may better find a workaround. Overland travel is mired with difficulty as you wade through the corpses.

### 42. Finger Paintings of the Insane
Slick oily paints, forever wet upon an old wooden palette. Those touching any colour test Toughness **DR12** or lose themselves for a minute painting the nearest surface with heretical imagery. Marking [[Powers|scroll]] text with the paint imbues it with those lost to the pigments' souls — **−d4 from the Presence test to cast**.

### 43. Flower of Disease
Seeds for a rapidly spreading thorny weed with a poisonous aroma. Can **swallow a small village in two days** if not fought. Weak against boiled oil. **Fire does nothing.**

### 44. Foehammer
Cursed warhammer (**2d6 damage**) whose wielder develops a new grudge with a random person daily, lasting until Foehammer caves their head in. Neglecting a grudge for too long counts as an infection.

### 45. Cauldron of Lies
Beverages cooked in this cauldron and shared allow all imbibers to **recognize when another is lying for a month**. Used in cults and secret societies to single out traitors.

### 46. Cursed Tongue of the Naga
Putting this long forked tongue into a skull pushes the skull upright, **screaming bloody filth while running about on each tongue-tip**.

### 51. Chaos Blade
Zweihänder dealing **2d8 damage**. On maximum damage **the Power [[Death]] is cast**.

### 52. Chains of Death
A set of frigid black manacles. If you die wearing them, they **unlatch, ensnare your killer, and drag them to another world before vanishing**.

### 53. Claw of the Sloth
Dagger (**d4 damage**). On a 1, attack and defense DR against that target are **−2 next round**. If DR reaches 0 the target is **frozen in place, permanently**.

### 54. Blood of the Serpents
Green-red liquid, highly acidic and poisonous. Turns blood neon-green and spreads through bodily liquids. When the infected is attacked, the attacker tests Agility **DR12** to dodge a spray of blood — **reduces Armor one tier** and **transmits the condition**.

### 55. Crown of Burning Stars
Adds **Omens equal to the number of [[Calendar of Nechrubel|Miseries]] that have transpired** to each character's pool. **Miseries now occur on 1 & 2.**

### 56. Book of Oblivion
Sentient black leather tome with blank pages. With Presence **DR12**, a creature **permanently transfers knowledge — a thought or a memory — to the book**, forgetting the thing transferred. On failure the book takes something extra. You can learn anything the book previously absorbed in exchange for something you don't want to give it.

### 61. Antlers of Lightning
A crackling mantle. When reading any [[Powers|scroll]], flip a coin. On Heads, the scroll's effects are **replaced by Nine Violet Signs Unknot the Storm**. Upon the wearer's death, lightning rends the flesh from bone, **leaving only the mantle**.

### 62. Eyes and Teeth
Replace **d10 missing teeth and d2 eyes**. Toughness **DR14** or rejection causes infection. Effects of new eyes/teeth (d4 each):

**Eyes (d4):**

| d4 | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Wandering.** DR +2 to ranged attacks. |
| 2 | **Morbid Visions.** Every rest, share your nightmare with the group. |
| 3 | Let there be light! (no ill effects) |
| 4 | **SIGHT BEYOND SIGHT!** Test Presence DR10 for eyes to twitch in danger's presence — *it's not YOUR eye though, it can lie.* |

**Teeth (d4):**

| d4 | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Toothache.** DR +2 to Powers' Presence roll. |
| 2 | Functional but hideous. |
| 3 | You got teeth! (no ill effects) |
| 4 | **FANGS!** Gain bite attack (d6 damage); on 1–2 an enemy gets a free attack. |

### 63. Black Candles
When lit, the jet-black flame **flickers toward the greatest threat within a mile** (or kilometer).

### 64. Robe of Bones
This lumpy cape rattles in the wind. Once daily, the wearer may stare blankly at a humanoid creature as the cape guides their hands. With a fold of the robe, the target tests Toughness **DR13** or a **random limb's bone snaps soundlessly** as the robe crackles.

### 65. Ash of the Mind
Smear a sigil onto one's palm and touch another sentient being to **read their thoughts**. Their Presence **DR12** test, or **their thoughts become yours**.

### 66. Bowels of a Baby Killer
Take the bowl and drape the entrails over you, gaining a monster's scent. **Evil creatures must make a DR14 check** when attempting to discern your location. Should they fail, you pass by unnoticed.

**Connections:** [[Powers]], [[SHE]] (tears extinguish Voodoo Fire), [[Blood-drenched Skeleton]] (Veil of Blood victims rise as these), [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] (Crown of Burning Stars accelerates Miseries), [[Death]] (cast on Chaos Blade max damage)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Several items reference world-events implying lore beyond the line — *"the first time in a century"* (Spine of God), *"the tears of SHE"* (Voodoo Fire), the *"another world"* where Chains of Death drags killers. The d66 numbering suggests room for entries 11–16 / 21–26 / etc. in the gaps — homebrew expansion is invited by the table's own shape.
