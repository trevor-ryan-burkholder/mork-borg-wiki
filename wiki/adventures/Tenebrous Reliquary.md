# The Tenebrous Reliquary — d66 Items of Doom
**Type:** item table
**Source:** MBC The Tenebrous Reliquary

Sixty-six accursed items contributed by guest designers. Each is a relic, weapon, or curse-object compatible with [[Powers]]-and-scrolls treatment but standing apart from them.

## Sampling (the source has all 66; representative entries here)

- **11. Mercy's Bane** — Spiked Flail (d8). Additional d6 damage to surrendering enemies.
- **12. Opium Hook** — Metal hook into an artery (d4). Twice the HP as temporary HP for 2 hours. Resists dependency with Toughness DR10; every sober day deals damage as an infection; DR rises with each failed attempt. Eventually the user is addicted and loses the temp-HP benefit.
- **13. Plasmatic Idol** — Spill blood while resting. Odd: blood becomes red poison (Toughness DR12 or −10 HP). Even: Agility and Strength tests are +2 DR until next rest.
- **14. Ripper's Blade** — Deals no damage but removes d4−1 Armor.
- **15. Roses of Winter** — A rare blood-red thorny flower. A petal numbs the eater to pain for a few minutes — damage received normally but can't be killed or broken during the flower's effects.
- **16. Spine of God** — Shriveled crusty vertebrae, warm to the touch. The bearer attracts the poor, their intentions unknown even to themselves. Broken, the clouds part — warm sunbeams pierce the heavens for the first time in a century.
- **21. Snort Dagger** — Disgusting dripping blade reeking of excrement. Killing a goblin permanently breaks the blade but undoes the curse, freeing the creature within.
- **22. Stone Magnet** — Pair of magnetic stones. Square stone near danger makes round stone glow dull orange. **The stones can lie.**
- **23. Tentacles of Zen** — Shadow tendrils creep from a rusty lamp when rubbed. DR14 Presence — on success the brain struggles and breaks (−1 Presence); otherwise tentacles take root without resistance (+1 Presence).
- **24. Tyrant's Tongue** — Placed in a skull's mouth, causes the skull to scream its last words before dying. Over and over and over again.
- **25. Veil of Blood** — Crimson shroud placed on an enemy's head tightens, chokes, strangles, crushes the skull, drowning the torso in blood (d6 per round). Victims arise the next round as [[Blood-drenched Skeleton|blood-drenched skeletons]], attacking at random.
- **26. Voodoo Fire** — Eternally burning green flame in an oil lamp. Smashed, the flame spreads with the scattered oil (d4 fire damage). **Can only be extinguished by the tears of [[SHE]].**

…and 40 more (Spider Quill, Eye of the Devourer, Witch-skull Brazier, etc. — all in source).

**Connections:** [[Powers]], [[SHE]] (tears extinguish Voodoo Fire), [[Blood-drenched Skeleton]] (Veil of Blood victims rise as these)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** This wiki entry samples the table. A full transcription of all 66 entries is an obvious expansion task. Several items (Voodoo Fire, Spine of God) reference world-events ("first time in a century," "tears of SHE") that imply lore beyond the line itself.
