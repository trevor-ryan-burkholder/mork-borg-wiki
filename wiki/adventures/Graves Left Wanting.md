# Graves Left Wanting
**Type:** adventure / scenario
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting

An immense cemetery, older and greater than memory, [[Graven-Tosk]] sits in [[Sarkash]] like a festering scab. To the east, the [[Shadow King]]'s manse looms always, impossibly equidistant. Although abandoned since the palace's ruination, bodies somehow keep finding their way inside. All the while, living and unliving attempt to claw their way out.

Intended as a starting adventure, a one-shot, or a post-TPK reset. The PCs awaken in the Plague Pit — in coffins under suffocating earth, or wherever the GM finds appropriate. There isn't a defined ending or clear escape path. Once the **Undertaker's Hut** is explored, players should plan a getaway or be hopelessly lost.

## Locations
- **The Strangly Tree** (1)
- **The Plague Pit** (2)
- **The Origin Fountain** (3)
- **Maus' Vomatorium** (4)
- **The Colander Room** (interior)
- **The Catacomb Corridor** to **Orerhard's Tomb**
- **The Undertaker's Hut**

Travel between places takes 15 minutes; roll d4 to see where the path leads (the fog rearranges things). Once all four primary locations are visited, the next path leads to the Undertaker's Hut. The Roach Herder knows which way to go.

## Encounters (d10, per traverse)
1. d4 **Rotted Skeletons** skulking.
2. a ghastly **Widow-wraith**.
3. d4 **Unbred Mutts** digging.
4. d4 **Half-billed Ravens**.
5. d4 **Hungry Zombies** eating.
6–10. A random PC experiences **Sensory Strangeness** (a d8 of small dreadful disorientations — muffled scratching, everyone walking backwards, sweating-while-spine-cold, etc.).
