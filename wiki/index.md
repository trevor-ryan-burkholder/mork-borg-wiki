# MÖRK BORG — A Canonical Wiki of a Dying World

*The Wind from the west. From the sundered land. Rot rides it, and the stench of blood.*

This wiki extracts the lore, places, people, monsters, items, and tables of MÖRK BORG from canonical source — the **Bare Bones Edition**, the **Rules Reference**, the official adventures **Rotblack Sludge**, **Devil's Tomb**, **The Death Ziggurat**, **Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch**, **Bloat**, **The Goblin Grinder**, **Graves Left Wanting**, **Eat-Prey-Kill** — the classes **Forlorn Philosopher**, **Pale One**, **Cursed Skinwalker**, **Dead God's Prophet**, **Sacrilegious Songbird** — the creatures **Bone Bowyer**, **Membrane of Sarkantha**, **Sarcopha-Ghost** — and the table-toolkits **Tablets of Ochre Obscurity**, **d100 Items and Trinkets**, **Blackpowder Weapons**, **Overland Travel**, **Unheroic Feats**, **Tenebrous Reliquary**, and **The Merchant**.

Nothing is invented. Where canon leaves a gap, the entry's **Gaps/Expansion Notes** section flags it for later development.

## Top-Level Expansion Folders
These folders are intentionally empty in the canon-extraction pass. They are where **new** content goes — see `CLAUDE.md.md`:
- `/monsters` — new creatures built to spec
- `/locations` — expanded regions and places
- `/factions` — new cults, organizations, powers
- `/tables` — new random generators
- `/adventures` — new scenario seeds and modules

The wiki under `/wiki` is canon. Everything outside is expansion.
