# Dead God's Prophet
**Type:** class
**Source:** MBC Dead God's Prophet

You hear voices in your head, telling you what to do. Or at least you think you do. You are the prophet of a god; only your god is dead, slain by the Basilisk [[SHE]] within the wretched peaks of [[Bergen Chrypt]]. Now no one wants to hear the words of your god. But you can still hear them. Day and night. Like hot wires through your brain.

- **Begins with:** 1d6×5s and d3 Omens.
- **HP:** Toughness + d4.
- **Abilities:** Obsessed — 3d6+2 Presence. Weak through lack of care for bodily functions — 3d6−2 Toughness.
- Roll d4 on the weapon table and d2 on the armor table.
- **Literacy:** You are literate and can use scrolls, but must pass a Presence DR12 test when encountering them not to immediately attempt to destroy them as words of false gods. If you start with a scroll, consider that you passed the test.
- Roll randomly for **two gifts** from your dead god.

**Name your god... (3d10):** three columns — adjective + title + dread-domain — e.g. *Häil, King of panic* or *Öde, Song of the flesh*. Sample roll: Acrophoe Beacon of blood; Elioch Bearer of death; Ekk Bride of decay; Ghzat Child of flies; Gnost Herald of impotence; Häil King of panic; Kvera Lady of rot; Malais Prince of rust; Varkka Queen of unease; Öde Song of the flesh.

**Two gifts (d8):**
1. **Blood Tells.** Flagellant — beat yourself before combat (d3 self-damage) for +2 to a number of rolls equal to HP lost.
2. **Lord of Nothing.** Bizarre kingly garb — +2 to rolls to impress the easily fooled.
3. **Smell of Decay.** Tests to defend against attacks +2 due to gut-wrenching stench.
4. **Incoherent Sophistic Babble.** Listeners test Presence DR10 or lose next action.
5. **Ichor of Rot.** Black stigmata — DR12 attack to wipe hands on bare skin; victim Toughness DR12 or frothing spasms for two turns.
6. **My Body, A Vessel.** Once a day let your god's ghost enter you; ask the GM one yes/no question they must answer truthfully. You are then left weakened and frail.
7. **In Death I Live.** When you die, Presence DR14 — pass and your god refuses you the afterlife and you recover with 1 HP. Fail and your body is consumed in holy flames.
8. **Eyes of Holy Fire.** Pupils burn — once per combat, fire attack from eyes (DR10) d6 damage; blinded for the rest of the fight.

**Connections:** [[SHE]], [[Bergen Chrypt]], [[Heretical Priest]] (parallel hunted-prophet archetype), [[Forlorn Philosopher]] (parallel listened-too-long archetype)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The 100 possible god-names generated by the table are not given individual lore; each is a hook. SHE's god-slaying spree in Bergen Chrypt is canonical and entirely unmapped — how many gods? When? Why? An entire god-graveyard adventure waits there.
