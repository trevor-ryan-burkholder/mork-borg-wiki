# Heretical Priest
**Type:** class
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.54–55

Hunted by the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] of the One True Faith, this heretic can be found raving in ruins, traipsing endlessly down dusty roads, and desecrating cathedrals by night.

- **Begins with:** 3d6 × 10s and d4 Omens.
- **HP:** Toughness + d8.

**Unholy origin (d6):**
1. [[Galgenbeck]], near the [[Cathedral of the Two-Headed Basilisks]].
2. Massacred [[Alliáns]] cult, sole survivor.
3. The crypts of [[Grift]].
4. Temple ruins in the [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]].
5. One of the many [[Graven-Tosk]] thief-tunnels.
6. Secret [[Bergen Chrypt]] church.

**Connections:** [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Inquisition]], [[Galgenbeck]], [[Alliáns]], [[Grift]], [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Bergen Chrypt]], [[Dead God's Prophet]] (a parallel heretic class), [[Forlorn Philosopher]] (a hunted intellectual)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Source plaintext has the rest of the playbook (abilities, gifts) largely blank — origin table is complete, the rest is gap.
