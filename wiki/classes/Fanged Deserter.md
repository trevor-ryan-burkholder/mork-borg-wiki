# Fanged Deserter
**Type:** class
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.46–47

*You have thirty or so friends who never let you down: YOUR TEETH.* Disloyal, deranged, or simply uncontrollable. Any group that didn't boot you out, you left anyway. But your parliament of teeth — enormous, protruding, thick, and sharp — have always been your allies.

- **Bite attack:** dr10 to attack, d6 damage. Must be close to target. 1–2 on d6 chance the enemy gets a free attack.
- **Begins with:** 2d6 × 10s and d2 Omens.
- **HP:** Toughness + d10.

**Earliest (d6):**
1. A burnt-black building in [[Sarkash]]. Your home?
2. A derelict rotting ship rolling endlessly across a grey sea.
3. A brothel in [[Schleswig]]. Quite a friendly environment.
4. *(unwritten in plaintext source)*
5. Following an army in eastern [[Western Kingdom]].
6. Suckling a wolf in the wild of [[Bergen Chrypt]].

**You begin with one of (d6):**
- 5: **Ancient gore-hound** — asthmatic, deluded, and on its last legs. Superb nose. Sniffles up treasure in the most disgusting debris. Attacks dr10 (bite d6), defends dr12, 10 hp. Becomes frenzied around goblins and berserkers.
- Others 1, 2, 3, 4, 6: blanked in source plaintext.

**Connections:** [[Sarkash]], [[Schleswig]], [[Western Kingdom]], [[Bergen Chrypt]], [[Endless Sea]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The Bare Bones plaintext we are working from has several blank table entries (the gear/companion table, abilities). Treat the missing entries as deliberate gaps to fill from the full-art rulebook or to invent at the table.
