# Sacrilegious Songbird
**Type:** class
**Source:** MBC Songbird

Always the bringer of unwelcome sounds, your vocation as a bard left you destitute and disreputable… until the rueful day when a being of impious character made you the deal of a lifetime. Your soul may be screaming, but your throat sings sweetly, and your purse ever sweeter. A small price to pay.

- **Begins with:** 4d6×10s and d2 Omens.
- **HP:** Toughness + d6.
- **Abilities:** Amplified personality — 3d6+2 Presence. Hard life — 3d6−2 Toughness.
- Roll d6 on the weapon table and d3 on the armor table.
- **Unnatural charm:** spend a use of your daily Powers to add or subtract d6 to any Morale check.

**A deal was struck (d6):**
1. In an outhouse just outside [[Grift]].
2. Hiding from guards in [[Galgenbeck]].
3. In a [[Western Kingdom]] noble's bedchamber.
4. Dreaming in a disremembered ditch.
5. In a [[Schleswig]] prison cell.
6. Minutes from freezing to death on the way to [[Alliáns]].

**You were gifted one accursed instrument (d6):**
1. **[[Gut Pipes of Destiny]]** — sown hairy gutsacks with bone pipes. Listeners filled with unearthly week-long hunger.
2. **[[Hurty-Gurdy]]** — droning stringed thing. Presence DR12 or take d2 damage (escalating).
3. **[[Spinal Husk]]** — drum made from a hellion's shell. 2d6: 6+ all fall prone, 11 spine-breaking d4 creatures, 12 deafen yourself for 24 hours. Also doubles as a shield.
4. **[[Devil's Aquifer]]** — horn of a beelzebubonic creature from the deepest depths of the [[Endless Sea]]. Toughness DR14 or fill lungs with seawater.
5. **[[Mouth-to-Mouth-Organ]]** — bony harp. Broken listener recovers d4 HP and cannot speak until full health.
6. **[[Lute of the Acute Brute]]** — axe-shaped (d8) lute. Strength and Presence scores swap for d6 days in any listener.

**Connections:** [[Grift]], [[Galgenbeck]], [[Western Kingdom]], [[Schleswig]], [[Alliáns]], [[Endless Sea]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The "being of impious character" with whom the deal was made is unspecified — could be any of the dying world's many devils, demons, or Death-Realm denizens. The "beelzebubonic creature from the deepest depths" is named only as the Devil's Aquifer's source.
