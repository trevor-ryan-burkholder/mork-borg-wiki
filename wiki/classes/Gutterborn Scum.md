# Gutterborn Scum
**Type:** class
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.48–49

An ill star smiled upon your birth. Poverty, crime, and bad parenting didn't help either. In your community an honest day's work was never an option. Not that you ever tried, what are you, some kind of mug? A razor blade and a moonless night are worth a week of chump-work.

- **Begins with:** 1d6×10s and d2 Omens.
- **HP:** Toughness + d6.

**Bad Birth (d6):**
1. Dumped onto a moving shit-cart still in your birth caul.
2–6. *(blank in plaintext source)*

**Specialty mechanic:** The first time the Scum gets better (see Getting Better, page 33) another specialty is rolled. From the second time the Scum gets better, it's possible to reroll these two specialities or just one.

Compare the [[Scum]] creature entry — Bent and his ilk are gutter-born too. A successful Gutterborn Scum has more or less escaped that fate.

**Connections:** [[Galgenbeck]], [[Schleswig]], [[Scum]] (creature)

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Source plaintext has the Bad Birth table and Specialties largely blank. Treat as expansion seeds for a slum-themed playbook variant.
