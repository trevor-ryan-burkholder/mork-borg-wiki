# The Paunchy Swine
*a tavern where the mystics drink and don't shave*

**Type:** location / tavern
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.69 (Adventure Seeds, Who 14)

A tavern named once and only once in canon — the kind of place an *"unshaved mystic"* sends adventurers from, in the Adventure Seeds table. Beyond that, the smell of cheap beer, hog fat, and somebody else's prophecy. The Swine is wherever the GM needs a quest to start.

**Connections:** [[Adventure Seeds]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** City and street are unwritten — could be in [[Galgenbeck]], [[Schleswig]], or any nameless crossroad. Innkeeper, regular patrons, and what's on the menu are all unfilled. A perfectly serviceable "drop the party here" location.
