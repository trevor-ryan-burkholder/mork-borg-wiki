# Wästland
*the dry, dead, blasted west — the sundered land*

**Type:** region
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.15–16; MBC The Merchant, p.5 (Wästland wares)

The western reach of the dying world, called **Wästland** *"in the songs of the simple and rhymes of the poor."* Once the realm of [[Lake Onda]]'s fish and a thriving river-trade, now a slow-dying countryside of starved villages and empty roads. The kingdom that rules it is [[Western Kingdom|the Western Kingdom]] of King [[Fathmu IX]], schemer of [[Schleswig]]'s sad-but-gaudy halls. From the west, says Misery **4:3**, *"forth comes fire, and a horde, and the Kingdoms burn."*

What the locals carry says what the locals are. [[Mikhael the Merchant]]'s Wästland goods — pouches of ashy valley-vapors not made for living lungs, jars of troll piss for the desperate camp, gaudy clothing for those who'd be mocked as nobles, a bloodstained pilgrim's compass that points only toward what will kill you, a peasant's kuksa that turns any liquid into adequate drink, a lucky fishing spear that always strikes *something* — paint a region of the half-starved and the half-resigned. Pilgrims who walk pointing at their own deaths. Fishermen reduced to one trick. Peasants drinking from a cup so they will not drink from the river.

The [[Fanged Deserter]] (origin 5) follows an army in eastern Wästland. The [[Pale One]] (origin 5) hatches from an egg in a Wästland swamp. The [[Forlorn Philosopher]] (origin 3) is brought up by pig-ignorant churls in a peaceful Wästland forest. The [[Sacrilegious Songbird]] (origin 3) makes their bargain in a Wästland noble's bedchamber. The [[Wretched Royalty]] (origin 1) loses a Wästland palace, reduced to rubble. Local fauna includes the **[[Liar-bird]]**, **[[Three-thirds-pheasant]]**, **[[Feather Fox]]**, **[[Bautaboar]]** (*"only thirteen left in Wästland"*), **[[Schleswig Bog-feeder]]**, and the **[[Gold-crested Filth-crow]]** (per *Eat-Prey-Kill*, p.9).

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Wästland and the Western Kingdom are not clearly delineated in source — *Wästland* is the song-name of the place, *Western Kingdom* the political name; this entry is the regional/cultural one. The geography of the west — what dried, what survived, where the river went, where the army goes — is entirely undescribed. Bog-witches living in Wästland swamps are mentioned only once. Villages other than Schleswig are unnamed. The bards of the simple who *call* it Wästland leave no songs in canon.
