# Graven-Tosk
**Type:** cemetery / region
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.12; Devil's Tomb; recurring

A truly ancient cemetery filled with mausoleums, blank-eyed cherubs, stagnant fountains, plague pits, and ordinary graves. It lies somewhere in [[Sarkash]] — always where one least expects to find it, in a halo of dying trees.

Misery **3:1** reads: *"At Graven-Tosk the soil shall grow warm and those who rest be made to walk."*

Rising over Graven-Tosk like rage rising over pain is the [[Palace of the Shadow King]]. Deep beneath it stretches the [[Devil's Tomb]] — a former hellhole now infested by [[Drowned]] and the [[Plant of Life]] (a giant pulsing cactus growing toward the [[Pit to Hell]]).

The [[Pale One]] may have been accidentally summoned here by cultists (origin 2). The [[Heretical Priest]] (origin 5) may come from one of Graven-Tosk's many thief-tunnels. There is talk of "a forgotten part of Graven-Tosk" in the adventure seeds.

**Connections:** [[Sarkash]], [[Palace of the Shadow King]], [[Devil's Tomb]], [[Drowned]], [[Plant of Life]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] (3:1), [[Pale One]], [[Heretical Priest]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Source describes the cemetery in atmospheric strokes, not maps. The plague pits, the stagnant fountains, individual mausolea, and the network of thief-tunnels are all undescribed. Whether the warming soil of Misery 3:1 has begun even before the prophecy formally activates is a GM call.
