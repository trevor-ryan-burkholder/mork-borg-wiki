# Bergen Chrypt
**Type:** mountain range
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.11; recurring

The massive western wall of the [[The Dying World]] — catacombs and ice-caked peaks east. Called **The Spears**. From the cracks of Bergen Chrypt, when the world was but water and dust, [[SHE]] first crawled. She spawned many basilisks here, casting them down its cliffs — only [[HE]] survived. The dead god of the [[Dead God's Prophet]] was slain by SHE within the wretched peaks of Bergen Chrypt.

Misery **2:3** reads: *"And from the Spears: a frost. Born from Bergen Chrypt and covering all."*

The [[Fanged Deserter]] (origin 6) may have been suckled by a wolf in the wild of Bergen Chrypt. The [[Pale One]] (origin 3) may have been vomited forth by a foul beast here. The [[Forlorn Philosopher]] (origin 5) may have been abandoned in Bergen Chrypt's catacombs. The [[Heretical Priest]] (origin 6) may come from a secret Bergen Chrypt church. The [[Cursed Skinwalker]]'s bear-form (creature shape 4) is "Bear from Bergen Chrypt." [[The Basilisks Demand]] occasionally asks for *"the gall of a Chrypt-vulture."*

Adventure seeds: "A weirdness leaves Bergen Chrypt" (85–86). "At the Bergen Chrypt tree line" (10) one risks one's life.

**Connections:** [[SHE]], [[HE]], [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]] (where HE lives, gazing back at the peak), [[Dead God's Prophet]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] (2:3), [[Fanged Deserter]], [[Pale One]], [[Forlorn Philosopher]], [[Heretical Priest]], [[Cursed Skinwalker]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The catacombs are referenced over and over but never mapped. Chrypt-vultures, bears, foul beasts that vomit out Pale Ones, secret churches, dead gods slain in the peaks — all named, none detailed. Bergen Chrypt is canon's deepest gap; it should be a whole mountain-cycle module.
