# The Derelict Mill
**Type:** building / dungeon
**Source:** The Goblin Grinder

A boarded-up mill in or near [[Galgenbeck]]. Recently inherited by the alchemist [[Nagel Krat]] from his father (and victim) [[Urvan Krat]]. Inside, Nagel discovered an enormous contraption labeled **The [[Goblin Grinder]]** along with a stash of alchemical supplies — and built a scheme: get corpses from gravedigger [[Qarg]], feed them into the Grinder, produce living goblins that infest Galgenbeck, sell a fake "[[Goblin Cure]]" at a steadily-rising price.

Four floors plus a basement:
- **Barricade & Cannonball** (1a–1b) — crude trash-wall barricade and a cannon aimed at the entrance, manned by giggling goblins
- **Basement** — Urvan Krat's body, a spare Grinder key in his boot
- **Fire! Fire!** (2) — three archer goblins with flaming arrows and one bucket-of-oil goblin
- **Laboratory** (3) — boiling alchemy tables and an [[Alchemical Ooze]]
- **The Goblin Grinder** (4) — the device itself, fed by key, fuel, and one human corpse

The mill connects to [[Medickal Shoppe]] (Nagel's storefront in Galgenbeck) where the fake Cure is sold.

**Connections:** [[Galgenbeck]], [[Nagel Krat]], [[Urvan Krat]], [[Qarg]], [[The Bastard]], [[Goblin Grinder]] (device), [[Goblin Cure]], [[Goblin]], [[Goblin Curse]], [[Medickal Shoppe]], [[Alchemical Ooze]]
