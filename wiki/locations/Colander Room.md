# Colander Room
*every hole drips, none of them clean*

**Type:** location / sub-site of [[Graven-Tosk]]
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.8

A chamber south of [[Erhard's Tomb]], named for the perforated stone of its ceiling — water, mud, and worse strain through the holes onto the floor below. Whatever was sieved here once is long since gone; only the dripping remains, and the smell of old wet stone.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], [[Erhard's Tomb]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** What the room was originally used for — funerary draining, ritual washing, torture — is not specified. Whether the dripping ever stops, in any season, is canonically silent.
