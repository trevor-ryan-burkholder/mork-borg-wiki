# Roach Herder's Lair
*throne of trash, herd of vermin, hermit of the cemetery*

**Type:** location / sub-site of [[Graven-Tosk]]
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.13

A hot and humid chamber that stinks of rancid sweat, reached through the north tunnel of the [[Catacomb Corridor]]. [[The Roach Herder]] sits here on a throne built of debris and grave-loot, roach whip in grubby hand, while Meatroaches and [[Twice-grown Corpse Fly|Twice-grown Corpse Flies]] sit and scurry on the shit-stained floor. A hatch opens into the [[Palace of the Shadow King|Shadow King's Sewer]] beneath.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], [[The Roach Herder]], [[Catacomb Corridor]], [[Meatroach]], [[Twice-grown Corpse Fly]], [[Palace of the Shadow King]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the throne's stolen valuables are inventoried anywhere — and what the Roach Herder has actually managed to keep — is left to the GM. The hatch to the sewer is one-way in the booklet's description; whether anything comes up through it is unstated.
