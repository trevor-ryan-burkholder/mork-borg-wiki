# Roseate Baritona
*a cursed organ, an ivory horn bedecked with the skulls of a noble family*

**Type:** cursed item / heirloom of [[Fela Maus]]
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.10 (Maus' Vomatorium)

Human-sized ivory horn bedecked with [[Fela Maus|Maus']] family's skulls and bound in magical entrails. When blown, it spews a noise that forces listeners to test Toughness DR10 or vomit acid, taking d6 damage. *Blower beware.* It floats now in the gall beside Maus' bones in [[Maus' Vomatorium]] — the bog hag's curse turned it against the family that owned it, and the skulls on its bell are theirs.

**Connections:** [[Fela Maus]], [[Maus' Vomatorium]], [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Graves Left Wanting]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the Baritona can be played without being lifted from the gall, and whether removing it from the chamber dispels or transfers the curse, is unwritten. The number of skulls on its bell — and whose, by name — is canon-silent.
