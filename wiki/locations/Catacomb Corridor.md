# Catacomb Corridor
*the long damp throat between the dead and the deader*

**Type:** location / sub-site of [[Graven-Tosk]]
**Source:** MBC Graves Left Wanting, p.11

A narrow crawl space and corridor between [[Erhard's Tomb]] and the [[Roach Herder's Lair]], its walls stacked with urns and skull-shelves. Out of the soft skulls hatch [[Twice-grown Corpse Fly|Twice-grown Corpse Flies]], drawn by warmth and the smell of the still-breathing. The fog of [[Graven-Tosk]] follows you in and does not follow you out.

**Connections:** [[Graven-Tosk]], [[Graves Left Wanting]], [[Erhard's Tomb]], [[Roach Herder's Lair]], [[Twice-grown Corpse Fly]], [[The Undertaker]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The number of urns is uncounted; whose ashes they hold is not written. Whether the corridor's length changes with the cemetery's fog-shifts — as the surface trails do — is unstated.
