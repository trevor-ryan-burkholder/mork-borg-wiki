# The Medickal Shoppe
**Type:** business
**Source:** The Goblin Grinder

[[Nagel Krat]]'s storefront in or near [[Galgenbeck]]. Smells vaguely metallic. Shelves brim with the fraudulent [[Goblin Cure]]. [[Qarg]] stands around during busy afternoons looking tough, occasionally breaking up fights. Nagel carries all his silver in his apron, planning to deposit it later at the [[Derelict Mill]].

Hidden beneath a latch in the floor behind the counter is the **Goblin Crate** — d4+1 goblins ready to leap out and attack everyone except Nagel if he needs to escape.

Sells:
- **Goblin Cure** (40s, +5 daily) — postpones total transformation by d6 days, isn't actually a cure
- **Flash Powder** (45s) — likely blinds you as well as your enemies
- **Healing Tincture** (30s) — mostly mouse dung, dirty water, and red pigment; on a roll of 1, attacks/defence at −2 until full rest
- **Invigorating Elixir** (25s) — short-term +1 Agility for 1 hour; might stop your heart
- **Juice** (7s) — this is just juice. He uses apples? It's apple juice.

**Connections:** [[Nagel Krat]], [[Qarg]], [[Goblin Cure]], [[Derelict Mill]], [[Galgenbeck]], [[Goblin Curse]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Source places the shop near the mill but not on a city map. Other Galgenbeck competitors and the Inquisition's possible interest in Nagel's fake cure are open.
