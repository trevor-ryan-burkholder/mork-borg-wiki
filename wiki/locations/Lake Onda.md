# Lake Onda
**Type:** lake
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.15

Once the lake gifted fish and the river-trade thrived. The [[Western Kingdom]] knew peace and wealth then. That was before.

[[The Basilisks Demand]] occasionally calls for *"a body drowned in Lake Onda."* Adventure seed 9–10: "Children missing at Lake Onda."

## Regional Fauna (Eat-Prey-Kill, p.10)

What the lake gives up now, instead of fish:

- [[Cursed Trout]] — a hollow fish. Gutting one earns d6 days without sleep.
- [[Rusty Bass]] — eats metal, tastes of metal, gives you "deathetanus."
- [[The Groan]] — *"the wind stops when the Groan is near."* Defence rolled with Presence.
- [[Carcasswan]] (lone or pair, d2) — mates for life. The survivor eats the dead, and you.
- [[Unresting Duck]] (d8) — never lands.
- [[Sursturgeon]] — ferments alive.

**Connections:** [[Western Kingdom]], [[Schleswig]], [[The Basilisks Demand]], [[Cursed Trout]], [[Rusty Bass]], [[The Groan]], [[Carcasswan]], [[Unresting Duck]], [[Sursturgeon]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Why the fish are gone and the river-trade dead is unstated. The disappearing children of seed 9–10 are unexplained. What lives in the lake now is open.
