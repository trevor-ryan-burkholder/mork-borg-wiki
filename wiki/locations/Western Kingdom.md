# The Western Kingdom (Wästland)
**Type:** kingdom / region
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.15–16

Called **Wästland** in the songs of the simple and rhymes of the poor. Once home to peace and wealth when [[Lake Onda]] gifted fish and the river-trade thrived. Now terror and despotism stalk.

In the secret citadel of the sad-but-gaudy city of [[Schleswig]], King [[Fathmu IX]] schemes. Paranoid, fat, and increasingly mad, he is consumed with psychosis and invisible fears. Obsessed with the prophecies of [[Verhu]], the King raids and invades houses and villages, barns, and temples. Nowhere and no one is safe, especially the poor. Taxed into starvation, the contents of their larders and storehouses are carted off by Fathmu's men.

The [[Fanged Deserter]] (origin 5) may have followed an army in eastern Wästland. The [[Pale One]] (origin 5) may have hatched from an egg laid by a bog-witch in a Wästland swamp. The [[Forlorn Philosopher]] (origin 3) may have been brought up by illiterate, pig-ignorant churls in a peaceful Wästland forest. The [[Sacrilegious Songbird]] (origin 3) may have struck their bargain in a Wästland noble's bedchamber.

Misery **4:3** reads: *"Look to the West. Forth comes fire, and a horde, and the Kingdoms burn."* The Merchant carries "Wästland" goods: Pouch of Valley Vapors, Jar of Troll Piss, Fine but Gaudy Clothing, Pilgrim's Compass, Peasant's Kuksa, Lucky Fishing Spear.

The [[Swamp Witch]]'s [[Sepulchre of the Swamp Witch]] is reached by intercepting messengers wearing Fathmu IX's crest.

**Connections:** [[Schleswig]], [[Fathmu IX]], [[Lake Onda]], [[Verhu]], [[Swamp Witch]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] (4:3), [[Sacrilegious Songbird]], [[Pale One]], [[Forlorn Philosopher]], [[Fanged Deserter]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** The full geography of Wästland — its swamps, forests, villages (only [[Nechdorf]] is named in source, and not as a Wästland village explicitly), and its army — is undescribed. The river-trade that once thrived from Lake Onda is gone; what happened to the river is implied but unwritten. Bog-witches living in the swamps are referenced only as a Pale One origin point.
