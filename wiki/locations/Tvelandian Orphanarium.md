# Tvelandian Orphanarium
*the institution they fled, the slum that bred them*

**Type:** location / slum institution
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.48 (Gutterborn Scum, Bad Birth 5)

A slum orphanage in or near [[Tveland]] — named only in the [[Gutterborn Scum]] bad-birth table as the place they *escaped*. Whatever wardens run it, whatever rations they feed the children, whatever the children turn into when they leave through the wrong door — none of it is on the page. What is on the page is that escape is what one does there.

**Connections:** [[Tveland]], [[Gutterborn Scum]], [[Galgenbeck]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Location within Tveland is not specified — south of Galgenbeck is implied by the trade routes, but unwritten. The orphanage's wardens, founders, and patron (if any) are blank. A small faction or starting-location hook ripe for expansion.
