# The Dead Gods
**Type:** slain pantheon / cosmology
**Source:** MÖRK BORG CULT: Dead God's Prophet, p.1

Ten names. Ten gods. All of them slain by the basilisk [[SHE]] within the wretched peaks of the [[Bergen Chrypt]]. Their corpses are scripture nobody reads anymore. Their prophets still hear them — hot wires through the brain, day and night, the voice of something that should have gone silent.

No one wants to hear the words of a dead god. They speak anyway.

## What Their Murder Means

These were *somebody's* gods. Cults that ran older than Verhu's prophecy. Names spoken before the Two-Headed Basilisks built their cathedral on top of [[Anuk Schleger]]'s tomb. [[SHE]] descended on each of them inside the Bergen Chrypt and put them down — the same mountain where she crawled from the cracks at the start of time, the same mountain where she spawned and slaughtered her own.

Now the prophets wander with rotten gospels, beaten and babbling, smelling of the gods that died on top of them. Some still work miracles — the dead god enters the flesh of its last witness and answers one yes/no question before withdrawing.

Whether any temples to these gods remain standing — sacked, defaced, sealed — is wide-open expansion territory. The mechanism of god-murder ("slain by the Basilisk SHE within the wretched peaks") is unwritten; presumably the same agonies she visits on her own spawn cast from the cliffs.
