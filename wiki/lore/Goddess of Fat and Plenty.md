# Goddess of Fat and Plenty
**Type:** deity
**Source:** MBC Bloat

The patron goddess of an obscure cult of bacchanalian priests whose labyrinth Silas the vagabond stumbled into. Her statue stands in the Chapel of Filth — an imposing, corpulent woman with opal eyes — and compels viewers to visit Silas as priests. The [[Gourmand's Cutlery]] is presumably her relic.

**Connections:** [[Bloat]], [[Silas the Fattened King]], [[Gourmand's Cutlery]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Her proper name is unwritten — she is named only by domain. Whether other cult sites of hers exist, what became of her bacchanalian priests, and whether the obscure cult survives elsewhere is all open.
