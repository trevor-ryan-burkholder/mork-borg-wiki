# Daejmon
**Type:** entity / underling
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.19 (Psalm VI, 6:6)

The **left underling** of [[Nechrubel]]. At Misery **6:6** the unnamed enter the earth, passing through the Veil as it is sundered by Daejmon. Whatever the Veil is, Daejmon tears it open.

**Connections:** [[Nechrubel]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]], [[Misery Table]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Daejmon is one sentence of canon. Form, function, history, and the "right underling" are all undefined. The "unnamed" who enter the earth through the torn Veil at 6:6 are unspecified — heralds of 7:7? Spirits? Other dimensions? Wide open.
