# Heedless Creation
**Type:** cosmic force
**Source:** The Death Ziggurat, p.6 (the Spiral Chapel)

A spiraling cosmic force. The [[Spiral Chapel]] within the ruins around the [[Death Ziggurat]] is dedicated to it — the ceiling a painted night sky with a galaxy whirling at its center. Behind a mural of a priest gazing out over the Cosmos is hidden the [[Spiral Crown]], a silver halo set with gemstones in a swirling pattern. Whoever wears the crown becomes a cosmic vessel and sees strange and nightmarish visions of the infinite, collapsing Cosmos.

This is older than the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] and older than the prison-cult of the [[Creton Order]] that bound [[Akünh]]. What it is exactly — a power, a process, an entity — is not said.

**Connections:** [[Spiral Crown]], [[Death Ziggurat]], [[Creton Order]], [[Akünh]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Heedless Creation may simply *be* the cosmos collapsing — the same end that [[Yetsabu-Nech]] heralds, viewed from a different theology. Or it may be a rival force. Source gives a chapel and a crown; everything else is open.
