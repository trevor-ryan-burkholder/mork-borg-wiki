# The Dark Spider, Harbinger of the End
**Type:** entity / deity
**Source:** The Merchant

Invoked once. The "foul priestess" who saved [[Mikhael the Merchant]] from crucifixion told him: *"Repent for the sins of your family and be forgiven by the Dark Spider, Harbinger of the end."* Forgiveness cost Mikhael his death.

**Connections:** [[Mikhael the Merchant]], [[Nechrubel]] (possible aspect or rival), [[Calendar of Nechrubel]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Total mystery. May be an alternate name for [[Nechrubel]], an underling thereof, or a separate doom-deity with its own cult. The "foul priestess" is unnamed. Whether other unfortunate-undeads (like the [[Lady Porcelain]] dolls) are also bound to the Spider is a natural expansion thread.
