# Verhu
**Type:** basilisk / deity
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.10–11

The head of **Prophecy**. One of two heads borne by [[HE]], the surviving basilisk-spawn cast from the cliffs of [[Bergen Chrypt]] who turned and cursed his mother [[SHE]] from the floor of the [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]]. His twin head is [[Gorgh]], rotted with envy that only Verhu knows the truth.

Verhu predicts inexorable annihilation, and since he is always right he has become utterly full of himself. The whispers he gave to the monk [[Anuk Schleger]] became the [[Nameless Scriptures]] — every prophecy fulfilled in turn. He has eclipsed all other faiths and his clergy, the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], rule from [[Galgenbeck]].

It is said [[Nechrubel]] whispered the apocalyptic prophecies into Verhu's ear in the first place. Verhu loves his position. He hungers for temples in his name and demands strange tributes (see [[The Basilisks Demand]]).

At **7:7** Verhu will beam with delight as [[Yetsabu-Nech]] stands before the sun.
