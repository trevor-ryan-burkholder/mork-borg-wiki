# Nechrubel
**Type:** deity
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.12

The shadow that covers all. Nechrubel is melancholy, crop failure, conflict, and war. It is said he whispered the apocalyptic prophecies into [[Verhu]]'s ear. The arch-priestess [[Josilfa Migol]] is rumored to collude with him, and to have been granted eternal life in exchange.

The end times take his name: the [[Calendar of Nechrubel]] counts down to the world's silence.

His underlings include [[Daejmon]], the left underling, who sunders the Veil at Misery **6:6** so the unnamed may enter the earth.

The clergy of Nechrubel is not the [[Two-Headed Basilisks]] — they worship [[Verhu]] — though the two faiths share the same forecast. Whether Nechrubel has his own priesthood or moves only through whispers and famine is left to the GM.

**Connections:** [[Verhu]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]], [[Josilfa Migol]], [[Daejmon]], [[Yetsabu-Nech]], [[Galgenbeck]], [[Two-Headed Basilisks]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Nechrubel's form, his cult, his right underling (only the left is named), and the nature of his bargain with Josilfa are all blank. Death-obsessed cultists (see [[Death Ziggurat]]) seek the [[Shimmering Fields]] without naming Nechrubel directly — possible expansion linking him to that afterlife.
