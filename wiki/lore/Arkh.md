# Arkh
**Type:** basilisk head
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.11

One of two heads borne by [[SHE]]. **Deception**. She claims to be the first prophet of truths now prostituted by [[Verhu]]. Few have ever seen her. Many walk her twin paths.

At Misery **4:4** *"The liar, Arkh shall make knots of the hearts of men, sundering the strongest of bonds."*

**Connections:** [[SHE]], [[Lusi]], [[Verhu]], [[Two-Headed Basilisks]], [[Calendar of Nechrubel]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Whether the "twin paths" of Lusi and Arkh hide a true rival faith to Verhu's is left to GMs. Her followers and methods are undocumented.
