# The Dying World
**Type:** lore / cosmology
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.7, 11

*The Wind from the west. From the sundered land. Rot rides it, and the stench of blood.*

The world is on a countdown. No one has seen the sun in years. Distances shift; paths between places warp. The world possesses a will and a bitter life, its mercy curdled to wrath over a too-long age. Reality decays. Truth becomes dream and dream truth. Cracks grow in the once-stable structures of the past, allowing things misshapen and vile to worm through, emerging into the day's wan light.

The world closes in. To the west the massive [[Bergen Chrypt]] with its catacombs and ice-caked peaks. The [[Endless Sea]] surrounds the rest. Many have ploughed the wave's furrow searching for new lands. They all return, against their will. Alive or dead.

Within these shrinking borders lie the kingdoms: [[Tveland]] (with [[Galgenbeck]] and [[Sarkash]]), the [[Western Kingdom|Wästland]] (with [[Schleswig]] and [[Lake Onda]]), [[Grift]] on its peninsula, frozen [[Kergüs]] in the north with [[Alliáns]], and the [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]] where [[HE]] is coiled.

The old care more for sacrifice and god-offerings than their bawling spawn. Doomsayers are proved right time and again and embraced by hidden powers. Maybe it's best to surrender — to trust your own instinct and skill rather than the whim of the dice? Before all is drowned in welcome silence.

Life locked and failing in a **DARK FORT**.

**Connections:** [[Calendar of Nechrubel]], [[Bergen Chrypt]], [[Endless Sea]], [[Tveland]], [[Western Kingdom]], [[Grift]], [[Kergüs]], [[Valley of the Unfortunate Undead]], [[Galgenbeck]], [[Sarkash]], [[Two-Headed Basilisks]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** "MÖRK BORG" is Swedish for **DARK FORT**, pronounced MURK BORG. The world has no formal name in source — only the kingdoms within it. Whether the warping distances and shifting paths can be exploited (or are themselves a Misery in motion) is undefined.
