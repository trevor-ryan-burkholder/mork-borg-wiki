# Lusi
**Type:** basilisk head
**Source:** MÖRK BORG Bare Bones Edition, p.11

One of two heads borne by [[SHE]]. **Denial**. She looks up and down and says: *yet all shall be well.*

Her twin head is [[Arkh]], **Deception**.

**Connections:** [[SHE]], [[Arkh]], [[Two-Headed Basilisks]]

**Gaps/Expansion Notes:** Of all four basilisk heads, the least detailed in canon. Her cult, if any, is undocumented. The phrase "many walk her twin paths" suggests a counter-faith to Verhu's apocalypse-cult, but no group bearing her name is named in source.
